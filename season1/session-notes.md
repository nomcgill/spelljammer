## The opening premise

> You still remember the day you arrived at Warden's Maw, the prison tucked into a nook of an oblong asteroid of the Astral Sea. The prison used to house those of many degrees of criminality, but since the asteroid collided with another and was bumped out of Wildspace two decades ago, it drifted away from the observance of any eyes, authority, and government. These days it's owned by Githyanki scum who have developed multiple schemes for making money.
>
> The warden collects beings of all types in the Maw's cells. Oblivious passing ships are ransacked. Some are bought for cheap from the black market. Most prisoners are purposed for mining the coal and speckles of diamond. Those prisoners that are determined to be of value are kept for one day blackmailing someone, somewhere. But it's hard to keep track of thousands of prisoners and remember why they're here. That's why most prisoners understand, regardless of the plane or land that they might call home, that this place is where they will die.

The Githyanki Damselfly is created entirely with Githyanki technology. Jetson has his work cut out for him as he surveys the screens before him.

**Jetson gives the rundown.**

- A.R.I.A. — "This is not the fastest Damselfly in the Warden's Maw fleet." (Astral-Responsive Intelligence Assistant. Sounds elegant and mysterious, like a helpful but possibly sassy voice.)
- A.R.I.A. — "Also, you should know that there is already a Githyanki guard aboard this ship. I would check the hyperspace drive."
  - [Encounter - Aryndac](http://dndbeyond.com/encounters/5f1367a5-f105-4105-bef4-610d494d0254)

**Jetson is poisoned / hallucinating.**

- "Space has never looked so gray before…"
- Medicine check: poisoned/hallucinating from Aryndac's longsword. The poison is affecting his brain's "synaptic transmissions".
- A.R.I.A. checks — he has 48 hours to live unless they find an antidote from a Wildspace system.

**Ship comes to an unexpected lurching halt.** Acrobatics or Athletics to stay standing.

A squid-style ship, but its front tentacles have broken off, and those ends have decayed. Something is in the air.

- **Korran's Perception check:** it is the ship he used to be a pirate on — an ancient ship called *The Star-Reaver*, whose hull was built from the ribcage of a dead god.
- **Nature check:** the ship's air envelope is larger than your own ship's, and the toxicity will cause your own ship to become increasingly toxic in the next few hours.
  - A.R.I.A. — "The toxicity seems to be evenly distributed throughout this air envelope. Extra Anti-Hazard precautions will serve no purpose."
- Abandoned. However, Korran remembers that the ship had been loaded with many magical items that his group had plundered.

**Explore the Star-Reaver.** [Korran](../player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md) took the **Fish Suit** and **Boots of Gravitation**. No one got the Spelljamming Helm, Star Chart of the Lost Spheres, or Void Crystal Focus — see [`items.md`](../items.md).

**Notes.**

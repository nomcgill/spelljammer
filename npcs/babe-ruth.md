# Baby "Babe" Ruth

**Appears in:** Season 1

The **interplanetary pizza prince of the criminal underworld.** [Luckyluciano](../player-characters/luckyluciano-ruth.md)'s father, husband of [Marfa](marfa-ruth.md), head of the Ruth Syndicate.

## Status

Offscreen. Named but never appeared.

## The operation

The syndicate runs **Babe Ruth's Galactic Pie Parlors**, an interplanetary chain of family eateries known for meteor-spiced crusts and deep-space delivery. In reality they are money-laundering hubs for illegal cargo running and weapons smuggling across Wildspace systems.

Luckyluciano ran one of the flagship parlors orbiting the Rock of Bral, handling codeword orders, scrubbing astral manifests, and coordinating shipments disguised as pepperoni pallets. She was also her father's favoured errand girl for sensitive tasks — his "personal warlock." **He is literally her warlock patron.**

## Family

- **[Luckyluciano](../player-characters/luckyluciano-ruth.md)** — younger daughter. Wants to prove to him that she can run the family.
- **[Bugsy](bugsy.md)** — older daughter, and the one he sees the crime family eventually going to. She doesn't want it.
- **[Jetson](jetson.md)** — Bugsy's son, so his and Marfa's grandson.
- **[Marfa](marfa-ruth.md)** — his wife.

He owns a legendary **Stromboli recipe**, which Nicole has pitched as the target of a theft in Lucy's side quest.

## Why it matters to the campaign

The syndicate's commercial reach is the party's diplomatic weapon.

It also cuts the other way: the Psurlons at the shipping hub were specifically interested in favours connected to Luckyluciano's family, and the real [Krysta](krysta.md)'s body had a **Pie Parlors IOU** on it. The syndicate is already entangled with the Psurlon operation.

See [`factions.md`](../factions.md) for the Crustfall Conspiracy that put Luckyluciano in prison, and [Tino Fennetti](tino-fennetti.md) for the cousin who informed on her.

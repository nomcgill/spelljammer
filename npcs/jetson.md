# Jetson

**Appears in:** Season 1

The party's pilot, and the reason they have a ship at all. Family connection to the Ruth syndicate. A **Giff**.

## Status

Alive, on the [Rock of Bral](../locations/rock-of-bral.md). His Githyanki poison was healed by [Megan Forthfang](megan-forthfang.md) in Session 7. He passed out at the end of Session 6 and was unconscious or barely functional through the mothership's arrival. [Marfa](marfa-ruth.md) beamed him off the Damselfly with the rest of the party and flew him to Bral in the [*Slice Runner*](../ships/ruth-family-ships.md#the-slice-runner).

## Who he is

[Luckyluciano](../player-characters/luckyluciano-ruth.md)'s **nephew**, and much younger than her. He's the son of her older sister [Bugsy](bugsy.md), so **"Aunt Lucy"** is literal. That makes [Babe Ruth](babe-ruth.md) and [Marfa Ruth](marfa-ruth.md) his grandparents. His father is `[unconfirmed]`.

He crashed into Warden's Maw in the *DiceGunner*, a Flying Fish and sister ship to Luckyluciano's *Slice Runner*, which he had "borrowed" from the family without permission. It was shot down in the escape.

He attended an Academy and took one semester of Gith, which is the only reason anyone can read the controls of a stolen Githyanki Damselfly.

## Season 1

### In play

He is the mouthpiece for the ship's situation — speed, distance to Bral, what's broken. His catchphrase on launching the hyperspace drive is **"PUNCH IT."**

His first read of the escape: they'd reach Bral within a day or two, everyone was going home, and — a beat later — "You're all good people, right?"

### What happened to him

[Aryndac](aryndac.md) stowed away aboard the Damselfly and drove his longsword into the hyperspace drive. Jetson took the poison from that blade deep in his abdomen. It attacked his brain's "synaptic transmissions" — he started hallucinating ("Space has never looked so gray before…") and [A.R.I.A.](aria.md) gave him 48 hours. The wound also slowed the ship's maximum speed.

The party spent most of Sessions 3 through 6 hunting astral silk to bind the wound. They never got it; Megan healed him outright instead.

# Warden's Maw

**Appears in:** Season 1

The prison where the campaign started, and where all four player characters met.

## What it is

A prison tucked into a nook of an oblong asteroid drifting through the Astral Sea. It once housed criminals of all kinds under some form of authority, but roughly **two decades ago the asteroid collided with another and was knocked out of Wildspace**. It drifted beyond the reach of any government or oversight, and is now run by Githyanki purely for profit.

## How it operates

The warden collects beings of all types. Passing ships are ransacked for prisoners. Some captives are bought cheap off the black market. Most are put to work mining **coal and flecks of diamond**. Those judged valuable are simply kept, on the theory that one day they might be useful for blackmailing someone, somewhere.

With thousands of prisoners, nobody keeps track of who they are or why they're there. The prevailing understanding among inmates is that this is where they will die.

Time is distorted and difficult to track outside a Wildspace system, so "night" is a loose concept.

## Features

- **Cafeteria**, with assigned meal times. All four PCs were assigned the same one.
- **Cells** with windows facing outward — which is why [Gu](../npcs/gu.md) kept warning people to stay away from them.
- **A ship harbor / hangar**, holding a fleet of Githyanki **Damselflies**. The party stole one.
- **Mines** for coal and diamond flecks.
- A **balcony** overlooking the drop into the Astral Sea.

## The asteroid

Prisoners tell stories about **Braxats** wandering the asteroid, which the Githyanki supposedly defend the prison against. No prisoner has ever been found who has actually seen one.

## Personnel

[Aryndac](../npcs/aryndac.md) and Aristeth, the two guards described as mean and violent. The warden himself is unnamed and never appeared.

## Season 1

### Why the party got out

A plasmoid fleet bombarded the prison to extract Gu, their vice president. The party's escape was a side effect. That same fleet — the mothership under [Alkgleb](../npcs/alkgleb.md) — is the force that caught them again in Session 7.

Note that [Tilptek](../npcs/tilptek.md) on the Rock of Bral knew about the breakout **within a few hours.** For a prison nobody supposedly watches, news travelled remarkably fast.

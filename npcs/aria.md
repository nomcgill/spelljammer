# A.R.I.A.

**Appears in:** Season 1

**A**stral-**R**esponsive **I**ntelligence **A**ssistant — the intelligence built into the stolen Githyanki Damselfly.

## Status

**Offline, and left behind.** The plasmoid mothership's Dispel Magic wave knocked her out in Session 7 along with most of the ship's systems. The Damselfly, with her aboard, was then left behind so the party could get away. What becomes of her is `[unconfirmed]`.

## Voice

Elegant and mysterious. Helpful, but possibly sassy. Her first line to the party, on being told they'd escape at three million miles per hour, was: *"This is not the fastest Damselfly in the Warden's Maw fleet."*

## Season 1

### What she did

She was the party's sensor suite and their source of bad news:

- Flagged that a Githyanki guard was already aboard and suggested checking the hyperspace drive — which is how they found [Aryndac](aryndac.md).
- Diagnosed [Jetson](jetson.md)'s poison and gave him 48 hours to live.
- Ruled out a workaround on the *Star-Reaver*'s toxic air envelope: *"The toxicity seems to be evenly distributed throughout this air envelope. Extra Anti-Hazard precautions will serve no purpose."*
- Marked three candidate coordinates for the dead god where the Psurlons were rumoured to live. The first turned out to be a Cosmic Horror. The second was the real shipping hub. **The third was never visited.**
- Noticed Jetson passing out, and warned of the enormous entity closing from behind.

That unvisited third coordinate is a loose end worth remembering.

# Season 2 Encounters

Every combat encounter of Season 2. The party is **level 7** going in, with [Megan](../npcs/megan-forthfang.md) fighting alongside them.

## Session 1 — The Kenku assassin

At the [Pie Parlor](../locations/bral-pie-parlor.md) on the Rock of Bral, just as [Marfa](../npcs/marfa-ruth.md) asked the party to help solve the Case of the Missing Stromboli.

| Creature | CR | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kenku assassin ×1 | `[unconfirmed]` | Stood by the parlor's exit with a blowgun and poison darts. Wore a ruby **Ring of Disintegrate** (homebrew) under a glove. Stat block not recorded |

**How it went:**
- **Spotting it:** everyone rolled Perception, and only Korran noticed it.
- **The dart:** Korran threw a dagger and missed. Megan lifted Krysta's head as a shield, and the dart hit the head.
- **The fight:** in initiative, the whole party landed attacks.
- **The Kenku's turn:** it went last, pulled off its glove, and **disintegrated itself** with the ring. It left only dust and the ring, which has **one charge remaining**. See [`items.md`](../items.md).

**Who hired it** is unknown at the table. In planning, Nolan has it **contracted by the [Iron Annex](../locations/iron-annex.md)** It was a member of **The Unkindness**. **Its real target was Krysta's head, not Marfa** (Nolan's ruling). The players don't know this yet; the [Session 2 plan](../season2/session2-plan.md#what-the-players-dont-know) reveals it at the Annex.

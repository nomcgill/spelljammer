# Season 2, Session 1: Plan

The working plan for Session 1, in the order it runs at the table. Ideas that didn't make it in are in the [Session 1 brainstorm](session1-brainstorm.md), and the season plan is in [`plot/season2-planning.md`](../plot/season2-planning.md).

**Played October 12, 2026.** What actually happened, including where it differed from this plan, is in [`session-notes.md`](session-notes.md). For example, the dock officer turned out to be **Lawrence**. This file stays as the prep record.

**Nothing here is canon until it's run.** The read-aloud text and anything marked *proposed* is my draft for Nolan to edit. Everything else is Nolan's plan. **Spoilers:** keep the Stromboli section away from the players, especially Nicole, since it builds on her pitch.

**Where Sessions 1 and 2 are headed:**
- **The Case of the Missing Stromboli** is solved by the end of Session 2.
- Everyone leaves Bral on Korran's ship, bound for the **[Ral'taka Expanse](../locations/raltaka-expanse.md)**.
- Session 2 opens with a cold open in the Expanse.

## 1. Arrival at the Rock of Bral

*Proposed read-aloud, seen from the ship windows:*

> It starts as a smudge of light in the silver haze of the Astral Sea, and then it's simply *there*: a mountain of black stone drifting in nothing, ringed in a faint shimmer where its air envelope meets the void. The whole upper face of the asteroid is city. Towers lean against towers, domes against spires. Bridges strung between them sag with laundry and lanterns, and everything is lit in a hundred colours that don't belong to any one world.
>
> Ships swarm it like gulls around a fishing boat: a hammerhead hauling ore, a pair of wasp-ships chasing each other through the docking lanes, something enormous and organic that might be alive, and dozens of skiffs too small to name. The edges of the rock bristle with piers. Signal lamps blink in a code you half-remember from the Maw. Somewhere a bell rings, and a voice amplified through a brass horn shouts a berth number at no one in particular.
>
> For the first time since Warden's Maw, nobody is shooting at you. The Rock of Bral, where everyone ends up eventually.

**Bral is a hub world**, so put any species on the piers that you like.

## 2. The dock check

Both ships berth side by side, Korran's Shrike behind Marfa's [*Slice Runner*](../ships/ruth-family-ships.md#the-slice-runner).

*Proposed description of the dock officer, a hard-G Giff like the Ruths:*

> He's already standing at the bottom of the gangplank when you come down, as if he's been there since before you docked. He's a Giff, broad as a door, in a dock-blue uniform pressed so sharp it could cut rope. Every brass button is polished to a mirror. A row of service ribbons climbs his chest, and a long-barrelled musket is slung across his back, its stock lovingly oiled. He holds a clipboard the way other people hold a sword. His tusks are capped in brass, and his moustache is waxed into two perfect points that tremble when he breathes in.

> **"OI! YOU THERE! Papers, permits, and planetary origin! No docking without proper documentation! And if I find so much as ONE unauthorized space hamster aboard, I'll have your vessel impounded!"**

*Proposed name, if you want one:* **Dock Sergeant Brumble Thackeray**.

**How it plays:**
- **He recognises Marfa** and folds at once. He takes only **names, plus the owner of each vessel**.
- **The party doesn't have to give real names.** Let each player choose. They're escaped prisoners, and some names carry weight:
  - Pip is a plasmoid prince arriving in a plasmoid ship.
  - Korran chooses between "Cloudjumper" and "Driftblade."
  - Megan will very likely announce his full royal title.
  - Lucy was arrested on Bral.
- **Korran's ship.** The sergeant tells Korran that his plasmoid ship, and probably whoever took its helm, can be **scry-located until it's re-registered**. The DAV (Department of Astral Vehicles) opens in a couple of hours. Re-registering wipes the trace and makes the ship officially Korran's.
- *Proposed:* the DAV will ask for the **ship's name**, which gives Brad a fun moment at the counter.
- **Small question:** whose name goes down as the *Slice Runner*'s owner? It's Lucy's ship, the one seized in her arrest, but Marfa is flying it. The sergeant's eyebrow at the answer is a free bit of character.
- **Tamenik** hurries off as soon as the check is done. He wants to beat his relative home while the CEO seat at Freelin Corporation is empty.

*The Remove Curse prologue fits best aboard the* Slice Runner *on approach, before this scene.*

## 3. Breakfast at Lucy's old Pie Parlor

Marfa talks the group into breakfast at Lucy's old flagship parlor. **Bugsy has reopened it.** Marfa shoos her away when they arrive, because she wants the room to herself so she can learn about this unlikely group. That's what she does. She's the social genius of the family: she learns who people are and what they can do for her, finds the strings to pull, and then makes trades.

**One thing to square:** canon says Lucy's flagship parlor **orbits** the Rock of Bral. So either they take a short hop up in the *Slice Runner*, or a lift or shuttle runs up from the docks. The description below assumes orbit.

*Proposed read-aloud:*

> Babe Ruth's Galactic Pie Parlor hangs above the Rock of Bral like a chrome doughnut, turning slowly, and the whole city wheels past its windows. Inside, it smells of yeast and woodsmoke and something faintly electric.
>
> The floor is black-and-white checkerboard. The booths are cherry-red vinyl trimmed in chrome, with little ringed-planet lamps on every table. A long counter of mint-green enamel runs the length of the room, lined with spinning stools. Above it, a neon sign in looping script reads ***"Out Of This World Since Forever."*** Behind the counter, the brick oven is shaped like the bell of a rocket engine and glows a deep, patient orange. A rotating glass carousel of pies turns beside it like a tiny solar system.
>
> The jukebox in the corner plays something brassy. The big round porthole windows look straight down onto Bral. In the far corner there's one booth set apart from the others: bigger, older, its vinyl cracked with use. It has a little brass plaque you can't quite read from here.

## 6. The Case of the Missing Stromboli (proposed)

This is Nicole's [pitch](../player-characters/luckyluciano-ruth.md#pitch-the-case-of-the-missing-stromboli), under its proper name.

**Parked: Nolan is saving the full case for later.** Session 1 only establishes that **Krysta's head holds clues to the theft of the Stromboli recipe's ingredient**. What follows is a draft to come back to, not something to use in Session 1.

Nolan asked for this to be designed from scratch. It's built from canon pieces: Nicole's pitch, the Joisey Jiffs, the Pie Parlors IOU on Krysta's body, the Psurlons' trade in favours, and the plasmoid court. It's built to send everyone to the Ral'taka Expanse.

### What everyone believes

- **The recipe.** Babe's legendary Stromboli is made from a handwritten recipe card kept in a gold-edged leather folio. Nobody outside the family has ever seen it.
- **The opening night.** A few weeks ago, while Lucy was still in the Maw, Bugsy held the **reopening night** of this parlor. Babe came in person and sat at **his booth in the corner**, the one with the brass plaque. He made the Stromboli himself, as he only does for special occasions, then left the folio with Marfa and Bugsy to lock away.
- **The theft.** By closing, the folio was empty.
- **The clue.** The next morning, a napkin from **Astro Nommie's Slicehouse** was found tucked into the booth.
- **The cover-up.** All signs point to the Joisey Jiffs, as in Nicole's pitch. **Babe doesn't know it's gone.** Marfa and Bugsy have been covering for weeks. That's why Marfa needs outsiders: none of the family's own people can be trusted to keep it quiet, and Bugsy is under a cloud because it happened on her watch.

### What actually happened

- **The thief** was a **Psurlon Ringer**. It ate one of the new hires Bugsy took on for the reopening, a busboy, and has been wearing him ever since. It's still working at the parlor, listening to Ruth business.
- **The Psurlons were brokering.** They trade in debts and favours, and they've been doing business with the Ruths for a while (the IOU on Krysta's body proves it). Someone paid them to get the card.
- **The Jiffs are guilty, but not of the theft.** They bought the **recipe** from the Psurlons, because they want the Stromboli to beat the Ruths. The napkin was the Psurlons framing them, and the Jiffs really do have a copy. That keeps Nicole's "all signs point to the Jiffs" true while it's still not the whole truth.
- **The real buyer is the plasmoid court**, Pip's brother's people. They wanted something else on that card.

### The twist: "What if the recipe was never meant to be stolen?"

- **The secret ingredient.** It's **a pepper that grows only on the gold-veined floating islands of the Ral'taka Expanse**. Proposed name: *goldfleck pepper*.
- **The last line of the card** isn't an ingredient. It's **the supplier's route into the Expanse**. Babe has bought the pepper for decades through one discreet Hadozee trader.
- **What that makes the card.** It's the only written way into the Expanse that isn't a pirate's map, hidden in plain sight as "just a recipe."
- **Why the court wanted it.** The Expanse has an excess of gold and no defences, which makes it perfect for funding the usurper's war chest.
- **So the theft was never about pizza.** Lucy's family has been sitting on the way into Korran's homeland the whole time.

Whether the Hadozee trader is from Korran's own Cloudjumper Tribe is a question for Brad.

### Why it sends everyone to the Ral'taka Expanse

- **Korran:** the court is heading for his homeland, and he has the map to the hoard.
- **Pip:** he can hit his brother's funding before it exists, with Ruth backing.
- **Lucy:** she can secure the family's supply line, beat the Jiffs and prove herself to Babe.
- **Megan:** it's the plasmoids who took Thorndumm, and the Psurlons who ate his daughter.

### Clues for Session 2

- **The missing busboy's family.** He stopped writing home the week before the reopening, which is when he was eaten.
- **The busboy himself.** He's never seen eating, and he flinches at Megan.
- **Astro Nommie's.** The Jiffs are serving a suspiciously good Stromboli, and they'll admit they *bought* the recipe.
- **The Psurlon connection.** Megan's lead on Speak with Dead, the Krysta IOU and the busboy all point back to the Psurlons.
- **The DAV.** A plasmoid court vessel registered at Bral last week and filed a flight plan toward the Expanse. Korran can spot this while he's there for his own ship.

## 7. The blowdart (how Session 1 ends)

**Nolan's plan:** someone has been eavesdropping elsewhere in the restaurant. **The session ends with a poisoned blowdart hitting Krysta's head**, which Megan is holding. The head holds clues to the theft of the Stromboli recipe's ingredient. Then an encounter follows. **The attacker was contracted by the [Iron Annex](../locations/iron-annex.md)** to take out Marfa or Krysta's memories.

*Proposed* ways the dart ends up in the head:
- **It was meant for Marfa.** Megan sees it and swings the head up as a shield, without a flicker of emotion. That's darkly funny, and it fits how casually he carries it.
- **It was meant for the head.** The poison is an alchemical memory-rot, built to dissolve whatever a Ringer's brain still holds. Now the clues are on a clock, which gives Session 2 urgency without anyone chasing the party.

**How the Annex knew to send someone:** Tilptek could have read the dock record from the morning's check. The Annex has also hunted this party once before.

### Who the Annex sent (proposed)

Bral is a hub world, so any race works. These fit the job best:

1. **Kenku.** My top pick.
   - **Why they fit:** kenku repeat what they hear in the exact voice they heard it, which makes them perfect eavesdroppers. The Annex gets a word-for-word "recording" of everything Marfa and the party said.
   - **At the table:** in the fight, the kenku taunts the party *in their own voices*, quoting the recap they just gave Marfa. That's creepy, and it tells the players exactly how much was overheard.
   - **The motto fits too:** *"Discretion. Efficiency. Results. Results."* The repetition reads like something a kenku would say.
2. **Grung.**
   - **Why they fit:** they're small poison-frog people whose skin produces the poison, so the dart is loaded from the attacker's own body. They're easy to hide under booths or behind the pie carousel.
   - **The catch:** the bright colours give them away the moment they move, so they work best as a team of two or three.
3. **Changeling.**
   - **Why they fit:** one could have been the server who brought their breakfast. It's a good parallel to Krysta's Ringer, and it makes everyone paranoid about who in the parlor is real.
   - **The catch:** that's also its weakness. A second impostor straight after the Psurlon Ringer story might feel repetitive.
4. **Autognome.**
   - **Why they fit:** it's a Spelljammer construct folk, and it could be working as the parlor's busing unit. Nobody looks at the help, and it's built for a contract like this.
   - **At the table:** a mechanical dart launcher in its forearm.
5. **Githyanki.**
   - **Why it fits:** this ties into your prison-revenge hook. A Warden's Maw Githyanki takes the Annex contract because it's personal.
   - **The catch:** it's flashier than blowdarts. Save it if you want the revenge thread for later.

**My suggestion:** a **kenku contractor** with a couple of hired toughs. The kenku shoots and talks; the toughs block the exits.

**Stats:**
- **The kenku:** use a Spy or Assassin stat block (Assassin for a real threat), with a blowgun and kenku Mimicry flavour.
- **The toughs:** Thug or Bandit Captain stat blocks.
- **Megan:** he's far stronger than all of them, so let him be busy protecting Marfa and the head while the PCs fight.

**Earlier attacker ideas:** these were written for the full Case of the Missing Stromboli, which is now parked.

1. **The busboy (the Ringer) and two or three hired toughs in Astro Nommie's delivery jackets.**
   - **Why now:** he overheard Marfa hiring outsiders to find the recipe. He also saw **Megan walk in carrying a Psurlon's head**.
   - **The jackets** frame the Jiffs again.
   - **How it ends:** the busboy escapes, which keeps the mystery alive for Session 2, and at least one tough is caught. The tough says he was hired that morning at Astro Nommie's back door, which points the wrong way.
   - **A Session 2 clue:** in Season 1, Krysta's head stayed lion-shaped after death, so if the busboy is killed he won't change back. Something else should be wrong with him instead, such as no heartbeat or a mouth that's slightly too wide.
2. **Real Joisey Jiff enforcers.** This is the straight red herring: they're there to warn Marfa off the hunt, not to kill her.
3. **A plasmoid court agent** who pours up out of the floor drain. This is more dramatic, but it tells Pip the court is involved much earlier.
4. **Tino Fennetti's hired help.** Tino is still out there and still selling the family out. It's a good secondary thread, but it doesn't advance the main mystery.

## 8. Ending the session

**Nolan's plan:** the dart hits the head, and the encounter follows.

*Proposed* ways to end the encounter:

- **The attacker is caught.** All it will say, in Marfa's own voice if it's a kenku, is *"Discretion. Efficiency. Results."*

Korran still has his DAV appointment. It can open Session 2, or close Session 1 if there's time.

# Nix

**Appears in:** Season 2 (unused)

Tiefling. Lookout. Night-shift, debt, not unfriendly. One horn chipped. Sits by a viewport with her boots on the frame. Owes the [Ruths](marfa-ruth.md) and wants the debt paid so she can disappear. The Expanse is far enough.

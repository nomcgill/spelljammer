# Factions

The organisations in play. Every one of them has a personal claim on at least one player character, which is the engine of the campaign: the party is being hunted by their own families and employers.

## The Ruth Syndicate

**Led by** [Baby "Babe" Ruth](npcs/babe-ruth.md), the interplanetary pizza prince of the criminal underworld, with [Marfa Ruth](npcs/marfa-ruth.md) speaking for the business.

**Front:** Babe Ruth's Galactic Pie Parlors, an interplanetary chain known for meteor-spiced crusts and deep-space delivery. Underneath: money laundering for illegal cargo running and weapons smuggling across Wildspace.

**PC tie:** [Luckyluciano](player-characters/luckyluciano-ruth.md) is Babe and Marfa's daughter, ran the flagship parlor orbiting the Rock of Bral, and served as her father's personal warlock — Babe is her warlock patron. Her older sister [Bugsy](npcs/bugsy.md) is Babe's intended heir and doesn't want it. Bugsy's son [Jetson](npcs/jetson.md) is Lucy's nephew.

**House drink:** **Hawaiian Punch** is the primary drink at the Pie Parlors, their equivalent of Coca-Cola.

**Family treasures:** Babe Ruth's legendary **Stromboli recipe** — the object of Nicole's pitched side quest (see [Luckyluciano](player-characters/luckyluciano-ruth.md#pitch-the-case-of-the-missing-stromboli)).

**The Crustfall Conspiracy** is the sting that broke the outer-system operation and put Luckyluciano in Warden's Maw. Her cousin [Tino "The Saucepan" Fennetti](npcs/tino-fennetti.md), caught during a failed jellyfish venom deal with a deep-space beholder cartel, took immunity and gave up everything. He alleged she had charmed port inspectors, teleported contraband via eldritch shortcuts, and bound imprisoned souls into cursed pizza ovens to destroy evidence — which her own backstory notes may or may not be true. She was taken mid-deal, trading a fake "moonstone pie" for a void-forged grimoire linked to the Nine Hells.

**Current role:** the party's rescuers. Marfa threatened to cut the plasmoids off from Pie Parlor business, then beamed the party out in the *Slice Runner* and flew them to the Rock of Bral.

### Rivals of the Ruths

From Nicole's notes on Lucy. Neither has appeared at the table yet.

- **The Joisey Jiffs** — the Ruths' oldest rivals. They're **soft-G** Giffs — "Jiffs" — as opposed to the **hard-G** Ruth Giffs. That pronunciation split is campaign homebrew, not published Giff lore. Nicole's pitch makes them the prime suspects in the stolen Stromboli recipe.
- **Astro Nommie's Slicehouse** — the Joisey Jiffs' pizza operation, and the rival to Babe Ruth's Galactic Pie Parlors.

## The Plasmoid Kingdom

**Led by** [Pip](player-characters/pip-oozewald.md)'s brother, the king. **Unnamed** — worth fixing.

**Known members:** [Alkgleb](npcs/alkgleb.md), a mothership general. [Gelp](npcs/gelp.md), the king's best High Wizard. Pip's **cousin** (unnamed; served the king through the coup). [Gu](npcs/gu.md), described by the fleet as their vice president.

**PC tie:** Pip is **Prince Philip Oozewald**, and he led an unsuccessful coup against his brother. That's why he was in Warden's Maw. The plasmoids call him their "slime-brother-nemesis." Pip regards his brother as a **usurper**, and now plans to gather allies on the Rock of Bral and lead a revolution against him.

**Recent actions:** bombarded Warden's Maw to extract Gu, which accidentally freed the party. Attacked and conquered [Thorndumm](npcs/megan-forthfang.md), where Gelp petrified the Leonin king. Now pursuing the party with a [mothership](ships/plasmoid-mothership.md).

Note the tangle: the plasmoids freed Pip by accident, are hunting Pip deliberately, and separately created the powerful ally now protecting him.

## Freelin Corporation

**Led by** [Kirrak](npcs/kirrak-freelin.md), CEO, a Tortle. Based on or near the Rock of Bral.

**PC tie:** [Tamenik Freelin](player-characters/tamenik-freelin.md) is Kirrak's uncle. Kirrak had him imprisoned for being annoying with his drums. The company knows Tamenik was sent away; most don't know who did it.

**Current role:** Kirrak has bought passage aboard the plasmoid mothership to retrieve his uncle personally, and would likely have him detained as a threat on sight.

## The Iron Annex / Spherewide Contracting Compact

**Staffed by** [Tilptek](npcs/tilptek.md), a Thri-kreen desk clerk. The Annex is a **division** of the Compact, which is otherwise undescribed.

**Role:** contract brokerage on the [Rock of Bral](locations/iron-annex.md). It paged Kirrak within hours of the breakout and supplied the method for finding an unscryable target.

**The open thread:** Tilptek acts on instructions from **"the boss"**, who has never been named. That's the most interesting unexplained actor in the campaign — someone who knew within hours both that Tamenik couldn't be scried and that Pip was a usable proxy.

## The Githyanki of Warden's Maw

**Role:** they own [Warden's Maw](locations/wardens-maw.md) and run it for profit — ransacking passing ships for prisoners, buying captives cheap, working most of them in coal and diamond mines, and keeping the valuable ones for future blackmail.

**Known members:** the unnamed warden, [Aryndac](npcs/aryndac.md), and Aristeth.

**PC tie:** [Korran](player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md)'s pirate crew was ambushed and captured by a githyanki patrol, which is how he ended up inside.

**Current role:** quieter than expected. They can probably **scry the stolen Damselfly**, and Jetson's warning was that stopping would let them catch up. The party has stopped repeatedly and the Githyanki have never appeared. Aryndac is still at large.

## The Psurlons

**Role:** operate a [shipping hub](locations/psurlon-shipping-hub.md) inside the hollowed-out remains of a dead god. They trade in cargo, debts and favours — "Would you like to incur a debt?" — and are unmoved by intimidation.

**Notable:** a **Psurlon Ringer** ate Megan's daughter [Krysta](npcs/krysta.md) and wore her. The real Krysta's body carried a **Pie Parlors IOU**, so the Psurlons and the Ruth syndicate are already doing business.

**Standing with the party:** bad. The party killed several Psurlons escaping, took a Ringer's head, and left after spending a favour. Megan wants to fight them on sight, considering them inherently evil.

## The Red Lions of Thorndumm

**Led by** [Megan Forthfang](npcs/megan-forthfang.md), who says he is their king.

**Status:** invaded by plasmoids; the king was petrified and shipped off as cargo. What remains of the kingdom is unknown. Megan has never been on a spaceship, which raises the question of how a Leonin king from "the lands" fights like a 17th-level paladin.

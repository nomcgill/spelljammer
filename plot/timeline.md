# Timeline

The campaign's story as a single narrative, one section per season.

## Season 1

A single narrative pass over Sessions 1–7. Derived from [`season1/session-notes.md`](../season1/session-notes.md) and kept aligned with the player-facing [`../season1/session0-recap.md`](../season1/session0-recap.md).

**Session 0 read-aloud:** [`../season1/session0-recap.md`](../season1/session0-recap.md) — the synopsis for the week before Season 2.

Elapsed in-world time is roughly a week. The journey from Warden's Maw to the Rock of Bral was quoted at one to two days at the start, but sabotage, a Cosmic Horror, and a supply stop stretched it out. The party finally reached Bral at the very end of Season 1 — without the Damselfly.

### Act 1: Out of Warden's Maw (Session 1)

All four player characters were prisoners at Warden's Maw, a prison in an asteroid that drifted out of Wildspace two decades ago and is now run by Githyanki for profit. None of them chose each other; they were assigned the same cafeteria meal time.

That night the sky tore open. **Plasmoid ships bombarded the prison to free their vice president, Gu** — not for the party, not for mercy. Gu was the soft-spoken inmate who had warned them for a thousand meals to stay away from the windows; nobody believed him; he had been right.

The fleet freed Gu. Instead of vanishing into their arms, he came for the party — **cell by cell, one by one** — *"Follow me!"*

He led them onto a balcony and screamed his freedom into the void — *"I'm free! I'm down here! Here!"* — and a Githyanki ship blasted him. **Gu survived that blast.**

In the chaos, Luckyluciano's **nephew** Jetson pulled up in a borrowed family Flying Fish, the *DiceGunner*, crashed it, and the party fought with him toward a stolen Githyanki Damselfly while a plasmoid battleship charged its cannon at the dock. They killed Aristeth. Gu tackled Aryndac off the harbor into the Astral Sea; unnoticed, Aryndac **Misty Stepped aboard the Damselfly** mid-fall and vanished into the walls.

Onboard, they **punched it into hyperspeed.**

### Act 2: Sabotage and a Ghost Ship (Session 2)

The Damselfly was entirely Githyanki technology. Jetson pieced the controls together from one semester of Gith at the Academy and hit three million miles an hour — Rock of Bral one or two days out. **A.R.I.A.** (**A**stral-**R**esponsive **I**ntelligence **A**ssistant) noted dryly that this was not the fastest Damselfly in the fleet — and that a Githyanki was already aboard.

Aryndac had driven his longsword into the hyperspace drive. He pulled it free, smirked, and teleported away. Consequences: a crippled drive, and a poison in Jetson's abdomen that would kill him in forty-eight hours.

**Spelljammer ships automatically exit hyperspeed when they encounter a planet or entity of note.** The Damselfly lurched to a halt beside a derelict squid ship in a toxic air envelope — broken tentacles, hull built from a dead god's ribcage, an acidic green aura of ailment. Korran recognised her: the *Star-Reaver*, the pirate vessel that had plundered his homeland, enslaved him, and for a time been his home. Investigating the wreckage, he took the **Fish Suit** and **Boots of Gravitation**; the Spelljamming Helm, Star Chart, and Void Crystal Focus were left behind as the ship's condition pressed the party's anxiety.

On the next jump, something followed them — a **Feyr**, a tentacled horror that feeds quietly on strong emotions and prefers to devour nightmares while its victims sleep. Luckyluciano burned half the cargo rations trying to kill it.

### Act 3: The Wrong Dead God (Sessions 3–4)

Tamenik fell ill with **Blackstar Fever**: black veins glowing faintly beneath his shell. A curse, not a disease. Unconscious, feverish, nightmares. The party needed cures for him and for Jetson.

They went looking for a nearby Psurlon civilization in the hollowed remains of a dead god. A.R.I.A. marked three candidate coordinates. The first was not a dead god. It was a **Cosmic Horror**.

It tore a wing from the ship. The party climbed onto the roof and worked the cannons while the hyperspace drive spun — thirty seconds that felt like forever. Mid-fight, when the ship was about to take massive damage, time stopped for everyone but Pip. A **Chwinga**, freed from a Feyr's bronze eye, giggled, called him a funny color, offered to help the big thing attack the ship, and left him the Charm of Ruin — and a game of hide and seek that has not ended yet.

They escaped, minus a wing, and on the next jump reached the real destination: a Psurlon shipping hub in the ribs of a dead god. Three things were needed — diamond for the Hyperdrive Core, voidfish eggs for Tamenik's fever, astral silk for Jetson's wound. The Psurlons wanted favours, especially anything stamped with Luckyluciano Ruth's family name.

### Act 4: The Dream and the Shipyard (Session 5)

Fever-dreaming, Tamenik met **Captain Gloamcast** — the old Hadozee captain of the *Star-Reaver* — sitting at its spelljamming helm. (Gloamcast's corpse had sat at that helm when the party found the derelict.) Gloamcast had carried Blackstar Fever before him and called it a curse. He told Tamenik he needed **Greater Restoration** or voidfish eggs within the week, and that Korran's treasure-map cipher was **Korran's late mother's birthday**. Gloamcast had loved her.

Tamenik woke to Jetson screaming in his face. The ship was empty; five Psurlons were trying to spell their way inside. They abruptly turned and left.

At the yard, Krysta — presenting herself as a Leonin king's daughter — showed the party around while clearly communicating with the Psurlons. **Korran slipped off among the containers**, searching for their goods or anything valuable, and found a Leonin petrified by Flesh to Stone: **Megan Forthfang**, King of the Red Lions. A Scroll of Greater Restoration was stuck to the container with green goo; Korran used it and freed him.

Meanwhile Pip swallowed 50,000 gp of diamonds and noticed he was being scried. Krysta tried to turn the party against Megan — then was revealed as a **Psurlon Ringer** who had eaten the real Krysta and stolen her memories. The shipyard fight broke out.

### Act 5: Cured, Then Caught (Sessions 6–7)

The party escaped the hub with Megan, 100,000 gp of diamonds inside Pip, and Krysta's severed head.

**Megan Forthfang — a high-level paladin — cured Tamenik's Blackstar Fever with the spell Greater Restoration.** (Not the Chwinga, not the Charm of Ruin, not the unicorn horn.) He later healed Jetson's poison, claimed Speak with Dead, owed Korran a debt, and had never been on a spaceship before.

The diamonds fixed the Hyperdrive Core, putting Bral one day out. Then Jetson collapsed, and A.R.I.A. reported an enormous entity closing from behind — a familiar ship: the **same plasmoid mothership** that had bombarded Warden's Maw.

A wave of Dispel Magic hit the Damselfly like an EMP: auxiliary power, failing comms, A.R.I.A. offline, emergency lighting only. On the flickering monitor: **Alkgleb**, a plasmoid general serving Pip's brother the king, and **Kirrak**, Tamenik's own nephew and CEO of Freelin Corporation, who had locked Tamenik away for being annoying with his drums.

They had found the party by scrying Pip. **Tamenik cannot be scried.** That is still true. Kirrak paid his way aboard the plasmoid ship to collect his uncle personally.

*"Send in the other ones,"* Alkgleb said in Primordial. A silent silver plasmoid **assassin** snuck aboard the dispelled Damselfly with a silver sword and summoned a Yochlol, which the party killed. Tamenik opened a bedroom door and found someone who should not have been there.

Meanwhile Korran made his stealth check (29) and crossed the void in the Fish Suit to the gargantuan mothership. He found a **crack in its hull** and got inside, planning to sabotage either the ship itself or Alkgleb and Kirrak. Instead he found a **smaller ship in the mothership's loading bay**.

On the Damselfly, the party **killed the assassin**, buying just enough time. **Marfa arrived in the *Slice Runner*** and **beamed the crew off the Damselfly** — including Jetson and Megan. The **Damselfly, with A.R.I.A. aboard, was left behind** so they could get away. Korran was not aboard. He had **taken the spelljamming helm of the smaller ship, hijacked it, and escaped** the mothership on his own.

Korran flew the stolen ship back to the **Rock of Bral** alongside the *Slice Runner*, which carried the rest of the party and Marfa. **Everyone made it back.**

### Season 1 ends: the Rock of Bral

The party finally reached the destination they'd been chasing all season. On arrival, **Pip's plan is to gather allies and lead a revolution against his usurping brother, the king.**

## Season 2

Matt isn't playing, so the party is Korran, Luckyluciano and Pip. Session-by-session detail is in [`season2/session-notes.md`](../season2/session-notes.md).

### Session 1: Breakfast at the Pie Parlor

**The docks.**
- The *Slice Runner* and Korran's Shrike docked side by side on the Rock of Bral.
- A Giff dock officer, **Lawrence**, wanted ID but gave way to Marfa's influence. He took names, waived the costs, and told Korran his ship still had to be registered at the DAV when it opened.
- Korran had "not much of a last name to give." Luckyluciano declared the *Slice Runner* "property of the Ruth family."
- **Tamenik left** to withdraw money and get home to Freelin Corporation while Kirrak's seat sat vacant.

**Breakfast.**
- Marfa took the three PCs, Megan and Jetson to Luckyluciano's old flagship Pie Parlor, now run by **Bugsy**. She shooed Bugsy off and sent Jetson to sit with his mother.
- Over pizza and Hawaiian Punch, the party told Marfa **their entire story**, and she now knows everything.
- Korran's version of how he ended up in the Maw: failed raids on Githyanki outposts, then imprisonment out of petty revenge.

**The assassin.**
- Marfa asked for their help with the **Case of the Missing Stromboli**, which began at that very table.
- Korran spotted a **Kenku assassin** at the exit and threw a dagger, which missed. Megan raised **Krysta's head** as a shield, and it took the poison dart.
- The party piled onto the Kenku. On its turn it **disintegrated itself** with a ruby ring, leaving only the ring.

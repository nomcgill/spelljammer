## Background — The Crustfall Conspiracy

Daughter of **Baby "Babe" Ruth**, the interplanetary pizza prince of the criminal underworld, and [Marfa Ruth](../npcs/marfa-ruth.md). His favoured errand girl for the more sensitive work, and his **personal warlock**: **Babe Ruth himself is her warlock patron**, so their already complicated father-daughter relationship has a magical side too. She grew up around the family business and learned early that **family, loyalty, and keeping your mouth shut** mattered more than anything else.

**How the pact began** (played as a flashback in Season 2, Session 2): when she was a child, Babe told her she was too young to learn the family's secrets; he was protecting her. Then he **tapped her on the head with the folder holding the Stromboli recipe**, which she was too small to see. That was the moment she entered her warlock pact with him.

She is **not the heir.** Babe sees the crime family eventually going to her **older sister, [Bugsy](../npcs/bugsy.md)** — who doesn't want it and just wants to be a mom to her son, [Jetson](../npcs/jetson.md). So Jetson is Lucy's **nephew**, and "Aunt Lucy" is literal.

The [Ruth Syndicate](../factions.md) runs **Babe Ruth's Galactic Pie Parlors**, a chain of family eateries famous for meteor-spiced crusts and deep-space delivery, and in reality a money-laundering network for cargo running and weapons smuggling across Wildspace. Luckyluciano ran one of the flagship parlors orbiting the [Rock of Bral](../locations/rock-of-bral.md): codeword orders, scrubbed astral manifests, high-level shipments disguised as pepperoni pallets.

Her cousin [Tino "The Saucepan" Fennetti](../npcs/tino-fennetti.md), a crust captain caught during a failed jellyfish venom deal with a deep-space beholder cartel, took immunity and gave up everything — including her role and her front. He claimed she had modified her pact magic to charm port inspectors, teleport contraband by eldritch shortcut, and **bind imprisoned souls into cursed pizza ovens** to destroy evidence. Her backstory notes, deliberately, that this *may or may not be true*.

Her ship, the [*Slice Runner*](../ships/ruth-family-ships.md), was found adrift, dripping with illusory glamers and runes etched in Infernal suggesting extra-dimensional storage. She was arrested mid-deal, trading a fake "moonstone pie" for a void-forged grimoire linked to the Nine Hells. She maintains she **didn't betray the family**, and has kept her own mouth shut ever since.

## Goals

**Infamy and fortune.** Her father stands in the way a bit: she's determined to prove to him, her patron, that she is capable of running the family, since Bugsy doesn't want it.

Nicole likes the revolution (Pip's plot) but doesn't expect much development for Lucy in it, so she has pitched a personal side quest.

## Season 1

The party's face and closer — the one whose name opens doors.

- **Session 4.** Burned **half the cargo rations** with a fireball trying to kill a Feyr.
- **Session 5.** At the Psurlon hub, the Psurlons were unmoved by intimidation but very interested in favours connected to her family's influence. Chased [Krysta](../npcs/krysta.md) through the container yard.
- **Session 6.** Traded her **family favour IOU** to the Psurlons for the diamonds and voidfish eggs the party needed. That trade is what fixed the ship and cured Tamenik.
- **Session 7.** With [Jetson](../npcs/jetson.md) unconscious, she became the **primary pilot** of the Damselfly, with Korran spelling her enough to take a long rest. Her mother, [Marfa](../npcs/marfa-ruth.md), arrived just in time in the *Slice Runner* — Lucy's own ship — beamed the party off the Damselfly, and flew them to the Rock of Bral.

## Season 2

- **Session 1.** At the Bral docks she told [Lawrence](../npcs/lawrence.md) the *Slice Runner* was **"property of the Ruth family,"** not specifically hers or her mother's. She **read her mother's intent** before anyone else: Marfa wanted to see whether the group could be persuaded, through an exchange, to help solve the **Case of the Missing Stromboli**. At her old flagship parlor, now [Bugsy's](../npcs/bugsy.md), she ordered a vegetarian pizza with anchovies. She shared the whole Season 1 story with Marfa and fought the Kenku assassin.
- **Session 2.** Marfa spoke with her alone in the back room, which set off a [flashback](../season2/session-notes.md#lucy-and-marfa-and-lucys-flashback) to the moment her pact with Babe began (see above). The talk went warmly: Marfa said she was so glad to have Lucy back, since trust is hard to find in their business, and **hugged her**. Lucy took **Jetson** along to "learn the business" and led the way to the Iron Annex. There she made it canon that **she used to date [Tilptek](../npcs/tilptek.md)**. When the Kenku cast Darkness, **she was the only one who could see.**

## Threads on her

Her mother saved the party in Lucy's own seized ship, the *Slice Runner*. How Marfa got it back from impound is `[unconfirmed]`. Marfa also radioed a threat to the plasmoid fleet: harm her daughter or her grandson and no plasmoid does business with the Pie Parlors again.

Her sister Bugsy is the heir who doesn't want the job, and Lucy wants it.

The real Krysta's body, back at the Psurlon yard, carried a **Pie Parlors IOU** — the syndicate was already doing business with the Psurlons before the party ever arrived.

And Tino is still out there.

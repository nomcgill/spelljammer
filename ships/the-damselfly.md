# The Damselfly

**Appears in:** Season 1

The party's stolen ship, and effectively the sixth character of Season 1. **It never got a name.**

## Fate

**Left behind at the end of Season 1, with [A.R.I.A.](../npcs/aria.md) aboard, so the party could get away.** Marfa beamed the party off it onto the [*Slice Runner*](ruth-family-ships.md#the-slice-runner), and Korran left in a [stolen ship](korrans-stolen-ship.md). The Damselfly stayed dead in space next to the [plasmoid mothership](plasmoid-mothership.md). What happened to it afterward is `[unconfirmed]`.

## Origin

A Githyanki **Damselfly**, taken from the ship harbor at [Warden's Maw](../locations/wardens-maw.md) during the breakout. Built entirely with Githyanki technology, which is why [Jetson](../npcs/jetson.md)'s single semester of Gith at the Academy matters so much — nobody else can read the controls.

[A.R.I.A.](../npcs/aria.md)'s assessment: *"This is not the fastest Damselfly in the Warden's Maw fleet."*

## Known features

- **Hyperspace drive** with a **Hyperdrive Core** that consumes diamond or diamond dust. Takes **5 turns (30 seconds)** to spin up before it can launch.
- **Three cannons on the roof**, doing **8d8 force damage** per blast.
- Cannons on the **bottom** of the ship, with a spot behind them that Korran identified as more vulnerable to infiltration.
- **Wings**, used only for flying through atmosphere. A History check (DC 5) confirms the ship doesn't need them in the Astral Sea.
- At least two **bedrooms**.
- A **window** Jetson can roll down.
- An **air envelope**, which can be contaminated by a larger overlapping one.

## Top speed

**3 million miles per hour** ("3 mmph") at its best, which put the Rock of Bral one to two days away. [Aryndac](../npcs/aryndac.md)'s sabotage of the drive reduced the maximum speed and made piloting much harder. Repairing the Hyperdrive Core with Pip's diamonds brought Bral back to one day out.

## Season 1

Jetson, primarily — his launch command is **"PUNCH IT."** In Session 7, with Jetson passed out, [Luckyluciano](../player-characters/luckyluciano-ruth.md) took over most of the piloting, with [Korran](../player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md) covering enough for her to take a long rest.

### The accidental protection

Something aboard prevented teleportation extraction. [Alkgleb](../npcs/alkgleb.md): *"I have a teleportation circle on board, but something on board is preventing me from grabbing you?"*

That something is almost certainly the curse on [Tamenik](../player-characters/tamenik-freelin.md)'s shell that blocks scrying and teleportation. If [Megan](../npcs/megan-forthfang.md) successfully removes it, the party loses this defence.

### Vulnerability

The Githyanki at Warden's Maw can probably **scry the ship itself** — Jetson's warning was that they shouldn't stop, or the Githyanki might catch up. They stopped repeatedly, and the Githyanki never appeared.

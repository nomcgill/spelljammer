# Plot Threads

Every thread the campaign has opened, with its status as of Season 2, Session 1. Each thread is tagged with the season it opened in — `(S1)` for Season 1, `(after S1)` for threads raised between seasons, `(S2)` for Season 2. Sourced from [`season1/session-notes.md`](../season1/session-notes.md) and the [Spelljammer Adventure Ideas](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1qV2FOTgUu6EJ_Ki1f8F3XIHX3K64nYGXikZsHe_P-h8/edit) doc.

## Open and pressing

**Pip's revolution** `(S1)`. Now on the Rock of Bral, Pip plans to **gather allies and lead a revolution against his usurping brother, the plasmoid king**. Pip sees the brother as the usurper, even though it was Pip's own coup that failed.

**The Case of the Missing Stromboli** `(after S1)`. This began as Nicole's pitch: Babe Ruth's legendary Stromboli recipe has been stolen, all signs point to the rival **Joisey Jiffs**, and Lucy wants it back to prove herself to her father. Nicole's twist: *"What if the recipe was never meant to be stolen?"* Full pitch in [Luckyluciano's file](../player-characters/luckyluciano-ruth.md#pitch-the-case-of-the-missing-stromboli).
- **Now live (Season 2, Session 1):** Marfa asked the party to help solve it. She says it **began at the corner table** of the [Bral flagship Pie Parlor](../locations/bral-pie-parlor.md).
- **Nolan's plan:** it's solved by the end of Session 2 and sends the party to the Ral'taka Expanse. Krysta's head holds clues to the theft of the recipe's ingredient. Prep is in [`season2/session2-brainstorm.md`](../season2/session2-brainstorm.md).

**The Kenku assassin** `(S2)`. A Kenku tried to dart the table the moment Marfa raised the Stromboli case. The dart hit Krysta's head, and the Kenku disintegrated itself with a ruby ring rather than be taken.
- **Open:** who hired it, and whether it was after Marfa or the head.
- **Nolan's plan:** the Iron Annex contracted it. The players don't know this. They'll probably need to go to the Annex to find out, since a lot of questionable contract work goes through it.

**The ruby ring** `(S2)`. A homebrew **Ring of Disintegrate** with **one charge left**. The players don't know what it is yet, and whether they picked it up is `[unconfirmed]`. See [`items.md`](../items.md).

**Marfa knows everything** `(S2)`. The party told her the whole Season 1 story at breakfast. She's the family's dealmaker, so she now knows exactly what each of them wants and how to trade for it.

**Lucy vs. the succession** `(after S1)`. Lucy wants infamy and fortune, and to prove to Babe (her father and patron) that she can run the family. Babe intends it for her older sister [Bugsy](../npcs/bugsy.md), Jetson's mother, who doesn't want it.

**The Damselfly left behind** `(S1)`. The party left the Damselfly, with A.R.I.A. offline aboard it, beside the plasmoid mothership so they could get away. What happens to it and A.R.I.A. now is open.

**Korran's stolen ship** `(S1)`. Korran hijacked a Shrike out of the mothership's loading bay. The plasmoids may want it back. It still has to be **registered at the DAV**, which wipes its scry trace. The costs were waived at the dock thanks to Marfa. See [`ships/korrans-stolen-ship.md`](../ships/korrans-stolen-ship.md).

**The Ruth family, up close** `(S1)`. Marfa rescued the party in the *Slice Runner* and they owe her. How she got Lucy's impounded ship back is `[unconfirmed]`, and Jetson still lost the family's *DiceGunner*.

**Kirrak wants Tamenik back** `(S1)`. Tamenik's nephew runs Freelin Corporation and had him imprisoned for being annoying with his drums. He paid his way onto a plasmoid warship to collect him, and the party slipped away. Freelin Corporation is based on the Rock of Bral, where the party now is. Tamenik's own read, from his backstory: if they meet, Kirrak would have him detained as a threat, assuming he wants revenge. **Season 2, Session 1:** Tamenik went home to Freelin Corporation while Kirrak is still away.

**The plasmoid king wants Pip** `(S1)`. Pip led a failed coup against his brother, who is king. Alkgleb serves that king. The plasmoids call Pip their "slime-brother-nemesis."

**Tamenik can't be scried or teleported** `(S1)`. Something about his shell blocks divination and teleportation. It is why the plasmoids scried Pip instead to find him, and why the teleportation circle couldn't extract the party ("something on board is preventing me from grabbing you"). Megan suspects a curse and has offered to try removing it — he hadn't attempted it as of the end of Session 7. Worth noting that this curse is currently also protecting the party. Marfa's beam-out worked anyway. Nolan hasn't decided how yet; his working idea is that the curse can be bypassed with Tamenik's consent. **Season 2 prologue:** Megan casts Remove Curse and lifts it — at least, Tamenik thinks so. Tamenik himself is out of Season 2.

## Open and slower-burning

**Korran's treasure map** `(S1)`. He now holds both halves of a Thieves' Cant map to the hoard the original *Star-Reaver* crew took from his homeland, including Hadozee gold. The cipher is his late mother's birthday, relayed via Tamenik's dream. He has made "a little progress" decoding it. Two symbols near the destination remained unreadable before the cipher was known.

**The Ral'taka Expanse** `(S1)`. Korran's home plane — a jungle world of floating islands, rich in gold that the Hadozee tribes treated as part of nature. The map gives navigation instructions through it. This is the obvious destination once Bral is behind them.

**The *Star-Reaver* itself** `(S1)`. Found derelict in a toxic air envelope, hull built from a dead god's ribcage, still loaded with plundered magic items the party may or may not have taken. Korran betrayed and killed its original crew as revenge for the sacking of his homeland. Per his backstory: some of that crew, or partnered crews, may still be alive and may want him dead. Where the ship is now, and whether anyone reclaims it, is open.

**Megan Forthfang's real story** `(S1)`. A Leonin who says he is King of the Red Lions of Thorndumm, has never been on a spaceship, was petrified by a plasmoid High Wizard named Gelp, and fights and heals like a level 17 paladin. Nolan's own prep note asks what his story actually is. He owes Korran a debt.

**Gelp the plasmoid High Wizard** `(S1)`. Cast the Flesh to Stone that trapped Megan. Best wizard of Pip's **brother the king**. Unseen so far. The king is still unnamed. Pip's separate cousin (who served the king through the coup) is also still unnamed.

**Krysta's head** `(S1)`. The party is carrying the severed head of a Psurlon Ringer wearing the memories of Megan's daughter. Megan claimed Speak with Dead, but per Nolan he doesn't know it. Nolan's prep asks what Krysta's story was and how it bleeds into the Psurlon who ate her. Her body, back at the shipyard, had an IOU from Babe Ruth's Galactic Pie Parlors on it — connecting the Psurlons to Luckyluciano's family.

**The Ruth Syndicate** `(S1)`. Luckyluciano's imprisonment came out of the Crustfall Conspiracy, a sting that broke open the family's pizza-parlor money laundering. Her cousin Tino "The Saucepan" Fennetti flipped and named her. Her ship, the *Slice Runner*, was found adrift full of Infernal runes. Tino is still out there.

**Pip's diamond stomach** `(S1)`. He is carrying 100,000 gp of diamonds internally. Some went into the Hyperdrive Core. The Chwinga finds this hilarious.

**The Charm of Ruin** `(S1)`. Pip can disintegrate a nonmagical 5-foot cube three times, then it vanishes. Uses spent so far are not recorded. Granted by the Chwinga after the Cosmic Horror fight — **not** related to curing Blackstar Fever.

**The Chwinga** `(S1)`. Freed from a Feyr's bronze eye, plays hide and seek, froze time once already, and turned up aboard the Damselfly mid-boarding. Nolan's prep: once found, an enormous anti-magic circle erupts. What happened to it after the boarding is unknown.

From the Season 1 adventure ideas doc and prep notes. The complete hook list, kept current, is in [`season2-planning.md`](season2-planning.md#nolans-hook-bank).

- Plane shifting is available and explicitly doesn't help them.
- With the spelljamming helm damaged, a one-day journey becomes 445 years. "Good thing time is almost just a construct out here."

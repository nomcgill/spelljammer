# Season 1 Encounters

Every combat encounter of Season 1, with the creatures reconstructed.

**Why this file exists.** The encounter links in Nolan's notes point at [D&D Beyond's encounter builder](https://www.dndbeyond.com/my-encounters), which is private to his account — the rosters can't be read from outside. But the notes name the monsters, so the rosters below are rebuilt from those names, and the stat summaries were looked up from public references. Anything not named in the notes is marked unknown rather than guessed at.

The party was **level 7** throughout Season 1.

## Session 1 — Against 2 Githyanki

Original link: [Encounter Against 2 Githyanki](https://www.dndbeyond.com/encounters/44be5be2-1860-4280-809e-815e9a9e0195)

In the hangar, escorting Jetson to the ships. The two guards, [Aryndac](../npcs/aryndac.md) and Aristeth, had just slaughtered a plasmoid and had their backs turned to "secure the Damselflies."

| Creature | CR | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Githyanki Gish ×1 (Aristeth) | 10 | AC 17, 123–130 HP. Longsword strikes add 4d8 psychic. Innate *plane shift*, *telekinesis*, *misty step*; 8th-level wizard spells including *counterspell*, *fireball*, *haste*. War Magic and Astral Step (recharge 4–6). *Monsters of the Multiverse* p. 140 |
| Second Githyanki ×1 (Aryndac) | `[unconfirmed]` | Stat block never specified. Wields a poisoned longsword; has *misty step* (used mid-fall) and can teleport at will |

**How it resolved.** The party killed Aristeth. Gu's obliterated remains tackled Aryndac off the harbor into the Astral Sea — and Aryndac Misty Stepped aboard the Damselfly mid-fall, which is how he became the Session 2 stowaway.

## Session 2 — Aryndac

Original link: [Encounter - Aryndac](http://dndbeyond.com/encounters/5f1367a5-f105-4105-bef4-610d494d0254)

A solo fight at the hyperspace drive against the stowaway. He pulled his longsword out of the drive, smirked, and teleported away — so this was a scripted exit, not a fight to the death.

His longsword carries a **Githyanki poison** that attacks the target's "synaptic transmissions," causing hallucination and death within 48 hours untreated. This is homebrew; see [`house-rules.md`](../house-rules.md).

## Sessions 2 and 3 — 1 × Feyr

Original link: [Encounter - 1 x Feyr](https://www.dndbeyond.com/encounters/08218d7c-4da5-4f0c-a96e-dbd9509c6741)

Referenced at the end of Session 2 ("upon the next jump — they are followed") and again in Session 3.

| Creature | CR | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feyr ×1 | 5 | Large aberration, AC 16, 88 HP, fly 50 ft. (hover), darkvision 120 ft. **Invisibility** and **Nightmare Fuel (1/day)**. Immune to frightened. *Boo's Astral Menagerie* p. 22 |

A feyr that finds a spelljamming ship enters its air envelope and stows away, staying hidden and invisible until it finds a sleeping victim. It feeds quietly on **strong emotions** and prefers to devour **nightmares while victims sleep**. It retreats rather than dying in a confrontation — which fits a creature that "followed" the party.

Notable: Luckyluciano **burned half the cargo rations with a fireball** trying to kill it. Also, the Feyr's **bronze eyes** turn out to have held [the Chwinga](../npcs/chwinga.md)'s trapped essence, which is a Nolan invention — the published creature's eye colour isn't specified.

## Sessions 3 and 4 — 1 × Cosmic Horror

Original link: [Encounter - 1 x Cosmic Horror](https://www.dndbeyond.com/encounters/68968d04-3c44-4f23-920d-ca38d417e2ea) · [monster page](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2821160-cosmic-horror)

The big one. Found at the first of A.R.I.A.'s three dead-god coordinates.

| Creature | CR | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cosmic Horror ×1 | 18 | Gargantuan aberration, AC 15, **280 HP**, speed 50 ft. / fly 100 ft., darkvision 240 ft. Legendary Resistance. Multiattack, Bite, Tentacle, **Crushing Tentacle**, **Psychic Whispers**, **Poison Jet**, **Teleport**. Immune to acid and poison; immune to charmed, frightened, poisoned. Telepathy 240 ft., Deep Speech. *Boo's Astral Menagerie* p. 18 |

A CR 18 monster against four level-7 characters is roughly quadruple a deadly encounter, and it was never meant to be beaten. It was run as a **survival clock**: the Horror tore a wing off the ship, the party manned the three roof cannons at **8d8 force damage per blast**, and the real objective was surviving **5 turns (30 seconds)** for the hyperspace drive to spin up. Nolan noted they were 3 of 5 turns in.

Mid-fight, three of its eyes fixed on Pip and time stopped — the Chwinga's interruption.

## Session 5 — Leonins & Psurlons at the Shipyard

Original link: [ENCOUNTER - Leonins & Psurlons at the Shipyard](https://www.dndbeyond.com/encounters/00c66241-2f07-4186-9184-9a2323f07031)

| Creature | CR | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Psurlon Ringer ×1 ([Krysta](../npcs/krysta.md)) | 1 | Must consume a Medium humanoid, then trance 8 hours to permanently take its appearance, memories and languages — undone only by *wish*. Mage armor, psychic crush (3d8+3), telepathy 120 ft. Other psurlons still recognise it. *BAM* pp. 44–45 |
| Leonin Iconoclast ×? | 5 | 67 HP. **Banishment 1/day**, *detect evil and good*. Chooses its battles and only fights when sure of victory. *Mythic Odysseys of Theros* p. 232 |
| Psurlons ×? | 2 | Rank and file. A Psurlon Leader is CR 6 if needed |

[Megan](../npcs/megan-forthfang.md) is not one of the Iconoclasts. He runs on **[Xenk Yendar](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/3342714-xenk-yendar)** — CR 10, AC 15, Oath of Devotion paladin, a Daggersword that transforms between a 30-ft. ranged impaling attack and a 10-ft. area flurry, and **Cleansing Touch** healing 27 HP once per day. Nolan's note: Megan is **as strong as a level 17 paladin**, so the printed block is a floor. He also cast a high-level Greater Restoration and claimed Speak with Dead, which isn't on Xenk's sheet either. Nolan has since ruled that he **doesn't know** Speak with Dead.

Exact numbers of Psurlons and Leonins aren't recorded. Session 5 separately mentions **5 Psurlons** trying to spell their way into the ship, and Session 6 says the party escaped "by running and killing a few Psurlon."

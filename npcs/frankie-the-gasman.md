# Frankie "the Gasman"

**Appears in:** Season 2

The [Ruth](../factions.md#the-ruth-syndicate) family's lawyer. Named by [Luckyluciano](../player-characters/luckyluciano-ruth.md) in Season 2, Session 4. Full name, species, and where he works are `[unconfirmed]`.

## Status

Alive, offscreen. Has not appeared at the table.

## Season 2

### Session 4

At the [Iron Annex](../locations/iron-annex.md), Lucy told Director Tallyworth to contact her lawyer, Frankie the Gasman, while trying to talk the party out with [Tino Fennetti](tino-fennetti.md). Her Persuasion failed. Tallyworth did not recognize her as a Ruth.

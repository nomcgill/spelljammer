# Marfa Ruth

**Appears in:** Season 1, Season 2

[Luckyluciano](../player-characters/luckyluciano-ruth.md)'s mother. Wife of [Babe Ruth](babe-ruth.md) and, by her own framing, the head of the Pie Parlors. The cavalry.

## Status

On the [Rock of Bral](../locations/rock-of-bral.md). She now **knows the party's entire story**, because they shared everything at breakfast. She has asked for their help solving **the Case of the Missing Stromboli**, and a Kenku assassin has just tried to put a dart into her table.

## How she works

The social genius of the family, and Babe Ruth's spouse. She learns about people, works out what they can do for her and how to pull their strings, then makes trades. Her name alone moves Bral's dock officers.

## Family

Mother of Luckyluciano and her older sister, [Bugsy](bugsy.md). Bugsy's son [Jetson](jetson.md) is Marfa's **grandson** and Luckyluciano's nephew.

Notably, she came for her daughter *and* her grandson, not for the rest of the party. She beamed everyone out anyway.

## Season 1

### Contact

She reached Luckyluciano through her ship's Sending comms:

> "Thank the CRUST GODS, you're finally on the same plane! I've got the ___name of ship___ and I'll be there in a few moments to help!"

The blank is the *Slice Runner* — Luckyluciano's own ship.

### The threat

She radioed the plasmoid mothership directly and told [Alkgleb](alkgleb.md) and [Kirrak](kirrak-freelin.md):

> "If any of your men or that Plasmoid fleet harms my daughter or my grandson, no Plasmoid will ever do Pizza business or other business with my husband Babe Ruth or our Pie Parlors again!"

This is real leverage — the Ruth syndicate's parlors are a laundering network across Wildspace and the plasmoids are customers. Nolan's prep note said the mothership would **eventually ignore** the request. Whether the threat mattered, or whether the rescue came down to timing alone, is `[unconfirmed]`.

### The rescue

She arrived in the [*Slice Runner*](../ships/ruth-family-ships.md#the-slice-runner) just in time at the end of Session 7 — how she got the ship back from impound is still undecided — **beamed the party off the crippled Damselfly**, [Jetson](jetson.md) and [Megan](megan-forthfang.md) included, and flew them to Bral, with [Korran](../player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md) alongside in his [stolen ship](../ships/korrans-stolen-ship.md).

## Season 2

### Session 1

- **At the docks:** her influence got the party past [Lawrence](lawrence.md) with only names taken, and got the registration costs on Korran's ship waived. She watched closely as the group described themselves as "evil," "violent," "a prince," and so on.
- **Breakfast:** she invited everyone except Tamenik, who had left, to breakfast at the [Bral flagship Pie Parlor](../locations/bral-pie-parlor.md). She shooed Bugsy away and sent Jetson to "join his mother."
- **The story:** she heard the whole Season 1 story. Then she asked for help with the family's **Case of the Missing Stromboli**, which **began at this very table**.
- **The attack:** at that moment a Kenku assassin by the exit fired a poison dart. Megan blocked it with Krysta's head. Whether she or the head was the target is open.

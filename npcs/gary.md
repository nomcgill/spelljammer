# Gary

**Appears in:** Season 2

A **Giff** with a big rifle, working as [Tino Fennetti](tino-fennetti.md)'s guard. He's less well dressed than Tino. Last name `[unconfirmed]`.

## Status

Alive. He walked away from the Iron Annex in Season 2, Session 3, and where he went is `[unconfirmed]`.

## Season 2

### Session 3

He was with Tino in the [Iron Annex](../locations/iron-annex.md) director's office, covering Tino's escape out a stuck window. Tino's order: *"Occupy them! This is why we pay you the big bucks!"*
- **Lucy's Suggestion:** she tried to make him stop Tino. He made the save.
- **The grenade:** he threw a glowing **force grenade** that hit Lucy, Pip and Megan for 10 each, and Lucy punched him for it.
- **Giving up:** once Tino went down, he asked, *"Do you guys mind if I just leave?"* Pip asked if he could eat him. Gary tossed a **protein bar** on the floor instead and left. Pip ate the protein bar.

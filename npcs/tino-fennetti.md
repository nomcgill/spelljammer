# Tino "The Saucepan" Fennetti

**Appears in:** Season 1, Season 2

A **Giff** (Nolan), a **crust captain** of the Ruth Syndicate, and [Luckyluciano](../player-characters/luckyluciano-ruth.md)'s cousin. The informant who put her in prison.

## Status

**Captured, barely conscious, in the party's hands** in the Iron Annex director's office (Season 2, Session 3). Beaten down non-lethally while trying to escape out a window.

## Background

Overambitious, and caught during a failed jellyfish venom deal with a deep-space beholder cartel. Offered immunity, he gave up everything — including Luckyluciano's role as [Babe Ruth](babe-ruth.md)'s personal warlock and operator of the Rock of Bral front.

His testimony was the core of **the Crustfall Conspiracy** sting. He claimed Luckyluciano had modified her warlock pact magic to charm port inspectors, teleport contraband with eldritch shortcuts, and bind imprisoned souls into cursed pizza ovens to hide evidence. Her backstory document notes, pointedly, that this **may or may not be true.**

## Why he matters

He is unfinished business. A family member turned informant, still alive somewhere, in a syndicate run by Luckyluciano's parents — and whose matriarch just flew into a warship standoff to get her back. Whatever the Ruths have done about Tino in the meantime has not been established.

See [`factions.md`](../factions.md) for the syndicate and the Crustfall Conspiracy.

## Season 1

Never appeared at the table. Known only from Luckyluciano's backstory.

## Season 2

### What the players don't know (Nolan's decision, planned for Session 3)

**Tino is the one who hired The Unkindness.** He's the middleman behind the Case of the Missing Stromboli:
- **What he stole:** being family, he knew about Babe's folder, the reopening night and the back kitchen. He hired the theft of the recipe card for **the grower's mark** at the bottom, not the recipe. The recipe was never the point.
- **Why:** a grudge against the Ruths, nowhere left to go, and a mark worth a fortune to the right buyer.
- **Who he sold it to:** a buyer **already gone to the Ral'taka Expanse**. Still the placeholder *Varrow & Daughters*, which the agent invented in Session 2 prep. It might be from the *Star-Reaver*'s past, but that's the agent's pitch and isn't decided.
- **Why the head was the target:** Tino bought **the route into the Expanse from the Psurlons**, and the Ringer wearing Krysta was there for the deal, so the head can identify him. He paid with a **Ruth IOU**, which is the Pie Parlors IOU found on Krysta's body.
- **Why he's at the Annex:** to raise the fee after the first Kenku failed, and to make sure the head is finished.

### Session 3

- **The back door:** it played as planned. During the lobby fight he opened the back door, saw the fight and shut it. **Only Lucy saw him**, and she whispered "Saucepan."
- **The contract:** Tilptek confirmed he'd been at the desk five minutes before the party arrived and **placed the contract** that the Kenku picked up. The Kenku itself only knew "an anonymous source."
- **The window:** the party found him in the director's office, squeezing out a stuck, half-open window while his guard [Gary](gary.md) covered him. *"Occupy them! This is why we pay you the big bucks!"*
- **Going down:**
  - Korran's Faerie Fire outlined him.
  - Pip shot him with a musket, and Megan's thrown longsword blade hit him.
  - Lucy's grapple failed, but she caught his coat and **a card fell out**: *Varrow & Daughters, Fine Spices*, with a dock address.
  - Korran stabbed him down non-lethally.
- **Now:** the party hauled him back inside, **barely conscious.** His confession hasn't been played yet.

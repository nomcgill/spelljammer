# Season 2, Session 3: Brainstorm

Ideas for what comes after the Kenku fight in the Iron Annex. What happened in Session 2 is in [`session-notes.md`](session-notes.md#where-session-3-picks-up). The unused scripts from Session 2 (Korran's flashback, the fight, the agent's escape, Tallyworth, Megan's goodbye, the DAV, the spice house) are in [`session2-plan.md`](session2-plan.md#4-the-iron-annex).

**Spoilers throughout. Nothing here is canon until it's run.** All of it is my proposal unless marked as Nolan's.

## Where things stand

- **The scene:** the Iron Annex lobby, in the Kenku's magical Darkness. Only Lucy can see. Tilptek is typing. Megan is there with Krysta's head, which the players now know is the target.
- **What the players don't know:**
  - The Kenku's name (Scritch, in the plan).
  - That the Darkness came from a ring, or that he has a Disintegrate ring too.
  - That the client's agent is in the building.
- **What they still want:**
  - Who hired the Kenku, and why.
  - Money for Megan's Speak with Dead at the nearby temple.
  - The Shrike registered and named.
  - Marfa's rewards: a crew of 6 for Korran, and coup support for Pip.
- **Where the season needs to go** (Nolan): **off Bral, toward the Ral'taka Expanse.** The case should close on Bral with the client already gone.

## The face at the back door (Nolan's opening beat)

**Nolan's beat:** in round one, just before Lucy's turn, **the person who hired the Kenku opens the lobby's back door**, sees the fight, is startled, and shuts it again.

**Why it works:** everyone inside the Darkness is blind, so **Lucy is the only one who sees who it is.** The Kenku "sees" by echolocation within 30 feet, so he may not notice at all. Then it's Lucy's turn: chase them, shout, or keep fighting. Whoever it is should be **someone Lucy would recognize on sight**, because that's what makes the moment hit.

### Who it could be

**Decided (Nolan): it's Tino**, with everything listed under option 1. The other options stay here for reference.

1. **Tino "The Saucepan" Fennetti** — **Nolan's choice.** Lucy's cousin, the informant whose testimony put her in Warden's Maw. He's been missing ever since, "presumably in witness protection or worse."
   - **Why him:** he's family, so he knows about **Babe's folder**, the **reopening night** and the back kitchen. He knows exactly what to steal and when. He has a grudge against the Ruths and nowhere to go, and the grower's mark is worth a fortune to the right buyer.
   - **Why the head was the target:** Tino brokered the deal that bought **the route into the Expanse from the Psurlons**. The Ringer wearing Krysta was in the room for it, so the head can identify him. It may also explain the **Pie Parlors IOU on Krysta's body**: Tino paid with a Ruth IOU. This is my idea, not established.
   - **What it gives Nicole:** the person who sent her to prison, caught mid-betrayal, and she's the only one who saw him. It also delivers her pitch's twist, *"what if the recipe was never meant to be stolen?"*: the recipe was never the point, only the mark at the bottom of the card.
   - **How it keeps Korran's past in play:** Tino is the middleman, not the end buyer. He sold the mark to **someone heading to the Expanse**, still the placeholder Varrow & Daughters, possibly from the *Star-Reaver*'s past (check with Brad). He came to the Annex today to raise the fee and make sure the head was finished.
2. **Bugsy.** Lucy's older sister, who doesn't want the family business.
   - **Why her:** the server heard "Bugsy" ask for the card. The plan explains that as Kenku mimicry, but that lead hasn't been played yet, so it could be true.
   - **Her motive:** selling the mark is her way out of the family. Or she's covering for someone.
   - **Costs:** it's a darker twist, it puts Jetson (her son, who is standing in the room) in an awful position, and it turns Lucy against her sister, not her rival.
3. **A Joisey Jiff.** The obvious suspects, which is the problem. Nicole's pitch says "all signs point to the Jiffs," and the twist only works if it's *not* them. Better as a red herring: a Jiff at the door means the Jiffs are a front for someone else.
4. **A stranger:** one of the "Daughters" of Varrow & Daughters. This keeps the plot pointed at Korran's past, but Lucy has nothing to react to. It works better as the person Tino answers to.

### What happens after the door closes

- **Lucy's turn:**
  - She can **chase** through the back door, alone and fast. This is where your Annex map comes in.
  - She can **shout what she saw.** The table hears "TINO?!" while the fight goes on.
  - Or she can **keep quiet and keep fighting**, keeping it to herself for now. That's a good roleplay choice for a mob daughter.
- **The Kenku hears the door too.** Once he realizes his client is exposed, he stops fighting for the head and covers the escape instead, maybe falling back toward the back door.
- **The chase through the back rooms** (the tube hub, the archive, the loading dock): Tino has **two hired Thugs** and a head start. At the loading dock, a waiting skiff or cargo lift gives a clear way to end the chase.
- **If they catch him:** an interrogation, with family stakes:
  - He admits he sold the mark to a buyer **already gone to the Ral'taka Expanse**, and that the head is the one thing that could tie him to the Psurlon deal.
  - **Lucy decides his fate.** Turn him over to Marfa for family justice, use him, or let him go. Whatever she picks says a lot about who she is now.
  - **Marfa** gets her scene: the nephew who sold out her daughter, caught again.
- **If he escapes:** he drops the **Varrow & Daughters trade card** that smells of gold-dusted pepper, from the Session 2 plan. Tino stays loose as a recurring villain who could turn up in the Expanse.

## What the fight's ending can set up

- **Scritch goes for the head with Disintegrate.** This is the big moment. If it works, the head is dust, Speak with Dead is lost, and the other clues have to carry the case. If the party stops him, the head is saved and they've earned it.
- **Scritch disintegrates himself** if cornered, like the first Kenku. The party might get his Darkness ring. They didn't spot it as a ring, so finding one in the dust is a small reveal.
- **The agent slips out the back** while everyone is blind. Lucy can see in the Darkness, so she's the one who might spot them going, and that sets up the chase.

## Using your Iron Annex map

None of these are required. They're ways to use the map if you want more combat or exploration.

1. **The chase out the back** (already in the Session 2 plan). The agent, two Thugs and maybe a wounded Scritch run through the back rooms: the **tube hub**, the **archive** and the **loading dock**. Every room has something to use: tubes firing contracts, archivists who keep filing, a cargo lift.
2. **The archive heist.** If the agent gets away, **the contract file** is still in the archive, and it has the client's name, the fee and the target. The Annex is all about "Discretion," so the party has to talk, sneak or fight their way to the file. **Tilptek is Lucy's ex**, so he might let them in, for a price or for old times' sake.
3. **The audience with Director Tallyworth.** When the lights come back, the speaker crackles: *"This is Director Tallyworth. Results... are pending."* Then the party is summoned upstairs. The autognome is coldly businesslike:
   - **Damages will be invoiced**, to the Ruth family.
   - **He'll void the contract,** for a counter-fee, or for a favor.
   - **He won't name the client,** because Discretion is the brand. But he might sell **a document** instead, such as a dock manifest showing where the client's ship is going.
   - **A long hook:** he already knows about Pip (the Annex arranged the Season 1 scrying). He can let the party know that he knows, without saying more.

## Getting the head questioned

If the head survives, this is probably the strongest scene of the session.

- **The money:** Megan needs the temple's fee. Pip is still carrying whatever diamonds didn't go into the Hyperdrive Core (how many is `[unconfirmed]`), so paying means a disgusting, funny moment. Lucy could ask Marfa. Korran could haggle.
- **The temple** is the nearest one, nondescript (Nolan). The priest only needs a single line of personality.
- **The rule:** Speak with Dead allows **five questions**. Let the players pick them together, with Megan pushing for his own. It's a strong table moment because there are never enough questions.
- **The twist:** the head answers with **the Psurlon Ringer's memories mixed with Krysta's**. Some answers come in her voice and some in something else's. The poison from the first dart has also been eating at it, so answers can come out broken.
- **What it could reveal** (from the Session 2 brainstorm):
  - The client's face.
  - **The route into the Ral'taka Expanse.** The Ringer learned it, and this is the strongest reason to go.
  - News of Megan's war: the Red Lions against the Brown Lions.
  - What the Pie Parlors IOU on Krysta's body was for.
  - How Krysta died.

## Closing the case on Bral

- **The spice house** (from the Session 2 plan): the party recovers **Babe's recipe card with the bottom torn off.** The client's ship has already left for the Expanse with the strip.
  - The "pepper man" clue wasn't planted in Lucy's flashback, so the torn edge needs another explanation. Options: **Marfa** recognizes what's missing, or **Babe** does (a chance to bring him on screen), or **Korran** recognizes the gold-dusted pepper smell from home.
  - **The cold open pays off:** the players already saw a ship arrive in the Expanse with a torn strip of card. When they see the torn card, they should get it.
- **The DAV:** the Shrike is registered and **Brad names it**. The register shows the client's ship leaving toward the Expanse days ago.
- **If time is short,** skip the spice house. Tallyworth's document or the head's answers can point straight to the Expanse.

## Reasons to leave Bral for the Expanse

Try to have at least one for each character before the session ends:

- **Lucy:**
  - The client has the mark that makes Babe's pepper supply possible, and maybe all of Babe's legacy. If they take over the growers, there's no more Stromboli.
  - Bringing back the strip is how she proves herself to her patron, her father.
- **Korran:**
  - It's his homeland.
  - Someone is going there using a trade relationship with his people.
  - He has the map.
  - If the client turns out to be from the *Star-Reaver*'s past, it gets personal. Check with Brad.
- **Pip:**
  - Marfa's support for the coup is the payment for closing the case properly.
  - The Expanse's gold is a war chest for the revolution.
- **Megan,** if he's still with them: the head's answers might point the same way, or he might leave to find his war, and show up again in the Expanse later. That return is already decided.

## Ending the session

**A launch.**
- The Shrike, newly named, leaves Bral with **Marfa's crew of 6** aboard. That's a good chance to introduce one or two crew members with names and personalities.
- **Jetson** at the helm (or in the co-pilot seat, "learning the business") gets to say his line: **"PUNCH IT."**
- Cut to black, or cut to the Expanse, where the growers are worried about their "friend."

## Possible running order

1. **Korran's flashback**, set off by the Darkness, then the fight.
2. **Round one: the face at the back door**, just before Lucy's turn.
3. **The chase or the fallout:** whoever it was escapes or is caught, the lights come back on, and Tallyworth speaks.
4. **The contract or the archive**, if you want to use the map. Otherwise go straight to the temple.
5. **The temple:** five questions for the head.
6. **The spice house or the DAV**, whichever the players chase.
7. **Launch.**

- Who are the crew of 6? Give one or two of them names.
- Who is the client? Varrow & Daughters is still a placeholder. Check with Brad if it's from Korran's past.

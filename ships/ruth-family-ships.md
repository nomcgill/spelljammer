# Ruth Family Ships

**Appears in:** Season 1

The [Ruth syndicate](../npcs/babe-ruth.md)'s vessels: a matched pair of Flying Fish.

## The Slice Runner

[Luckyluciano](../player-characters/luckyluciano-ruth.md)'s own ship, now flown by her mother, [Marfa](../npcs/marfa-ruth.md).

Seized during the Crustfall Conspiracy sting. Per her backstory, it was **found adrift, dripping with illusory glamers and runes etched in Infernal** suggesting extra-dimensional storage compartments — which is exactly the kind of evidence [Tino Fennetti](../npcs/tino-fennetti.md)'s testimony described.

- It has **Sending comms** capable of reaching Luckyluciano across the void.
- It can **beam people aboard** from another ship. How that got past the anti-teleportation curse on [Tamenik](../player-characters/tamenik-freelin.md)'s shell is undecided. Nolan's working idea: maybe the curse can be bypassed with Tamenik's consent.
- How the ship got from impound back into Marfa's hands is undecided. Whether the Infernal-runed smuggling compartments are still full is also `[unconfirmed]`.

## The DiceGunner

A **Flying Fish**, sister ship to the *Slice Runner*. Presumably destroyed.

## Season 1

### The DiceGunner crash

[Jetson](../npcs/jetson.md) "borrowed" the *DiceGunner* from the family **without permission** and crashed it into Warden's Maw during the breakout. It was **shot down** in the escape.

That theft is a small unresolved family matter — Jetson took a syndicate ship without asking and lost it, and Marfa had to fly in to rescue him and his aunt Lucy. Whether his mother, [Bugsy](../npcs/bugsy.md), knows is `[unconfirmed]`.

### The Slice Runner rescue

In Session 7 Marfa used the *Slice Runner*'s Sending comms to reach Luckyluciano and radio her threat to the plasmoid mothership. She then arrived in it, **beamed the party off the crippled [Damselfly](the-damselfly.md)**, and carried them — Jetson and Megan included — to the [Rock of Bral](../locations/rock-of-bral.md), flying alongside [Korran's stolen ship](korrans-stolen-ship.md).

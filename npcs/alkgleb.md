# Alkgleb

**Appears in:** Season 1

Plasmoid. A **general of the plasmoid mothership**, serving [Pip](../player-characters/pip-oozewald.md)'s brother, the plasmoid king.

## Status

Alive and in command of the mothership. The party escaped him at the end of Session 7 — beamed out by [Marfa](marfa-ruth.md), while [Korran](../player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md) stole a ship out of his loading bay.

## What he wants

Pip. The plasmoids call Pip their "slime-brother-nemesis." Pip led a failed coup against his brother the king, and Alkgleb serves that king.

He also brought Kirrak along as a paying passenger, which means his mission has two targets: Pip for the king, and Tamenik for Freelin Corporation.

## Leverage against him

[Marfa Ruth](marfa-ruth.md) radioed the mothership directly and told Alkgleb and Kirrak that if any of their men or the plasmoid fleet harms her daughter or her grandson, no plasmoid will ever do pizza or black-market business with Babe Ruth's Pie Parlors again. Per Nolan's prep, the mothership **eventually ignores** this.

He also attempted extraction by teleportation circle and failed: *"I have a teleportation circle on board, but something on board is preventing me from grabbing you? I'll need to see your ship to get you. Can you survive?"* The thing preventing it is the curse on Tamenik.

## Season 1

### What he did

His mothership is the same bomber that attacked Warden's Maw in Session 1 to free [Gu](gu.md) — so the ship that started the campaign by accidentally freeing the party came back for them.

He stopped the Damselfly cold with a wave of **Dispel Magic** that worked like an EMP: auxiliary power only, monitor and comms barely functioning, [A.R.I.A.](aria.md) offline, emergency lighting throughout the ship.

He then gave the order to board, in **Primordial**: *"Send in the other ones."* What came aboard included [the silver plasmoid](silver-plasmoid.md).

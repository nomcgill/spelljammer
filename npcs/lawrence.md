# Lawrence

**Appears in:** Season 2

A **Giff** dock officer at the shipyard on the [Rock of Bral](../locations/rock-of-bral.md). He's a hard-G Giff, pronounced like the Ruths, and dressed like an officer. Last name `[unconfirmed]`.

## Status

On duty at the Bral docks.

## Who he is

Militaristic and by the book, until a Ruth shows up. His opening line, from the [Session 1 plan](../season2/session1-plan.md#2-the-dock-check):

> "OI! YOU THERE! Papers, permits, and planetary origin! No docking without proper documentation! And if I find so much as ONE unauthorized space hamster aboard, I'll have your vessel impounded!"

## Season 2

### Session 1

Received the party when the *Slice Runner* and Korran's Shrike docked side by side. He wanted ID and more, but took [Marfa](marfa-ruth.md)'s influence to heart and settled for names. He waived the costs, but told [Korran](../player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md) his ship would still need to be **registered when the DAV opened** a couple of hours later.

He has a written record of the names the party gave. Korran gave "not much of a last name."

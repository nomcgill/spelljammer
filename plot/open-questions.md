## Resolved contradictions: Season 1 rulings

Nolan's rulings on contradictions in the Season 1 notes, including the ones he made between seasons.

**Aristeth / Aryndac / Gu (Session 1).** Both hangar guards were Githyanki, not Giff. The party killed Aristeth. Gu **survived** the balcony blast, then tackled Aryndac off the harbor; Aryndac Misty Stepped aboard the Damselfly mid-fall (unnoticed) and became the Session 2 stowaway. Gu's fate after going over the edge is unknown to the party.

**The *Star-Reaver* captain's name.** Canon is **Captain Gloamcast**. "Aachen Eelson" from the Session 5 fever dream is discarded.

**Jetson and Luckyluciano.** Jetson is Luckyluciano's **nephew** — the son of her older sister [Bugsy](../npcs/bugsy.md). "Aunt Lucy" is literal, and Jetson is **Marfa and Babe's grandson**. This replaces the earlier ruling that they were cousins and "Aunt" was an honorific for the age gap. Marfa's radio threat and the Session 6 flashback were corrected from "nephew" to "grandson."

**The rival family's name.** The **Joisey Jiffs** — spelled the way it's pronounced, soft G, as opposed to the hard-G Ruth Giffs.

**Pip's family.** The plasmoid king is Pip's **brother**. [Gelp](../npcs/gelp.md) is that brother's best wizard. Pip also has a **separate cousin**, who served the king through the coup. The Chwinga's "you're shinier than your cousin" refers to this cousin, not the king.

**Pip's "usurping relative."** It's his **brother, the king**. Pip considers him the usurper, even though Pip's own coup against him failed.

**Kirrak and Tamenik.** Kirrak is Tamenik's **nephew**. Iron Annex flashback wording that called Tamenik Kirrak's "cousin" is discarded.

**Tilptek.** Desk clerk at the Iron Annex on the Rock of Bral — not aboard the mothership. The figures threatening the party from the bomber are **Alkgleb and Kirrak**.

**Tamenik's Blackstar Fever cure.** [Megan Forthfang](../npcs/megan-forthfang.md) — a **high-level paladin** — cured him by casting **Greater Restoration**. The Chwinga and the Charm of Ruin were not involved. (Session 5 prep listed unused alternatives: Megan's unicorn horn, or a Chwinga-granted cure. "Pip was charmed/blessed by a Chwinga" refers to the Charm of Ruin from the Cosmic Horror fight, not a fever cure.)

***Star-Reaver* loot.** [Korran](../player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md) took the **Fish Suit** and **Boots of Gravitation**. No one got the Spelljamming Helm, Star Chart of the Lost Spheres, or Void Crystal Focus.

**Marfa's ship.** It's the ***Slice Runner***, Luckyluciano's own ship.

**Korran's excursion.** He got into the mothership through a hull crack, found a smaller ship in the loading bay, hijacked it, and flew it to Bral alongside the *Slice Runner*.

**The Session 7 finale.** The party **killed** the silver assassin. The **Damselfly, with A.R.I.A. aboard, was left behind** so the party could get away.

**Luckyluciano is not the heir.** Babe sees the crime family eventually going to her **older sister Bugsy**, who doesn't want it; Lucy is out to prove she's capable of taking it. This overrides the backstory doc's "not just the heir."

**Astro Nommie's Slicehouse** is the Joisey Jiffs' pizza operation.

**Megan and Speak with Dead.** The Session 6 notes say Megan *claimed* to have Speak with Dead. Nolan's ruling after Season 1: he **doesn't know it**. Whether the in-play claim was a bluff or a mistake is `[unconfirmed]`.

**Luckyluciano's nickname.** It's **Lucy**, not "Lucky." Use her full name or Lucy. This reverses a short-lived ruling that she went by "Lucky" and only Jetson called her Lucy.

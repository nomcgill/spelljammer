## Background

**He led an unsuccessful coup against his brother, and that's why he was imprisoned.** His brother is the plasmoid king.

He also has a **separate cousin**, who served that brother through the coup. A plasmoid High Wizard named [Gelp](../npcs/gelp.md) is his **brother the king's** best wizard — the same Gelp who petrified [Megan Forthfang](../npcs/megan-forthfang.md) during the conquest of Thorndumm.

The plasmoid fleet calls Pip their **"slime-brother-nemesis."** He is the only party member whose family sent a warship.

## Season 1

The front line, and the reason the enemy can find them.

- **Session 4.** During the Cosmic Horror fight, three of its eyes fixed on him and **time stopped** for everyone else. [A Chwinga](../npcs/chwinga.md) had frozen it, having been freed from a Feyr's bronze eye. It called him "a funny color," offered to help the big thing attack the ship, and left him the **Charm of Ruin**.
- **Session 5.** Used a moment of distraction at the Psurlon shipyard to **ingest 50,000 gp of diamonds**. In the same beat, he noticed that someone, somewhere, was **scrying him**. He also earned an Inspiration — awarded for his player's birthday.
- **Session 6.** Total swallowed reaches **100,000 gp of diamonds**, which becomes the fuel for the Hyperdrive Core and brings the Rock of Bral back to one day away.
- **Session 7.** Attacked by the silent [silver plasmoid](../npcs/silver-plasmoid.md), which summoned a separate sword at range specifically to strike him. The party killed it, and Marfa beamed them to safety and on to the Rock of Bral.

## Season 2

- **Session 1.** He gave his name at the docks; the party described themselves to Marfa as, among other things, "a prince." At the Pie Parlor he ordered a pile of random toppings, including pineapple, anchovies and mushrooms, put it all on the table, and **engulfed it with his whole body**. He shared the whole Season 1 story with [Marfa](../npcs/marfa-ruth.md) and fought the Kenku assassin.

## Threads on him

**The revolution.** On reaching the Rock of Bral, Pip's plan is to **gather allies and lead a revolution against his usurping brother, the king**. In Pip's eyes the brother is the usurper, even though it was Pip's own coup that failed.

**He is the tracking beacon.** [Tamenik](tamenik-freelin.md) can't be scried, so the hunters scried Pip instead and followed him to Tamenik. Wherever Pip is, the hunters can find him — which makes his presence both the party's greatest liability and the reason the plot arrives on schedule.

He is carrying a fortune internally. The Chwinga thinks the diamond stomach is the funniest thing in Wildspace: *"You're shinier than your cousin"* — meaning Pip's cousin, not his brother the king.

And the ally the party rescued was petrified by **Gelp, his brother the king's wizard.** Megan doesn't appear to know yet.

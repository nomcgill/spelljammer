## Korran's flashback: the raid

Nolan read the opening script, with a few changes:
- The ribcage ship is **"the size of a large, large hill."**
- **Several other pirate ships** come with it, each with its own unique features. Korran still recognizes ships like them today. Pirates like their ships fancy and unusual, and don't change what earlier owners did to them.

What Brad and Nolan established:
- **His age:** about **10 in human years.**
- **Where he was:** on a **smaller, lower floating island** he often visited. He heard his mother calling across the sky, but the noise of the landing ships drowned her out.
- **Getting home:** he **glided from island to island** toward her, the way his people do.
- **Gold among the Cloudjumper Tribe:** gold is so plentiful it's **a building material**, built into their structures. It isn't lying around loose.
- **What his mother taught him:** gold is part of their tradition, **how their people honor the land.** Brad added that this is why **the growers sprinkle it on their plants.** Nolan confirmed it: the gold-dusting is the tradition of Korran's people, **the peoples of the Ral'taka Expanse**, who aren't necessarily all Hadozee.
- **The pirates:** some were **Hadozee**, others were races he'd never seen. They rounded up villagers, bound them, and lingered over the gold in the buildings. Some were clearly delighted; some acted like this was routine.
- **Korran's reaction:** he hid and **cried quietly**, panicking about his mother, his cousins, and the rest of his family.
- **The *Star-Reaver*** hung over the canopy without landing, and more pirates came down its ropes and nets.
- **Gloamcast's arrival:** Nolan read the script. **Captain Gloamcast** glided down, and the pirates went quiet. His mother stood **shoulders back, chin held falsely high**, braver than Korran had ever seen her. Gloamcast looked at her for a long moment. *"The gold. And any who'll fetch a price. Not her."*
- **Korran's attempt:** as they took her away, Brad rolled a **Wisdom save (17)**, and Korran **ran out to protect her.** He slipped between pirates and got close, then **bit and fought** a Hadozee standing next to the captain.
- **A retcon:** Nolan first narrated Gloamcast admiring Korran's courage and offering a deal: come with him, and the village is left alone. Brad said he'd imagined being **simply kidnapped**, the way real pirates forced children into crew work (being "Shanghaied"). **Nolan retconned it: there was no deal. They just took him.**
- **The ending:** Korran doesn't know what happened to his people or his mother. **"Did you ever see your mother again?" "Probably not."** There were several ships, so she could have been taken aboard any of them. **Korran was taken aboard the *Star-Reaver*.** Nolan confirmed afterward: **he never saw his mother again.**

## The fight in the lobby

**Initiative:** Lucy went first with a 20, rolled with advantage. Korran rolled around 16 to 18 **[?]**, and Pip an 8 **[?]**. Nicole later said Lucy had "only taken half her health," so the Kenku may have hurt her earlier **[?]**.

1. **Lucy cast Psychic Lance** ("Linguine of Fate"). The Kenku failed the Intelligence save (14 against DC 15): **24 psychic damage, incapacitated** until the start of her next turn. **That broke his concentration, and the Darkness ended.**
2. **Megan charged.** *"Not my allies!"* He hit with his short sword and dagger, at advantage. The Kenku was **bloodied.** Meanwhile, the party heard scuffling behind the staff door, and an automatic metal door opened and closed.
3. **Tino at the door:** in the round the door opened, Lucy saw who it was and **whispered "Saucepan."** Nobody else saw him, as planned.
4. **Korran** chose **non-lethal strikes** "to figure out who he works for": **25 damage** with Sneak Attack, then the blunt end of his dagger.
5. **Pip** attacked with a **scimitar**, adding a third attack with the Nick property: 18 damage. (He carries a scimitar, a musket and his warhammer.) He was raging. (Jetson stayed back, told by Lucy to "stay out of this.")
6. **Lucy asked Megan** whether to talk to the Kenku or kill him. Megan: **leave him alive, he has questions.**
7. **Lucy cast Suggestion:** *"We're gonna stop hitting you, you're gonna stop hitting us, and you're gonna tell us what you want with Megan and who hired you."* He failed (13). The table debated whether an incapacitated creature can still hear Suggestion, and **Nolan ruled that it works.**
8. **Korran went for the rings.** Everyone rolled Perception and missed, but Korran patted the Kenku down with **Sleight of Hand: 29.** He spotted twitching fingers, **slid two rings off**, flashed them to the party behind the Kenku's head, and pocketed them. The Kenku smells bad. **Korran doesn't know what the rings do yet.**

## The fight in the office

Initiative again. Lucy went first with a 23; Pip had advantage from Danger Sense.

- **Lucy** had recovered a spell slot with Magical Cunning. She cast **Suggestion on the guard** ("Gary, stop him"), but **he made the save (18).** Because Suggestion needs concentration, **the Kenku's Suggestion ended.**
- **Korran** cast **Faerie Fire**. **Tino failed and is outlined**; Gary saved.
- **Pip** dashed in and fired his **musket**, which the table flavored as a laser rifle, at advantage: **12 damage** to Tino. He raged again. (The table worked out that a weapon with the Loading property fires only once per turn.)
- **Megan** detached his longsword's blade and **launched it into Tino's backside.** It's part of Xenk Yendar's stat block, which Megan uses: the blade **magically reattaches to its hilt** at the end of the turn, so he has it back.
- **Tino** wriggled another foot out, but both feet stayed on the floor inside.
- **Gary** didn't like being ignored. He threw a **glowing force grenade** behind Lucy, Pip and Megan: Dexterity saves, **10 force damage** each. Lucy got an opportunity attack and **punched** him for 3.
- **A Giff ruling** from the table, mostly Nicole: **Giffs dress like Winnie the Pooh**, in shirts and no pants. Nolan made it canon.
- **Lucy tried to grapple Tino** back through the window. He broke it (23 against her DC 13), but as her hands slipped she caught his coat, and **a card fell out.**
- **Korran stabbed Tino twice** with Sneak Attack. **Tino went limp in the window**, beaten non-lethally because killing him "would have been a waste." Per Nolan he's **barely conscious**, not fully out.
- **Gary gave up:** *"Do you guys mind if I just leave?"* Pip asked if he could eat him; Gary threw a **protein bar** on the floor and ran. **Pip ate the protein bar.** The table warned him that people will try to poison a king's food.
- **The party hauled Tino back inside.**

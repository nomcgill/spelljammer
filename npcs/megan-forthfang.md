# Megan Forthfang

**Appears in:** Season 1, Season 2

Leonin. Claims to be the **King of the Red Lions** of Thorndumm. He left the party in Season 2, Session 4, and is the biggest question mark among the NPCs.

## Status

Alive, on the [Rock of Bral](../locations/rock-of-bral.md), **no longer with the party**. He left after Speak with Dead in Season 2, Session 4, still carrying [Krysta](krysta.md)'s head, to earn passage home. Owes [Korran](../player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md) a debt: *"My debt to you stands, Hadozee."*

## Stats

Runs on [Xenk Yendar](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/3342714-xenk-yendar)'s stat block — CR 10, AC 15, an Oath of Devotion paladin with a transforming Daggersword and a once-daily Cleansing Touch — **but Nolan's note says he is as strong as a level 17 paladin.** Treat the stat block as a floor, not a ceiling. Greater Restoration is a 5th-level paladin spell, so casting it "high-level" already puts him past the printed block.

## History

Plasmoids attacked his kingdom of Thorndumm. A plasmoid High Wizard named [Gelp](gelp.md) — Pip's brother the king's best wizard — caught him in a [Flesh to Stone](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2109-flesh-to-stone). He was then shipped as cargo and stored, petrified, in a container at the Psurlon shipping hub, like Han Solo in carbonite. A Scroll of Greater Restoration was stuck to the outside of his container with green goo.

He has never been on a spaceship before.

**He can't read Common** (Nolan, Season 2 Session 3). He never had to: he was raised as a warrior despite being royalty, because where he comes from, strength in arms is respected far more than learning.

## Open threads

He wanted to fight the Psurlons on sight, on the grounds that they are inherently evil.

His own daughter Krysta had been eaten and replaced by a Psurlon Ringer, and the party killed it and kept the head. Whether he knows this, and what he does about it, is unresolved.

**His first priority** (Nolan): getting **home to help in the war between the Red Lions and the Brown Lions**. He doesn't know how it's going. How that war relates to the plasmoid attack on Thorndumm is `[unconfirmed]`.

**He left in Session 4,** keeping Krysta's head.
- **Why he left:** he doesn't know where home is, and the party can't help him find it. He'll **earn his own passage**, and find someone on the Rock of Bral who knows where Thorndumm is. From what has been described of the Expanse, **he doesn't believe Thorndumm is there** (Nolan).
- **No hard feelings:** he isn't bitter. He still owes **Korran** his life. He told Pip he has **no debt to Pip**.

**What the players don't know:**
- **His return:** he comes back later, in the Ral'taka Expanse.

He **hadn't seen Krysta in years**. In Session 4 he learned she'd been in space, and that he should have asked the head how. Why she was out there is `[unconfirmed]`.

**His motivation going into Season 2** (Nolan): he doesn't know how to get home, he doesn't know anyone, and he'd like some insight into the IOU that was on his daughter's body. He doesn't know Speak with Dead, which would likely help solve the mystery of her death.

Nolan's prep note asks outright: **what is Megan's story?** A Leonin king who has never left his world, yet fights and heals like a 17th-level paladin, is a deliberate mystery.

## Season 1

### What he did for the party

- Freed from the stone by Korran at the Psurlon shipyard (Session 5).
- Cured [Tamenik](../player-characters/tamenik-freelin.md)'s **Blackstar Fever** by casting **Greater Restoration** as a high-level paladin (Session 6). This is the canonical cure.
- Healed [Jetson](jetson.md)'s Githyanki poison (Session 7).
- The Season 1 notes say he claimed to have Speak with Dead available. **Nolan's ruling after Season 1: he doesn't know Speak with Dead.** That matters, since the party is carrying [Krysta](krysta.md)'s head.
- Suspects a curse on Tamenik that blocks scrying and teleportation, and has offered to try removing it. Had not attempted it as of the end of Session 7. **Planned for the Season 2 prologue:** he casts Remove Curse and lifts it — or so Tamenik thinks.

He is also carrying, or was offered as an option, a **unicorn horn** — an alternative Blackstar Fever cure that went unused.

## Season 2

### Session 1

- **Breakfast:** he accepted Marfa's invitation to the [Bral flagship Pie Parlor](../locations/bral-pie-parlor.md) and heard the party tell Marfa the whole Season 1 story.
- **The attack:** when the Kenku assassin fired, Korran's missed dagger bought him time to **lift Krysta's head like a shield**, and the poison dart hit the head instead.

### Session 2

- He followed Korran to the dock and asked for advice on getting home. Korran told him to give up; **Megan said he would not.**
- He asked where to find a **religious establishment that could cast Speak with Dead** on Krysta's head. Korran's History roll pointed him to **the nearest temple** (nondescript), and **Megan left the party with Krysta's head**. 
- Later he **walked into the Iron Annex** behind the party, still carrying the head, to ask them for **money to pay the temple** for Speak with Dead. Tilptek pointed him out to the Kenku as the target, and the Kenku cast Darkness. The session ended there.

### Session 3

- **The lobby:** he charged the Kenku with *"Not my allies!"* and bloodied it. Asked by Lucy whether to kill it or question it, he wanted it **alive**, because he had questions.
- **Tino:** he followed the party into the back, holding Krysta's head tight. At the window he **launched his longsword's blade** into the fleeing Tino. The blade reattaches by magic, per Xenk Yendar's stat block.
- **Gary's grenade** hit him for 10 force.
- **The card:** he picked up the card that fell from Tino's coat, but **couldn't read it**.
- **Still with the party, and still carrying the head.**

### Session 4

- He helped carry Tino out of the Annex, and went with the party to the Pie Parlor basement and then the temple.
- **Speak with Dead:** Lucy paid 2 gp at a temple of Mastercat. Megan asked all five questions (see [Krysta](krysta.md)). He fell to his knees when Krysta's voice said she loved him.
- **Goodbye:** *"It is not my daughter, but it was the last thing that saw her. I have a kingdom at war. I must find a way home."* He wrapped the head in **his own jacket** and left.
  - To Korran: *"My debt to you stands, Hadozee. If you see me again, I still owe you."*
  - To Lucy: the pizza was very good. He will remember.
  - To Pip: no debt. He refused to promise the rebellion.

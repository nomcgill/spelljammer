## Session 1 — Breakfast at the Pie Parlor

Played October 12, 2026. Plan: [`session1-plan.md`](session1-plan.md). Unless noted below, the session ran in the order of the plan.

**Remove Curse prologue:** the plan placed Megan casting Remove Curse on Tamenik aboard the *Slice Runner* on approach. Nolan's report doesn't mention it. `[unconfirmed]`

### Arrival and the dock check

- The *Slice Runner* and Korran's Shrike **parked next to each other** at the shipyard on the Rock of Bral.
- **Lawrence**, a [Giff dressed like an officer](../npcs/lawrence.md), received the group at the docks. He wanted more from them, such as ID, but took **Marfa's influence** to heart.
- Lawrence said the **costs would be skipped**, but Korran's ship would **still need to be registered** when the DAV opened in a couple of hours.
- **Names:**
  - Korran said he had **"not much of a last name to give."**
  - Everyone else gave their names.
  - Luckyluciano (Lucy for short) said the *Slice Runner* was **"property of the Ruth family,"** not specifically hers or her mother's.
- **Marfa** watched the group closely as they described themselves as "evil," "violent," "a prince," and so on.
- **Tamenik** hurried off to withdraw money and get a ride home, where his nephew Kirrak would have left the CEO seat vacant.

### Breakfast with Marfa

- The three PCs, Megan and Jetson all **accepted Marfa's invitation** for pizza.
- The party **rolled well on Insight** and grasped why Marfa was treating them and wanted their story. Luckyluciano already knew her mother: Marfa wanted to see whether they could all be persuaded, through an exchange, to help the family solve **the Case of the Missing Stromboli**.
- At the Pie Parlor they briefly met **Bugsy**, who was shooed away. Jetson was told to "join his mother," and he went off to eat a **sausage and heavy cheese pizza** at the end of the bar.
- **The pizza orders:**
  - **Korran:** a pizza with everything on it, including fruits, vegetables and meats. Far too many toppings.
  - **Luckyluciano:** vegetarian, with anchovies.
  - **Pip:** a bunch of random stuff, including pineapple, anchovies and mushrooms, all on the table, and he **engulfed it with his whole body**.
- **Hawaiian Punch** is the primary drink at the Pie Parlors, their equivalent of Coca-Cola.

### The recap

The Season 1 recap took the form of the party telling Marfa their story. **They shared everything. Nothing is a secret from Marfa now.**

**A backstory clarification from Korran's telling:** he was captured after a few **failed raids on Githyanki outposts**. There were a few successful raids too, but the crew bit off more than it could chew. He was sent to Warden's Maw for **petty revenge**. Nolan calls this "overall partial truth."

### The Kenku assassin

- When the recap finished, Marfa finally asked for their help solving the Case of the Missing Stromboli, which **began at this very table**.
- The moment she said it, everyone rolled Perception. **Only Korran noticed** a **Kenku assassin** by the parlor's exit, blowgun raised.
- Korran threw a dagger and **missed**. That gave **Megan** time to **lift Krysta's head like a shield**, and the Kenku's **poison dart hit the head**.
- Everyone rolled initiative, and the whole party got attacks in on the Kenku.
- The Kenku went last. Badly hurt, it **pulled off its glove, revealed a ruby ring, and used the ring's Disintegrate on itself**. It turned to dust, and **only the ruby ring remains**.

**What the players don't know yet:** the ring is a homebrew **Ring of Disintegrate** with **one charge remaining**. See [`items.md`](../items.md).

**Open after the session:**
- Was the assassin after Marfa or Krysta's head?
- Who hired it?
- What did the poison do to the head?
- ~~Did anyone pick up the ring?~~ **Yes.** Korran had it, and sold it empty for 1 gp in Session 4.

## Session 2

Date `[unconfirmed]`. Plan: [`session2-plan.md`](session2-plan.md). The session covered the plan's scenes 1 to 3 and the start of scene 4, with real departures noted below. It ended on a cliffhanger in the Iron Annex.

### Cold open: the Ral'taka Expanse

Nolan read the plan's [cold open](session2-plan.md#1-cold-open-the-raltaka-expanse) **word for word, so it's canon.** The players watched it without their characters. What it establishes:
- A **Hadozee grower** on a small floating island picks peppers that glint because they're dusted with gold. The name "goldfleck pepper" wasn't said aloud.
- A strange ship arrived along an old trade route that **only one buyer ever uses**.
- A figure at the rail held up a **strip of card, torn along one edge**, with a mark in **an old Giff's handwriting** that the grower has known for years.
- The grower lowered their spear and said, *"Welcome back, friend."*

### The Pie Parlor, after the fight

Marfa's line was read as written:
> "Okay. Okay! Nobody touch the dust. Bugsy, honey, get the mop. No, the *other* mop." *(a breath)* "This happens. Not often, but it happens. When somebody wants a Ruth dead, there's only one place in this city they go to hire it done. The Iron Annex. So we go see who paid, and then we either pay more... or we lean on somebody until they wish we'd paid more."

### Pip's flashback

**Trigger:** Pip pulled his **warhammer** away with blood on it. The scene was heavily improvised and **departed from the plan's script.** What was played:
- **When:** some months or years before Season 1 begins at Warden's Maw.
- **The setup:** when Pip's father, the previous king, died, he arranged for his **three children to take turns ruling, one year each**. It was Pip's turn, and his brother, the king, wasn't giving up power.
- **The argument:** Pip got angry. His brother said Pip would make a bad ruler because of his anger, which was showing right then. His brother suggested **putting it to a vote of the populace**. Pip refused; he wanted his turn.
- **Their sister** had been waiting for Pip just outside. She came in crying and pleaded with Pip to calm down and not be violent.
- **The break:** Pip drew his warhammer, and the **Giff guards** advanced. Pip **threw his warhammer out the window and jumped after it**. The guards watched him fall from the tower.
- **Afterward,** summarized by Pip: he survived the fall, got away, and spent time gathering allies for a coup that eventually failed, which sent him to prison at Warden's Maw.

**Not played from the plan:** the lantern smash, the injured Giff guard, and the war-map framing. Nobody was hurt in the scene. **His sister was named Phillis at the table.**

### Marfa's offers, and the party splits up

Back in the Pie Parlor, Marfa told Korran and Pip she could offer them what they want:
- **Korran:** a **crew of 6** for his upcoming journey in the Shrike.
- **Pip:** a real conversation about the Ruths helping with **another coup attempt** to take back the throne from his brother.

Marfa also told them about **The Unkindness**, and that **the Joisey Jiffs hadn't hired them** (from Nicole's recap at the start of Session 3; Nolan confirmed).

Then the party split:
- **Korran** went to check on his ship at the dock, and **Megan** followed him.
- **Marfa** asked to speak with **Lucy alone** in the back room.
- **Pip** waited at the parlor's front door for Lucy.

### Korran and Megan

- Walking to the dock, **Megan asked Korran for advice on getting home.** Korran told him to give up on it. Megan disagreed: he would not.
  - Megan then asked where he could find a **religious establishment to cast Speak with Dead** on his daughter's head, to learn how she died. **Korran rolled well on History** and directed him to **the nearest temple** (nondescript).
- **Megan left** for the temple, taking **Krysta's head** with him; he'd had it the whole time. **This departs from the plan**, which had Megan carry the head into the Iron Annex.
- Korran checked on his ship. Everything was fine. He headed back toward the parlor.

### Lucy and Marfa, and Lucy's flashback

- In the back room, **Marfa showed Lucy Babe's empty folder**, the one the Stromboli recipe card was stolen from (Nolan, confirmed after Session 3).
- Lucy talked with her mother, and had a **flashback to her father when she was a child.** Nolan: "very heartwarming."
  - Babe told her she was **too young to learn the family's secrets**. He was protecting her from all of that.
  - He **tapped her on the head with the folder holding the Stromboli recipe**. She was too small to see the recipe itself.
  - **That moment was when Lucy entered her warlock pact with her father**, which is how her powers began. (Babe as her patron was already canon. This is new: how and when the pact started.)
  - The plan's **"for the pepper man" clue was not planted.**
- Back in the present, the talk went warmly. Marfa said she's **so glad to have Lucy back**, especially since it's hard to find people to trust in their business. **She hugged Lucy.**

### To the Iron Annex

- Lucy grabbed **Jetson** to "learn the business," and they set out for the **Iron Annex** to find out who hired the Kenku and why. **Pip** followed.
- They met up with **Korran** on the way.
- **The group going to the Annex:** Pip, Lucy, Jetson and Korran. **Megan is not with them.**

### The Iron Annex

- **The entrance:** they wanted to walk in nonchalantly and get whatever they could out of Tilptek. **Everyone rolled extremely well on Charisma for "swagger."** Korran flipped a dagger and caught it as they walked in.
- **A Kenku was already at the front desk**, which they weren't expecting. **He wears a blindfold**, which Nolan described. **His name hasn't been said yet.** (In the plan he's *Scritch* of The Unkindness, collecting the reissued contract.)
- As they approached the desk, **Lucy made it canon that she has dated Tilptek in the past.**
- **Megan walked in behind them**, carrying Krysta's head. He told the group he **needs money to pay for Speak with Dead** at the nearby temple.
- **Tilptek delivered the "Efficiency" line**, because Megan had just walked in with the head: *"Your target has just entered the lobby." "Efficiency."* This is the reveal that the head was the target.
- **The Kenku turned and cast Darkness** on the group. **Lucy is the only one who can see** in it.
- **The party all failed their Perception checks**, so **nobody noticed the Darkness came from a ring.** They don't know about his rings.
- **Tilptek kept typing.**

**The session ended there**, on the Darkness, before initiative.

### Where Session 3 picks up

In the Iron Annex lobby, inside the Kenku's Darkness, with Megan and the head present. Planned but **not yet played**:
- **Korran's flashback** ("the day the pirates arrived"). Its trigger is the Darkness falling, so it can open Session 3.
- The fight, Tilptek trying to get the lights back on, the client's agent fleeing out the back, and Director Tallyworth's line over the speaker.
- Megan's goodbye.
- The DAV registration of the Shrike, and the spice house.

See [`session2-plan.md`](session2-plan.md#4-the-iron-annex) for the scripts.

## Session 3

Played October 26, 2026. Plan: [`session3-plan.md`](session3-plan.md). The session covered the plan's scenes 1 to 4: Korran's flashback, the lobby fight, and the chase, which ended at a window in the director's office. Unlike earlier sessions, this one was recorded. The raw transcript and a fuller blow-by-blow are in [`transcripts/season2/`](../transcripts/season2/session3-summary.md).

**At the table:** Nicole, Brad and Drew. Drew had lost his voice, so Pip's lines were typed and read out.

### Opening: Korran's flashback

Nolan read the plan's [raid script](session3-plan.md#the-raid-improvised) with changes, and the table filled in the rest:
- **Age:** Korran was about **10 in human years.**
- **The ships:** the *Star-Reaver*, a ribcage "the size of a large, large hill," came with **several other pirate ships**, each with its own unique features. The pirates were **Hadozee and other races** Korran had never seen.
- **Where Korran was:** on a smaller, lower floating island. He heard his mother calling and **glided island to island** toward home.
- **Gold:** in the Cloudjumper Tribe's home, gold is plentiful enough to be **a building material**. His mother taught him it's part of their tradition, **how their people honor the land.**
- **The growers:** the gold-dusted peppers from the Session 2 cold open come from this tradition. It belongs to **the peoples of the Ral'taka Expanse**, who aren't necessarily all Hadozee (Nolan, after the session).
- **Korran hid and cried quietly** while the pirates bound villagers and lingered over the gold.
- **Gloamcast's lines were read as written.** He glided down, looked at Korran's mother for a long moment, and said *"The gold. And any who'll fetch a price. Not her."*
- **Korran's attempt:** on a Wisdom save of 17, Korran ran out to protect her, and bit and fought a Hadozee beside the captain.
- **The retcon:** Nolan first narrated a deal, with Gloamcast taking Korran in exchange for leaving the village alone. At Brad's suggestion he retconned it. **There was no deal. The pirates simply took him** aboard the *Star-Reaver*.
- **The ending:** *"Did you ever see your mother again?"* **He never did** (Nolan confirmed after the session). She could have been taken aboard any of the ships. Korran doesn't know what became of her or his people.

### The fight in the lobby

- **Lucy** went first and cast **Psychic Lance**: 24 psychic damage, and the Kenku was incapacitated. **His concentration broke, and the Darkness ended.**
- **Megan** charged with *"Not my allies!"* and bloodied him.
- **Tino at the back door** played as planned. Only Lucy saw him, and she whispered **"Saucepan."**
- **Korran and Pip** struck non-lethally, so they could find out who he worked for.
- **Megan wanted him alive**, because he had questions. **Lucy cast Suggestion**, and it worked.
- **The rings:** Korran rolled **Sleight of Hand 29** and slid **both rings** off the Kenku's fingers. **He doesn't know what they are yet.**

### The questioning

- **The Kenku**, under Suggestion:
  - Megan *"was not the goal."* The target was **the head**, and the job was to **"eradicate"** it.
  - He was hired **"just now"** by **"an anonymous source,"** doesn't know "the Saucepan," and pointed them to Tilptek.
  - **Afterward:** the party left him slumped in the lobby. **He hobbled out and got away** (Nolan, not said at the table).
- **Tilptek**, intimidated by Lucy with Korran flourishing blades on the counter:
  - Lucy knocked over his computer, and he went for a blue **"MANAGER"** button. **Korran's Mobile Flourish, a natural 20, cut off one of his arms.** He has three left.
  - He gave up the client: *"That was him. He just left."* The man had been there five minutes before the party arrived, and **he placed the contract that the Kenku then picked up.**
  - Lucy asked him on a date for Friday. He'll be in the hospital.

### The back rooms

- **A map room:** a glowing map of the Rock of Bral, and a monitor showing the lobby.
- **A lounge:** Lucy Eldritch Blasted the security camera and taught Jetson the rules: *"Keep your trap shut, and don't leave evidence."* Jetson didn't recognize Tino: *"I was seven, Aunt Lucy."*
- **The director's office:** a big desk, and nobody at it. **Tino** was squeezing out a half-open, stuck window while a Giff guard named **Gary** covered him. *"Occupy them! This is why we pay you the big bucks!"*

### The fight at the window

- **The spells:** Lucy's Suggestion on Gary failed, which ended her Suggestion on the Kenku. Korran's **Faerie Fire** outlined Tino.
- **The hits:**
  - Pip shot Tino with his **musket**, for 12.
  - Megan **launched his longsword blade** into him. Per Xenk Yendar's stat block, it reattaches to its hilt.
- **Gary's grenade:** a force grenade hit Lucy, Pip and Megan for 10 each. Lucy punched him.
- **The card:** Lucy's grapple failed, but she caught Tino's coat, and **a card fell out.**
- **Tino goes down:** Korran stabbed him down non-lethally. He's **barely conscious**, and the party hauled him back inside.
- **Gary surrendered.** He tossed Pip a protein bar, which Pip ate, and left.

### The card

- **Megan picked it up but can't read Common.**
- It reads ***Varrow & Daughters, Fine Spices***, with a dock address.
- **Korran, Perception 22:** it smells of **gold-dusted pepper**, the smell of his childhood.

**The session ended there.** The table's jokes about Pip eating Tino are not canon.

### New canon from the table

- **Giffs dress like Pooh Bear:** shirts, no pants.
- **Megan can't read Common.** He was raised a warrior despite being royalty, because where he comes from, strength in arms is respected far more than learning.
- **Pip carries a scimitar, a musket and his warhammer.**
- **Lucy's spell names:** "Spicy Meatball" is her Fireball, and "Linguine of Fate" her Psychic Lance.
- **Jetson is a preteen**, shorter than Korran.

### Where Session 4 picked up

In the Iron Annex director's office, with Tino barely conscious in the party's hands and the Varrow card found. Session 4 played Tallyworth, Tino's confession, the temple, Megan's goodbye, and the start of the warehouse. **Not reached:** Marfa settling Pip's price, the DAV, boarding the Shrike, or Korran unfurling the chart.

## Session 4

Played Monday, November 2, 2026. Plan: [`session4-plan.md`](session4-plan.md). The session was recorded. The raw transcript and a fuller blow-by-blow are in [`transcripts/season2/`](../transcripts/season2/session4-summary.md).

**At the table:** Nicole, Brad and Drew. Drew had his voice back.

The session covered the plan's scenes 1, 2, 4 and the start of 5a. It **did not reach** Marfa's deal, the DAV, or boarding the Shrike. It ended inside the Varrow & Daughters warehouse.

### Opening: Tallyworth

- **The voice:** *"This is Director Tallyworth. Results... are pending."* A **security camera** sat beside the speaker. The party now knows his name.
- **Korran** shot that office camera with a crossbow (no roll) and rolled **Perception 21**: no other cameras in the room.
- **The invoice** rose to **26,225 gp**, then **+50,000** (to leave with Tino) for **76,225 gp**.
- **Lucy** named the family and Tino as a Fennetti, and told Tallyworth to contact her lawyer, **[Frankie "the Gasman"](../npcs/frankie-the-gasman.md)**. Her **Persuasion 7** failed. Tallyworth asked for **proof she's a Ruth** and said the Annex does not recognize her. After the session (Nolan): she's been in prison, so **the Annex doesn't currently have her face in the system as a Ruth**.
- **Korran** cast **Disguise Self as Tino**. They carried the real Tino out the front, Weekend-at-Bernie's style. **Korran left with the group out the front door, still disguised as Tino.** Megan helped; Jetson watched, excited.
- **Tino** is Lucy's **cousin on dad's side** (Nicole).
- **The lobby:** empty, **Tilptek's chair empty**. Lucy Eldritch Blasted a lobby camera.
- **The long hook:** Tallyworth, from an intercom: *"Do give my regards to the Prince. We have found him very... easy to locate."* Then: the Annex is happy to discuss the Prince's situation **if he can afford them**. Pip announced himself as the plasmoid prince and pitched a **noble rebellion**. The others cut him off.
- **On the way out:** disguised as Tino, Korran told a remaining camera to bill the damage to him. *"We have taken note."*
- **After the session (Nolan):** the disguise **didn't work**. Through the cameras in the other rooms, Tallyworth saw they were impersonating Tino. The **76,225 gp is billed to the Fennetti family**.

### The Pie Parlor basement

- On the street, nobody cared they were carrying a Giff. An off-duty officer walked past.
- They took Tino to the Pie Parlor back room and basement. **Marfa wasn't there.** Jetson asked **[Sandra](../npcs/sandra.md)** behind the bar; she's not at this location.
- Lucy and Korran chained Tino (19 and 17). Something **clinked** in his pocket.
- **Korran** pickpocketed **3 gp** and **a jar of gold-dusted peppers**. He recognized the smell from the Varrow card and from home, **pocketed the jar**, flicked two coins to the others, and kept one. Lucy later gave those two coins back to Tino.

### Tino talks

He woke up afraid of a **Beholder**, offered money, and wouldn't say where it is.

- **The toes:** Lucy bibbed Pip with a napkin, fork and knife. Joint Intimidation with advantage, then Lucy added a d10, for **26**. Pip started absorbing Tino's toes. Where the d10 came from doesn't matter (Nolan).
- **What he said** (and Nolan confirmed after):
  - Krysta *"was the only one that saw that I did a deal"* while she was still her. **Tino knew she had witnessed him making the deal on a spaceship**, and he told the party that.
  - He paid the **Psurlons** with a **Ruth/Fennetti IOU**. He no longer had a family tie, and was **deceiving them** that he was still good for it. That's how he got **the merchant route within the Ral'taka Expanse**.
  - He meant to use the route and the mark himself. **Varrow found out and paid more than it was worth** to Tino, so he sold both as a bundle.
  - It wasn't the recipe. It was **the grower's mark** Babe drew at the bottom.
  - **Varrow & Daughters** have a warehouse by the docks, at the card's address.
  - He wanted the head for its memories, not because Krysta was Megan's daughter.
- **Insight:** Pip rolled a **natural 1**. They couldn't tell how much was performance.
- **Decision 1: they let him go.** Lucy gave him the two gold back for "the bus home," told him not to forget their generosity, and corrected "Lucy" to **Luckyluciano**. Tino ran out the back door. Pip was disgusted by his own behavior.

### Short rest, the artificer, and Jetson

- Everyone took a **short rest** at the Pie Parlor over a free slice of pie.
- **Korran** found an unnamed **Loxodon artificer**, eight feet tall, in robes.
  - The Loxodon identified an Unkindness **Ring of Disintegrate** for free: **one charge left**. It does not have to be aimed at yourself. He can't recharge it.
  - **The Session 1 Kenku's ring** (empty, zero charges) was **sold for 1 gp**.
  - The other Annex ring is **Darkness, one charge**. Korran later gave it to **Pip** at the warehouse door.
- **Jetson:** Lucy invited him to the warehouse. **Bugsy didn't let him go** after he bragged. Nolan added: Jetson stole the family's *DiceGunner* to break the party out of Warden's Maw, there and back with a broken hyperspace drive, then lost the ship. **Repercussions not yet covered.** Brad's secret goal is to turn him into a pirate someday.

### The temple of Mastercat

Bral isn't a religious city, but there are a few temples. The table supplied **Mastercat**, a real 5e god (Nolan). A five-foot copper cat head hangs behind the altar.

- **Lucy paid 2 gp** for Speak with Dead. Megan asked all five questions. **Korran hid behind a pillar.**
  1. **"How did Krysta die?"** Krysta: *"I trusted it."* Then a colder voice: *"She tasted of lions."*
  2. **"How are the Brown Lions doing on the war front?"** Krysta: they were **at the gates when she left**.
  3. **"What is the route into the Expanse?"** **Only the Psurlon's voice:** *"Through the gold. Follow the warm current, until the islands."* After the session (Nolan): **the Psurlon that ate Krysta knew the route.**
  4. **"Who is Varrow?"** *"I don't know. The one with the daughters. They smell of..."*
  5. **"Did you love me?"** Krysta: *"I loved you with all my heart, Father."* Megan fell to his knees.
- Lucy promised regular donations in the Ruths' name. **Drop that as a hook** (Nolan).

### Megan's goodbye

He kept the head, wrapped in **his own jacket**. *"It is not my daughter, but it was the last thing that saw her."* He'll **earn his keep on a ship** and find someone who knows where home is. From what has been described of the Expanse, he doesn't believe Thorndumm is there.

- To Korran: *"My debt to you stands, Hadozee. If you see me again, I still owe you."*
- To Lucy: the pizza was very good. He will remember.
- To Pip: **no debt**. Pip asked him to show up for the rebellion; Megan refused.
- **What the players don't know:** he comes back later, in the Ral'taka Expanse.

### The warehouse

- Closed up, padlocks, no cameras. Guards patrol several warehouses in the area, not this one specifically.
- Korran picked the **back door** on a **17** (first pick broke, second worked).
- Inside: almost pitch black, not dusty, someone has been here recently. Korran: Perception 10, good Stealth.
- As he beckoned the others in, a **giant machine** groaned into motion **15 feet around a corner**, metal grinding, reaching up toward the ceiling. Korran has no darkvision and couldn't see what it was. After the session (Nolan): **undecided** what it is. Might be the giant space hamster, a pepper-processing machine, a fight, or only a scare.

**The session ended there.** Next session is **November 9**. Someone is out on **November 16**.

- How **plasmoids reproduce** is left a mystery.
- **Tino knew** Krysta witnessed the deal.
- The **Fennetti family** owes the Annex **76,225 gp**.
- **Frankie "the Gasman"** is the family's lawyer.

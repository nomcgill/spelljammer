- Korran is told that his newly stolen ship, and likely **"he-who-took-the-helm,"** will be **tracked until it's re-registered**. Plasmoid ships are intricate like that.
- Marfa waves away suspicion by stating her name and throwing her influence at the dock officers.
- The **DAV (Department of Astral Vehicles)** won't open for another two hours, and re-registering costs money.

**The invitation:**
- Marfa is skeptical of Lucy's new friends, but Jetson sticks up for them.
- So Marfa invites the group to a celebratory pizza dinner. The venue is to be decided; see [where's the pizza?](#wheres-the-pizza).
- She says she'd *"like to discuss business with all of you."* She doesn't actually know what the business is yet. She has a way of striking deals and making people valuable just by getting to know them, so the "business" turns out to be each PC's current motive.
- Megan is included. He carries his daughter's head as if it holds no emotional significance to him, and he's *"always willing to try new dishes."*

**Over pizza:** Marfa gets to know the group, finds out what each of them is after, and works out a trade. To get what they each want, **they must solve the Stromboli case**.
- **Lucy:** if she doesn't go, her father will hear about how she "avoided pizza with family." *(Nolan's note continued "And she's told that to solve…" and was cut off.)*
- **Korran:** Marfa will cover the registration-reset costs, so the ship becomes fully Korran's.
- **Pip:** Marfa promises to "discuss" a joint venture supporting his rebellion.

**Tamenik** skips the pizza invitation and rushes home to Freelin Corporation. Kirrak is still away aboard the plasmoid mothership, so Tamenik walks into the family company while its CEO is away.

## Korran's stolen ship

Canon so far: it's a smaller ship from the mothership's loading bay. Korran took its spelljamming helm alone and flew it to Bral. Per your outline, plasmoid ships track the ship, and probably the person who took the helm, until it's re-registered.

Options:

1. **A Shrike ship** (published, *Astral Adventurer's Guide*). It's a small, fast attack ship, which fits something a warship keeps in its bay as a scout or interceptor. It's quick and nimble, but it's a fighter rather than a home.
2. **A Lamprey ship** (published). This is a boarding vessel built to latch onto other ships. That fits the mothership's "send in the other ones" boarding in Season 1, so it could even be the craft the boarders came over in. It's cramped and ugly, which is a fun ship for a party to own.
3. **A homebrew plasmoid courier.** It has a gel-like living hull membrane, and the helm bonds to whoever sits in it. That gives the tracking a built-in explanation: the membrane pings the fleet, and the helm has "tasted" Korran. Re-registration at the DAV means flushing the membrane and re-keying the helm. As far as I know, published 5e Spelljammer doesn't give plasmoids a signature ship, so this would be yours to define.

**My suggestion:** use a published hull for the stats, probably the Shrike or the Lamprey, and add the plasmoid tracking membrane as flavour. Check the crew capacity in the *Astral Adventurer's Guide* before you pick. If it becomes the party's ship by the end of Session 2, it needs to fit Korran, Lucy, Pip and Megan, and Jetson if he comes too.

The "he-who-took-the-helm" detail gives Korran a personal stake beyond the ship: **he's tagged**, the way Pip is scryable. Re-registration frees him as well as the ship, and the dinner gives Marfa something real to offer.

## ID checks at the dock

- **The captain shows ID, everyone else gives a name** (your idea). The names go into a dock **manifest**. Korran is technically the captain of an unregistered plasmoid ship with no papers, which is what sends him to the DAV.
- **The names are a moment for the players.** They're escaped prisoners. Do they give real names or aliases?
  - **Pip** is Prince Philip Oozewald, a plasmoid prince arriving in a plasmoid ship. Expect a double take.
  - **Korran** says either "Cloudjumper" or "Driftblade," and that's Brad's choice to make.
  - **Megan** announces himself, loudly and in full, as King of the Red Lions of Thorndumm.
  - **Lucy** was arrested on Bral, so an officer could recognise her. That's the moment Marfa steps in.
- **A magical ID check tests the Remove Curse.** If the dock uses a scrying-based check, such as a lens or a glyph-reader, Tamenik's reading is the first hint of "at least, he thinks he is." It could glitch, come back blank, or read fine and leave everyone relieved.
- **The manifest is a record someone can buy.** If the Iron Annex reads dock manifests, that's how Tilptek and "the boss" learn the party is back on Bral. It's a quiet way to bring the Annex in.
- **Marfa's influence is shown, not told.** Her name makes the officers nervous. That shows the players how much weight the Ruths carry on Bral, which matters once she starts offering deals.

## Where's the pizza?

1. **Lucy's old flagship Pie Parlor**, orbiting Bral. This is canon: she ran one of the flagship parlors here until her arrest. It could be shuttered since the Crustfall sting, or reopened under new management (Bugsy? a Fennetti?). It's emotional for Lucy, and it **could be where the recipe was stolen from**, which puts the crime scene at the dinner table.
2. **The Ruth family table**, a private back room with **Babe's empty chair** at its head. Babe stays offscreen, but you can feel him there. The recipe vault is in the back.
3. **Astro Nommie's Slicehouse**, on purpose. Marfa takes them to the Joisey Jiffs' place to "know your enemy," or because it's neutral ground under an old truce. That puts the suspects in the room on night one.

**My pick:** the first one, Lucy's old parlor, reopened under someone else and robbed. It gives Lucy a personal reason to care about the case before Marfa even makes her offer.

## How the dinner leads to the end of Session 2

The dinner should set up a chain that ends with everyone choosing the same ship and the same destination:

1. **The dinner (Session 1).** Marfa learns, or reveals, that the recipe is gone. **Babe can't know**, so she needs people who aren't on the family payroll. Fresh-out-of-prison unknowns are perfect. Each trade is conditional on getting the recipe back.
2. **The investigation (Sessions 1 to 2).** Places to look:
   - the scene of the theft;
   - the Jiffs at Astro Nommie's;
   - the **Iron Annex**, if the thief was hired through it or Tilptek sells what he knows;
   - the **DAV**, where the re-registration can run in parallel as a B-plot;
   - Megan and **Krysta's head**: the Pie Parlors IOU on her body could connect the Psurlons to whoever bought the recipe.
3. **The solution (end of Session 2).** Getting the recipe back, or learning the truth about it, **points off Bral to one destination**, and that destination pays off everyone at once.

Two options for that destination:

- **The Ral'taka Expanse.** The recipe's secret ingredient only comes from there, perhaps literally Ral'taka gold. The Jiffs stole the recipe for the **plasmoid court**, which is moving into the Expanse to fund the usurper's war with its gold. This pays off every PC:
  - **Korran:** his homeland is under threat, and he has the map.
  - **Pip:** he can hit his brother's funding, with Ruth backing.
  - **Lucy:** she can secure the supply line, beat the Jiffs and prove herself to Babe.
  - **Megan:** it's the plasmoids again, the people who took Thorndumm.
- **The *Star-Reaver* first.** Navigating the Expanse needs the **Star Chart of the Lost Spheres**, which is still on the derelict. That makes the first leg the derelict, matching the [season shape](../plot/season2-planning.md#season-shape-rough).

**The ship** is Korran's newly re-registered one. Marfa paid for it, so the Ruths have a stake in where it goes.

**Nicole's twist,** *"what if the recipe was never meant to be stolen?"*, could mean that Babe staged the theft as a test of who should inherit the family, Lucy or Bugsy. That fits Lucy's arc, but it's Nicole's pitch, so check with her.

## Earlier ideas (before Nolan's outline)

Kept for reference and still usable.

### Cold open ideas

Each one points at Korran's past without giving it away.

- **The *Star-Reaver* has visitors.** A ship pulls up beside the derelict in its toxic air envelope. Someone boards, walks to the spelljamming helm, and finds Captain Gloamcast's corpse... or finds the helm empty. They take the Star Chart. The players know that ship.
- **The Ral'taka Expanse.** A Hadozee on a floating island watches a squid-shaped silhouette on the horizon, the way their elders described the raid. Someone is following the old route.
- **A survivor.** Somewhere in the Astral Sea, someone with an old *Star-Reaver* tattoo hears that "Driftblade" got out of Warden's Maw. *"So he's alive."* This doubles as the prison-revenge hook if they were also in the Maw.
- **The plasmoid court.** Alkgleb reports his failure to the king, with Gelp standing beside the throne. The king isn't interested in chasing Pip. He wants something else, and he's sending people to the Rock of Bral. This keeps "no one urgently after them" true while still making the court matter.
- **The neogi nightspider** from the hook bank. It boards some other ship, and the survivor drifts toward Bral carrying a story.

### Why the party is at the Iron Annex

- **Pip is hiring.** A revolution needs mercenaries, and this is where you hire them.
- **They need work.** No Damselfly, and no money except what's in Pip's stomach.
- **They need to re-register Korran's stolen ship** (Nolan's idea) so the plasmoids can't follow it so easily. This is now in the outline, via the DAV.
- **Lucy is chasing the Stromboli thief** and wants a tracker.
- **Tilptek pages them.** The Annex knows exactly who they are, since it organised the hunt for Tamenik. Now it has a job for them.

**Things to play with when they get there:**
- **Tilptek knows them.** He brokered the contract to find Tamenik by scrying Pip. Him calmly offering them work anyway is comedy and menace at once.
- **"The boss"** gives instructions offscreen. This is a chance to hint at who they are.
- **The motto:** *"Discretion. Efficiency. Results. Results."*
- **Bral is a hub world.** Anyone of any race can be in the lobby.

### Who walks in, and what they say

- **A bounty on "Driftblade."** Someone posts a contract for the man who killed the crew of the *Star-Reaver*. Only Korran knows that's him.
- **A Hadozee from the Ral'taka Expanse**, asking for help because raiders are back on the old route, the same route as Korran's map.
- **A *Star-Reaver* sighting.** Someone wants to hire a crew to salvage a dead-god-ribcage squid ship.
- **Kirrak comes home** and demands a refund for losing his uncle. Now that Tamenik goes home to Freelin Corporation, Kirrak could find him there.
- **A Ruth family problem.** A Pie Parlor courier posts an urgent, very discreet retrieval job.
- **Someone from Pip's court**, maybe his unnamed cousin, quietly asking after the exiled prince, possibly as a defector.

### Tying the Stromboli case to the others

Anything that changes Babe or the recipe should go past Nicole first.

- **Pip:** solving it earns Babe's favour, and the Ruth Syndicate becomes the revolution's first real ally. **Nolan picked this as the motivator.** The alternative still on the table is that the Jiffs stole it for the plasmoid court.
- **Korran:** the secret ingredient only comes from the Ral'taka Expanse, or Babe once fenced *Star-Reaver* plunder and the "recipe" is really a coded ledger.
- **Megan:** the Pie Parlors IOU on Krysta's body means the Psurlons did business with the Ruths. Speak with Dead on the head could show what the IOU was for. He's also a natural recruit for Pip, since Gelp petrified him.

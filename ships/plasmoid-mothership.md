# The Plasmoid Mothership

**Appears in:** Season 1

The warship that opened the campaign and closed Season 1. Commanded by [Alkgleb](../npcs/alkgleb.md).

## Status

Last seen beside the abandoned Damselfly, short of the Rock of Bral. Where it went next is `[unconfirmed]`.

## Capabilities demonstrated

- **Bombardment.** Levelled sections of a Githyanki prison. A plasmoid battleship descended on the harbor with a bright blue laser charging from beneath, aimed at the dock.
- **A psychic beam** strong enough to rock the Damselfly from a distance.
- **A wave of Dispel Magic** that functioned as an EMP: it stripped the Damselfly to auxiliary power, took [A.R.I.A.](../npcs/aria.md) offline, and left the comms and monitor barely functional.
- **A teleportation circle** aboard, for extraction — **blocked** by the curse on Tamenik. "I'll need to see your ship to get you. Can you survive?"
- **Summoned boarders**, sent in on Alkgleb's Primordial order, "Send in the other ones." Including [the silver plasmoid](../npcs/silver-plasmoid.md).

## Who's aboard

- Alkgleb, a general serving [Pip](../player-characters/pip-oozewald.md)'s brother the plasmoid king.
- [Kirrak](../npcs/kirrak-freelin.md), a paying passenger, there for his uncle.
- A fleet or crew referred to as "your men or that Plasmoid fleet."

## Known weak points

- A **crack in the hull** large enough for Korran to get through.
- A **loading bay** holding at least one smaller spelljamming ship, now stolen.

## Detection

The mothership found the party by **scrying Pip**, because [Tamenik](../player-characters/tamenik-freelin.md) cannot be scried. Wherever Pip goes, he can be found.

## Season 1

### The same ship, twice

This is **the same bomber that attacked [Warden's Maw](../locations/wardens-maw.md)** in Session 1 to extract [Gu](../npcs/gu.md). The party's escape was an accidental by-product of its raid. Six sessions later it caught up with them deliberately.

A.R.I.A.'s warning in Session 5 called it "a familiar ship" casting a psychic beam — waves of energy that rocked the Damselfly.

### Marfa's threat

[Marfa Ruth](../npcs/marfa-ruth.md) radioed a threat to end all plasmoid business with Babe Ruth's Pie Parlors. Per Nolan's prep, the mothership was to **eventually ignore** it.

### How Season 1 ended

It disabled and boarded the Damselfly, but the party got away. They killed its silent silver assassin, left the Damselfly behind, and [Marfa](../npcs/marfa-ruth.md) beamed them out in the [*Slice Runner*](ruth-family-ships.md#the-slice-runner). Meanwhile [Korran](../player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md) got inside through a **crack in its hull** and **hijacked a smaller ship from its loading bay** (see [Korran's stolen ship](korrans-stolen-ship.md)).

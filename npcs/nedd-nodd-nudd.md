# Nedd, Nodd, and Nudd

**Appears in:** Season 2 (draft, Session 5)

**Dohwar** steward triplets on [Korran's stolen Shrike](../ships/korrans-stolen-ship.md). Penguin-merchants. Not in 5e *Spelljammer: Adventures in Space* — 2nd-edition Spelljammer. Nolan is keeping them as table color.

Identical tuxedos and oilskins. They usually work in pairs, so a triad is already a joke. Names are a set on purpose, and they are **not** close to [Pip](../player-characters/pip-oozewald.md). **They do not speak.** They nod, they shake their heads, and their black beady eyes look glazed over almost all the time.

## Status

Waiting aboard the Shrike. [Marfa](marfa-ruth.md) promised [Korran](../player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md) a **crew of 6**. After [Dottie](dottie.md), [Wrench](rocco-wrench.md), and [Lumen](lumen.md), she still needed **two jobs**: cook, and someone for the hold. The triplets would not split. They sold themselves as **three for the price of two** so they could all get on the ship. Marfa paid five wages and delivered six people. [Brownish Greenspot](../player-characters/brownish-greenspot.md) is a seventh, not one of the six: Marfa's navigator, her condition of the voyage.

**Job:** stewards. Cargo, galley, stores. They cook, they stow, they nod at a price. Lumen counts; they haul. A Shrike is small, but somebody still has to feed the crew and sit the hold. Dottie does not cook. Wrench does not cook. Lumen does not cook.

They needed the deal: pie-credit and a cut of "whatever we happen to acquire." That is **spoils, not payroll.** They are not on a steward's wage. They work as a set or not at all.

**Korran's lever:** that cut. They nod at a price. Insight **5** was whether he could read them, not whether the deal is spoils — it is.

## How to play them

**One NPC in three bodies. No dialogue.** They nod, they shake their heads, they stare. Black beady eyes, glazed over almost all the time. Don't try to tell them apart at the table. Don't voice them. If a yes/no will not do, Dottie or Lumen can say what Marfa already agreed. They still cook, haul, and inventory; they just do not talk.

Marfa paid in pie-credit and a cut of "whatever we happen to acquire." They nodded through that deal.

One silent beat at the hatch, then they're in the galley.

## Look (draft)

Three identical penguin-merchants in tiny oilskins over tuxedos. Same orange beak. Black beady eyes, glazed over almost all the time. They stand in a row and lean the same way when they listen.

# Aryndac

**Appears in:** Season 1

Githyanki guard at [Warden's Maw](../locations/wardens-maw.md). The party's first real antagonist, and still at large.

## Status

**Alive and unaccounted for.** He teleported off the Damselfly in Session 2 and has not reappeared.

## Season 1

### What he did

At the prison he was one of two Githyanki guards — with Aristeth — described as mean and violent. During the breakout they were seen slaughtering a plasmoid in the hangar, then turning to "secure the Damselflies."

The party killed Aristeth in that fight. [Gu](gu.md) — having **survived** a Githyanki ship's blast on the balcony — then flew across the hangar and tackled Aryndac off the ship harbor into the Astral Sea. Mid-fall, Aryndac **Misty Stepped aboard the Damselfly** the party was stealing (unnoticed).

[A.R.I.A.](aria.md) later detected him and told them to check the hyperspace drive, where they found him with his **longsword driven into the drive itself**. He pulled it free, smirked, and vanished with a teleport.

That single act shaped the next five sessions:

- The hyperspace drive was crippled, making piloting much harder and cutting the ship's top speed.
- [Jetson](jetson.md) took the blade's poison deep in his abdomen — a Githyanki poison attacking his "synaptic transmissions," with 48 hours to live.
- Fixing the drive required diamond for the Hyperdrive Core, which drove the party to the Psurlon shipping hub and everything that followed there.

## Aristeth

The other hangar guard. A **Githyanki Gish** — CR 10, AC 17, around 123–130 hit points, psychic longsword strikes, and Astral Step (*Monsters of the Multiverse* p. 140). See [`bestiary/season1-encounters.md`](../bestiary/season1-encounters.md).

**Dead.** Killed by the players in the Session 1 hangar fight.

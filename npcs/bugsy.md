# Bugsy

**Appears in:** none yet (introduced after Season 1)

[Luckyluciano](../player-characters/luckyluciano-ruth.md)'s **older sister**, and [Jetson](jetson.md)'s mother. Daughter of [Babe Ruth](babe-ruth.md) and [Marfa Ruth](marfa-ruth.md). Full name `[unconfirmed]`.

Source: Nicole's notes on Lucy, passed on by Nolan after Season 1.

## Status

Offscreen. Never appeared at the table.

## Who she is

**Babe Ruth's intended heir.** Babe sees the crime family eventually belonging to her. **Bugsy doesn't want it.** She just wants to be a mom to Jetson.

That gap is the engine of Lucy's personal arc: Lucy is out to prove to her father, who is also her warlock patron, that she is capable of running the family if Bugsy won't.

# Gelp

**Appears in:** Season 1

Plasmoid **High Wizard**. Best wizard of [Pip](../player-characters/pip-oozewald.md)'s **brother**, the plasmoid king.

## Status

Offscreen. Named but never encountered. The king remains **unnamed**.

## Background

Gelp cast the [Flesh to Stone](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2109-flesh-to-stone) that petrified [Megan Forthfang](megan-forthfang.md), King of the Red Lions, during the plasmoid attack on Megan's kingdom of Thorndumm. Megan was then shipped off and stored as cargo in a Psurlon container yard.

## Why he matters

He is the link that makes Megan's presence personal rather than coincidental. The party freed a king who was petrified by the **court wizard of Pip's brother** — and Pip is travelling with Megan.

Whether Megan knows that, and what he does when he finds out, is unresolved.

A High Wizard capable of casting Flesh to Stone on a 17th-level-equivalent paladin is also, straightforwardly, a serious future threat.

## Season 1

Never appeared at the table. His work did: the party freed his petrified victim, Megan, at the Psurlon shipyard in Session 5.

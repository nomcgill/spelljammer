# Nedd, Nodd, and Nudd

**Appears in:** Season 2 (draft, Session 5)

**Dohwar** steward triplets on [Korran's stolen Shrike](../ships/korrans-stolen-ship.md). Penguin-merchants. Not in 5e *Spelljammer: Adventures in Space* — 2nd-edition Spelljammer. Nolan is keeping them as table color.

Identical tuxedos and oilskins. They usually work in pairs, so a triad is already a joke. Names are a set on purpose, and they are **not** close to [Pip](../player-characters/pip-oozewald.md).

## Status

Waiting aboard the Shrike. [Marfa](marfa-ruth.md) promised [Korran](../player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md) a **crew of 6**. After [Dottie](dottie.md), [Wrench](rocco-wrench.md), and [Lumen](lumen.md), she still needed **two jobs**: cook, and someone for the hold. The triplets would not split. They sold themselves as **three for the price of two** so they could all get on the ship. Marfa paid five wages and delivered six people. [Skipper](../season2/session5-plan.md#skipper-matt-session-6) is a seventh, not one of the six.

**Job:** stewards. Cargo, galley, stores. They cook, they stow, they try to sell at the next dock. Lumen counts; they haul and haggle. A Shrike is small, but somebody still has to feed the crew and sit the hold. Dottie does not cook. Wrench does not cook. Lumen does not cook.

They needed the deal: pie-credit, a cut of "whatever we happen to acquire," and a way into Expanse trade. They work as a set or not at all.

## How to play them

**One NPC in three bodies.** Same voice. They finish each other's sentences. They argue about who is eldest (four minutes apart, never settled). Don't try to tell them apart at the table.

They will inventory anything the party carried out of the warehouse and name a price. Marfa paid in pie-credit and a cut of "whatever we happen to acquire."

One shared line at the hatch, then they're in the galley.

## Look (draft)

Three identical penguin-merchants in tiny oilskins over tuxedos. Same orange beak, same judgmental little eyes. They stand in a row and lean the same way when they listen.

# Season 1 — Opening Prompt

The polished read-aloud text Nolan used to open the campaign. This is the tidied version; the rougher original wording is quoted inside [`session-notes.md`](session-notes.md).

---

You still remember the day you arrived at Warden's Maw, the prison tucked into a nook of an oblong asteroid floating through the Astral Sea. The prison once housed criminals of all kinds, but ever since the asteroid collided with another and was knocked out of Wildspace two decades ago, it has drifted beyond the watchful eyes of any authority or government. These days, it's controlled by Githyanki scum who have devised countless schemes to turn a profit.

The warden collects beings of all kinds in the Maw's cells. Oblivious passing ships are ransacked. Some captives are bought cheap from the black market. Most prisoners are forced to mine coal and flecks of diamond. Those deemed valuable are kept—one day, they might be used to blackmail someone, somewhere. But with thousands of prisoners, it's hard to keep track of who they are or why they're here. That's why most understand, regardless of the plane or land they once called home, that this is where they will die.

# The Iron Annex

**Appears in:** Season 1, Season 2

A contracting office on the [Rock of Bral](rock-of-bral.md). **A Division of the Spherewide Contracting Compact.** Where the manhunt for the party was arranged.

Motto, on a holographic sign suspended above the reception desk:

> **THE IRON ANNEX**
> "Discretion. Efficiency. Results. Results."

The repetition of "Results" appears deliberate in Nolan's text.

## Atmosphere

Pneumatic doors that hiss open with a sharp *chhk-thoom*, releasing a burst of sterile recycled air. Polished synth-metal walls humming with ambient arcane current. Modular light panels pulsing gently overhead, casting cold white light across brushed steel floors etched with shifting sigils. A smell somewhere between **ozone and printer ink**.

Behind a curved alloy desk sits [Tilptek](../npcs/tilptek.md), a Thri-kreen, working an arcane-laced glass monitor embedded in the desk. Its glyphs flicker in real time as he navigates a seemingly infinite stream of incoming contracts.

The tone is corporate-brutalist — a bureaucracy of contract killing and retrieval, run like a help desk.

## Who runs it

**Almost the entire Annex is operated by Thri-kreen**, from the front desk down (Nolan's ruling). The one exception is **the head honcho, "the boss," an autognome**. His name is **Director Tallyworth**. The players haven't met him or learned his name yet.

**The Kenku assassins** hired through the Annex each carry **two rings**. Every one of them wears a **Disintegrate** ring, so none of them can be taken alive. The second ring holds a spell **different for each Kenku**, which makes every one of them unique. The guild is called **The Unkindness**.

## The open question

**Who is "the boss"?** The Iron Annex is taking direction from someone unnamed who, within hours of a prison break at an unwatched asteroid, knew that one escapee was unscryable and that another was a usable proxy. That's the most interesting unexplained thing on the Rock of Bral.

**Where Bral hires its Kenku assassins.** Contracts with the Kenku guild, **The Unkindness**, go through the Annex. To lift a contract, you **outpay whoever placed it**, or apply the right **"pressure."** Threats on Ruth lives aren't infrequent, and checking the Annex is the family's usual first step (Nolan).

**What the players don't know:** the Kenku they fought in Session 3 is **Scritch**. He's blind, wears a blindfold, and "sees" by echolocation. His second ring is Darkness. His name hasn't been said at the table.

**The layout behind the desk** (Session 3):
- **A map room** with a glowing map of the Rock of Bral, and a monitor showing the lobby.
- **A lounge** with couches and a security camera covering the lobby. Lucy destroyed the camera.
- **The director's office:** a big desk, and a window whose crank sticks half open.

## Season 1

### What happened here

Shown as a **flashback** during Session 6, set a few days before the present.

[Kirrak](../npcs/kirrak-freelin.md), CEO of Freelin Corporation, arrived with a hulking Tortle bodyguard carrying a hand-laser crossbow. It had been a while since his last visit, but Tilptek knew why he was there — Tilptek was the one who had paged him, within a few hours of the Warden's Maw breakout, to say his relative was out.

Tilptek already had **instructions from "the boss"** on how Kirrak could retrieve Tamenik despite Tamenik's immunity to scrying. He also knew the plasmoids who had bombarded Warden's Maw were hunting Pip.

The plan that came out of it: scry Pip, follow Pip to Tamenik, and buy Kirrak a seat aboard the plasmoid warship.

## Season 2

### Session 2

The party's first visit in person: Lucy, Korran, Pip and Jetson walked in with swagger, looking to work [Tilptek](../npcs/tilptek.md) for information. A Kenku was already at the desk. Megan followed them in carrying Krysta's head, and Tilptek told the Kenku its target had arrived. The Kenku cast **Darkness** over the lobby, and the session ended there. Full log in the [session notes](../season2/session-notes.md#the-iron-annex).

### Session 3

- **The lobby fight:**
  - Lucy's Psychic Lance broke the Darkness, and the party beat the Kenku down non-lethally.
  - Korran took **both of its rings**.
  - Under Suggestion, the Kenku said its target was **the head**, to be "eradicated," and that it was hired "just now" by "an anonymous source." Left in the lobby, it **hobbled out and got away**.
- **Tilptek:**
  - He lost **an arm** to Korran when he reached for the **"MANAGER"** button.
  - He confirmed the client had placed the contract five minutes before the party arrived.
- **The back rooms:** the party pushed through to the director's office. **Tino Fennetti** was trying to escape out the window, with his Giff guard [Gary](../npcs/gary.md) covering him. They caught Tino and found a ***Varrow & Daughters, Fine Spices*** card on him.
- **Director Tallyworth** hasn't been seen or heard yet. Log in the [session notes](../season2/session-notes.md#session-3).

# Season 2, Session 2: Plan

The run sheet for Session 2, in table order. The ideas behind it, and the alternatives that weren't used, are in [`session2-brainstorm.md`](session2-brainstorm.md). What happened in Session 1 is in [`session-notes.md`](session-notes.md).

**Spoilers throughout. Nothing here is canon until it's run.** Read-aloud and scripted text is my draft for Nolan to edit.

## What the players don't know

**Nobody knows that Krysta's head was the target.** The dart hit the head because Megan blocked with it, so as far as anyone at the table can tell, the Kenku was after **Marfa**, and that's how Marfa reads it too. Play scenes 2 and 3 that way. **The truth comes out at the Iron Annex**, when the new Kenku sees the head and **attacks on sight**.

## Before you run this

Settle these first. Each one is marked **(confirm)** where it appears below.

- **The client.** The plan uses the placeholder *Varrow & Daughters, Fine Spices*, a spice import house in the docks. This session only needs the name on a trade card. Who's really behind it, and whether it's from Korran's past, can wait. **Check with Brad if it is.**
- **The ring.** If nobody picked up the Disintegrate ring in Session 1, Marfa has it.
- **Lucy and the Darkness.** Her sheet lists **120 ft darkvision**, and Giff don't have darkvision naturally. That's the range the warlock's **Devil's Sight** invocation gives, and Devil's Sight lets her see normally in magical darkness. If she has it, **Lucy can see in the Kenku's Darkness**, which is a great moment for her. Check her sheet.
- **Megan carries Krysta's head into the Annex** (Nolan), and that's what the Kenku recognizes. He parts ways *after* the Annex instead (end of scene 4). Whether he takes the head with him or leaves it with the party is still **(confirm)**. The plan currently has him leave it.
**The boss of the Iron Annex** is an **autognome** named **Director Tallyworth**. He's offscreen this session, apart from his line over the speaker at the end of the Annex fight.

## 1. Cold open: the Ral'taka Expanse

The players watch without their characters.

*Read-aloud:*

> Far from the Rock of Bral, the floating islands of the Ral'taka Expanse drift through warm gold light. Vines hang off their undersides like beards, and a thousand feet below them there's nothing but sky. On one of the smaller islands, a Hadozee grower moves along rows of squat, glossy plants, picking peppers that glint like they've been dusted with gold. Because they have.
>
> Something changes in the light. A ship is coming in, low and slow, along the old trade route that only one buyer ever uses. The grower squints. Wrong ship. Wrong flag.
>
> It drifts alongside. A figure at the rail holds up a strip of card, torn along one edge, and on it, in an old Giff's handwriting, is a mark the grower has known for years.
>
> The grower lowers their spear and smiles. *"Welcome back, friend."*

Cut to Bral.

## 2. The Pie Parlor, right after the fight

Pick up where Session 1 ended: dust where the Kenku was, a ruby ring on the floor, and a dart sticking out of Krysta's head.

### Marfa is flustered, but practised

*Marfa, smoothing her apron with both hands:*
> "Okay. Okay! Nobody touch the dust. Bugsy, honey, get the mop. No, the *other* mop." *(a breath)* "This happens. Not often, but it happens. When somebody wants a Ruth dead, there's only one place in this city they go to hire it done. The Iron Annex. So we go see who paid, and then we either pay more... or we lean on somebody until they wish we'd paid more."

### Megan

Megan pulls the dart out of the head and looks at it without much interest. *"It is not my daughter. It ate my daughter."* Like everyone else, he assumes the dart was meant for Marfa and the head just got in the way. He owes Korran, and he won't let the head out of his sight, so **he comes along to the Annex, carrying it.** His goodbye is at the end of scene 4.

### Marfa's deals

Now Marfa makes the offers the dart interrupted. She knows everything they told her, so she knows exactly what to offer each of them:
- **Lucy:** help solve the Case of the Missing Stromboli, and Marfa makes sure Babe knows Lucy is the one who did it. She also stays quiet about Lucy trading the family favour IOU to the Psurlons.
- **Korran:** the Shrike is already his once it's registered, since the costs were waived at the dock. Now that she knows about the map, Marfa offers Ruth supplies and crew for whatever trip comes next, for a cut.
- **Pip:** a real conversation about the Ruth Syndicate backing his revolution, if this goes well.

### Flashback: Pip

**Trigger:** Pip wipes the Kenku's blood off his warhammer.

**The background (Drew):** when their father died, he didn't leave the throne to his firstborn. Instead his three children were to **take turns**: **Pip Jr.**, the eldest, then **Pip**, the middle child, then **Phillis**, the youngest. Pip Jr. went first, and he won't let go. This scene is **the night Pip's turn should begin.**

**Why Pip Jr. won't hand it over** *(proposed; check with Drew)*:
- **His excuse is the war.** During his turn, Pip Jr. launched the conquests, including Thorndumm, where his wizard Gelp petrified Megan. His line is that *you don't change captains in the middle of a storm*: he'll hand over the throne when the war is won, and somehow it never is.
- **His justification is Pip.** He honestly believes Pip's temper would ruin the kingdom, and tonight Pip proves it in front of him.
- **That's why Pip calls him the usurper.** The crown stopped being Pip Jr.'s the moment his turn ran out.

*Read-aloud, to Drew:*

> The cloth comes away red. It's not your blood. You don't have any. You keep wiping, and the red keeps coming, and the parlor goes quiet around you...
>
> ...and you're standing in your brother's private chambers in the palace. Tomorrow was supposed to be your turn. The walls breathe with soft, slow light, a living gel that glows when the king is near. Pip Jr. stands at the window with his back to you, watching the city. On the table between you is a war map, covered in little gel markers, and most of them are pointed outward. He hasn't turned around since you came in.
>
> A Giff mercenary stands guard by the door. He's one of the hired ones, polished and silent, pretending he can't hear a word.
>
> *"You're asking me to step down,"* your brother says. *"Now. In the middle of a war. You, of all of us."*

Let Drew talk. This is Pip trying to talk his brother down first: honor their father's wish, end the war, hand over the crown. When it turns, and it should, here's the break:

> *"Go home, Philip."*
>
> Something in you goes hot and red. You don't decide to swing. The hammer is just *moving*, and it comes down on the glowing gel-glass lantern on the table beside you. It bursts. Shards fly across the room, and one of them catches the Giff at the door across the face. He drops to a knee with a grunt. When you look down, there's blood on your warhammer. Someone else's blood, for the first time.
>
> Your brother finally turns around. He looks at the guard, then at you. *"There it is,"* he says quietly. *"That's why it can't be you."*

Cut back: the cloth in Pip's hand, the red on the hammer.

**Prompts for Drew:**
- What did you say to try to talk him down?
- What did your brother say that you've never forgotten?
- Where was Phillis tonight, and whose side were they on?
- Is this the moment you decided to move against him?
- What happened to the Giff guard?

## 4. The Iron Annex

### Arrival

*Read-aloud:*

> The pneumatic doors hiss open with a sharp *chhk-thoom*, and a breath of cold recycled air rolls out over you. Polished synth-metal walls hum with arcane current. Light panels pulse overhead, and sigils shift slowly in the brushed-steel floor. It smells like ozone and printer ink.
>
> Thri-kreen everywhere. Rows of them at curved desks, four arms each, typing, filing, stamping, sliding contracts into brass tubes that whoosh away into the ceiling. Not one of them looks up.
>
> Above the front desk, the holographic sign turns slowly: **THE IRON ANNEX. "Discretion. Efficiency. Results. Results."**
>
> And behind the desk sits a Thri-kreen you may remember hearing about: matte black shell with a bronze sheen, a mug reading **"#1 Subordinate Drone,"** and a fidget spinner humming in one of his four hands. Tilptek.

**Almost everyone working here is Thri-kreen.** The only exception is the boss, Director Tallyworth, who isn't in the room.

### The Kenku at the desk

There's already a customer at the desk: a **Kenku** in a dark coat, **collecting the reissued contract**. The last Kenku failed, so the job went back out.

- **Name:** *Scritch*, after the sound of a pen. Kenku are named for sounds.
- **The guild:** **The Unkindness**.
- **He's blind.** A strip of black cloth is tied over his eyes, so it's obvious. Describe it when they first see him.
- **He echolocates.** Kenku can make any sound, and Scritch makes a soft, rapid *tk-tk-tk* and hears the echo come back. That's his **blindsight**. Let the party hear the clicking before they understand it.
- **He wears two rings.** Anyone who rolls **over 18 on Perception** when they first see him notices both. One is **Darkness**. The other is **Disintegrate**, the same as the Session 1 Kenku's ruby ring, and whoever has that ring will recognize it.

*Read-aloud:*

> At the front desk, a Kenku in a long dark coat waits with his head tilted, the way a bird listens. A strip of black cloth is knotted over his eyes. As you come through the doors, there's a faint sound from him, *tk-tk-tk*, quick and dry, like a fingernail on a tabletop.

Tilptek slides the contract across. Scritch's head turns toward the doors, clicking, and stops on **Megan and Krysta's head**. Tilptek's mandibles twitch twice.

*Tilptek, perfectly pleasant:*
> "Your target has just entered the lobby." *(a beat)* "Efficiency."

**This is the reveal:** the target was the head all along, not Marfa. **Scritch attacks on sight.**

### Darkness

Scritch closes his fist, and the first ring flares. **Every Kenku in The Unkindness carries two rings:** a **Disintegrate** ring, which all of them wear, and a personal ring with a spell of its own. Scritch's personal ring is **Darkness**. The lobby goes black.

**Scritch is blind and echolocates**, so the dark costs him nothing. He fights normally inside it, clicking. Everyone else, apart from Lucy if she has Devil's Sight, is blind inside it.

### Flashback: Korran

**Trigger:** the Darkness falls.

*Read-aloud, to Brad:*

> The light doesn't fade. It's *swallowed*, all at once, the way you've only seen it happen once before...
>
> ...and you're a boy again, lying on your back on a high branch at the edge of your island, the way you always did. Counting the stars above the Ral'taka Expanse, giving them names your mother would laugh at. The sky is warm and gold, and the islands drift.
>
> And then the stars go out. Not all at once, but in a line, one after another, as if something enormous is sliding across them. It's a shape: long, ridged, curved like the bones of something that died a very long time ago. A ribcage the size of a mountain, with a skull at its front. Behind it, things like tentacles trail through the sky.
>
> Its shadow falls across the island. Every bird goes silent. Somewhere below you, the warning drums start.
>
> And you hear your mother calling your name.

Let Brad play it. **The secrets stay Brad's call.** This scene is *before* the slave fight and long before the crew. Ask him beforehand whether it's played out in front of the table.

Then cut back: in the present, in the dark, **Scritch mimics a voice calling Korran's name.** Whether it's his mother's voice is Brad's call.

**Prompts for Brad:**
- How old are you, and what does the island look like?
- What did your mother teach you about the gold?
- What did you do when the ship came?

### The fight

Roll initiative.

- **Scritch:** use an **Assassin** stat block, plus **blindsight 30 ft. (echolocation)**, the Darkness ring and the Disintegrate ring. He fights from the dark, mimicking the party's voices so they can't tell who's where. **His target is the head**, so he goes for Megan first.
  - **His weakness:** his blindsight is sound. A Silence spell, or a loud enough noise (a slammed pneumatic door, the glitching motto sign, a burst of brass tubes), leaves him blind for that moment. Reward players who work it out.
  - **His second ring:** Disintegrate would finish the job instantly by turning the head to dust. Hold it as his desperate move, so the party has a turn to stop him. Or he saves it for himself, like the first Kenku.
- **Megan** fights alongside the party, protecting the head.
- **Tilptek** spends his turns **trying to get the lights back on.** He's impartial to everyone in the fight. Lines to use:
  - *"Please remain calm. The lighting is experiencing a disruption."*
  - *"I am not on anyone's side. But really, the lights* should *be on."*
  - *"Damages will be invoiced."*
- **The Thri-kreen clerks** don't sleep, and they **keep typing through the whole fight**, ducking with two arms and working with the other two.
- **Battlefield features:**
  - **The pneumatic doors** can slam shut on someone, or jam halfway.
  - **The floor sigils** flare under anyone who draws a weapon, which gives away positions in the dark.
  - **The light panels** strobe at the edges of the Darkness.
  - **The motto sign** glitches when it's hit: *"Results. Results. Results. Results."*
  - **The brass message tubes** whoosh contracts overhead.

### The client's agent, and the chase out the back

**Someone else is in the building:** the agent of whoever hired the Kenku. They came in to **raise the fee** after the first Kenku failed.

- **How the players spot them:** when the lights come back, or when someone with darkvision sees them, the agent is already backing toward a staff door behind the desk.
- **Who they are:** they're with **Varrow & Daughters (confirm)**. Proposed: one of the "Daughters." Choose any race you like, since Bral is a hub world.
- **Their help:** two hired toughs (Thug stat blocks) are covering the agent.

**The fight continues out the back.** The agent tries to sneak out through the Annex's back rooms, and Scritch covers the retreat. The back of the building:
- **The tube hub:** a room full of pneumatic tubes, with contracts flying past.
- **The archive:** stacks of contracts, with Thri-kreen archivists still filing.
- **The loading dock:** a cargo lift down to the street.

**How it ends:**
- **If they catch the agent:** a terrified confession. The agent knows the hire was about "the head" and "Varrow's ship."
- **If the agent escapes:** they drop a **trade card**: *Varrow & Daughters, Fine Spices*, with a dock address. It smells strongly of goldfleck pepper.
- **If Scritch is losing:** he escapes under cover of the Darkness. If he's cornered, he uses the Disintegrate ring on himself, like the first Kenku. Either way, the party might end up with his Darkness ring.

**Closing line,** over a crackling speaker as the lights finally come back on:
> *"This is Director Tallyworth. Results... are pending."*

### Megan parts ways

Out on the street, after the chase. Megan looks at the head one last time.

*Megan:*
> "It is not my daughter. Whoever wanted it quiet, that is your hunt now." *(He hands the head to the party.)* "I have a kingdom at war and no idea whether it still stands. I must find my way home." *(a long pause)* "The pizza. That was... very good. I will remember it."

He bows, clasps Korran's arm (*"My debt to you stands, Hadozee"*), and leaves. **He leaves the head with the party (confirm).**

**What the players don't know:** Megan returns later in the Ral'taka Expanse.

## 6. The spice house (climax, or the start of Session 3)

If there's time, raid **Varrow & Daughters**' dockside warehouse. **Recover Babe's recipe card, with the bottom torn off.** The recipe is all there, but the strip with the grower's mark is gone, and the client's ship has **already left** for the Expanse with it. The case closes on Bral, the client is gone, and the Shrike is ready to follow.

*Read-aloud, when they find it:*

> Babe's card, finally. The handwriting is unmistakable, the ink a little smudged with old flour. Every ingredient is there, every step. But the bottom of the card is gone, torn away in a clean, careful line, right where Lucy watched him write something "for the pepper man."

*If Lucy's flashback didn't plant the "pepper man" clue, cut the last clause.*

**Why this matters:** the ripped edge tells the party exactly what the thief was after. It wasn't the recipe. It was the mark.

**Combat set dressing** for the warehouse:
- cargo cranes;
- spice crates that burst into choking clouds;
- a treadwheel crane run by a **giant space hamster** that panics;
- a stack of Giff smokepowder that shouldn't be there.

**If the session is running long, stop after the Annex chase.** The spice house opens Session 3.

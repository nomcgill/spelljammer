# Season 2, Session 2: Brainstorm

Prep for Session 2. What happened in Session 1 is in [`session-notes.md`](session-notes.md). **Spoilers throughout. Nothing here is canon until it's run**, except the items under "Fixed."

## Fixed

- **Opens with a cold open in the [Ral'taka Expanse](../locations/raltaka-expanse.md).**
- **The Case of the Missing Stromboli is solved by the end of this session.** By then everyone is motivated to leave Bral **on the same ship, with the same destination**: the Ral'taka Expanse.
- **Krysta's head holds clues** to the theft of the Stromboli recipe's ingredient.
- **The Iron Annex contracted the Kenku assassin.** The players don't know this. Finding out who hired it will probably take them to the Annex, since a lot of questionable contract work goes through it.
- **The ruby ring** is a homebrew Ring of Disintegrate with one charge left.
- **Marfa knows everything** the party told her.
- **Korran's Shrike still needs registering at the DAV.** The costs are waived.
- **Marfa's deals weren't made yet.** She asked for their help, and the dart flew before she could say what she'd trade. So the session can open on the deals.

## The Case of the Missing Stromboli (proposed)

This version is built to fit everything that happened in Session 1. The earlier draft, parked before Session 1, is in [`session1-plan.md`](session1-plan.md#6-the-case-of-the-missing-stromboli-proposed). This one reuses its best parts.

### What Marfa tells them

- **The theft.** On Bugsy's reopening night, Babe came in person and made his legendary Stromboli at **the corner table**. Afterwards **the recipe card was gone**, and so was the parlor's **last jar of the secret ingredient**.
- **The cover-up.** Babe doesn't know yet. Marfa and Bugsy have been covering, but the parlors are running out of the ingredient, so **Babe will find out soon**. That's the clock.
- **The suspects.** Everyone points at the Joisey Jiffs, as Nicole's pitch says.

### What's actually going on

- **The secret ingredient** is a pepper that grows only on the gold-veined floating islands of the Ral'taka Expanse. Proposed name: *goldfleck pepper*. It's the "ingredient" in the theft.
- **The supply line.** The Ruths have bought it for years through the **Psurlon shipping hub**, because the Psurlons trade in cargo.
- **The real Krysta was the Ruths' contact there.** The **Pie Parlors IOU on her body** was payment for a shipment. This also starts to answer Nolan's prep question, "What was the real Krysta's story?" Check it against what you want for Megan.
- **The Ringer ate Krysta to take over the route.** A Psurlon Ringer keeps the memories of whoever it eats. So **the head holds Krysta's memories of the supply route**, plus the Ringer's own memory of **who it sold the route to**. That's why the head is the clue.
- **The client** bought the route from the Psurlons. They then hired the theft of the card and the last jar through the Iron Annex, so the Ruths can't make the Stromboli and can't follow the client to the source.
- **Nicole's twist: "What if the recipe was never meant to be stolen?"** It wasn't the point. **The route was.** The card and jar were taken so nobody could follow, and so the Jiffs would take the blame.
- **Why the Annex sent the Kenku.** The client learned that Marfa was recruiting outsiders, and that **the head had arrived on Bral**. The Annex reads the dock record Lawrence took, and Megan doesn't hide the head. The head was the one thing that could expose the client.

### Who's the client?

1. **Someone from Korran's past.** This is my recommendation, because it makes the case the doorway to the season's main driver. A captain from one of the crews that partnered with the *Star-Reaver*:
   - **What they want:** they've hunted the way back into the Expanse ever since the *Star-Reaver* vanished. The Ruths' pepper route is the only other way in.
   - **The goal:** the gold, and maybe the hoard.
   - **What they don't know yet:** that Driftblade is alive, sitting at Marfa's table, with the map.
   - **For Brad:** this also opens "some of the partnered crews may still be alive and want him dead."
2. **The plasmoid court.** The court wants the Expanse's gold to fund Pip's brother. This is a strong hook for Pip, but it pulls the season toward Pip's plot rather than Korran's. It works better as the client's backer or buyer later.
3. **The Joisey Jiffs.** They want the Stromboli and to ruin the Ruths. This is the most literal reading of the pitch, but nobody would expect it, and it doesn't lead to the Expanse. Better as the patsy: the client sold or leaked them a copy of the recipe so the evidence points at them.

### Was the Kenku after Marfa or the head?

The players will be asking this, so here are the options for the truth:
- **The head (my pick).** The contract was on "the lion's head in Marfa Ruth's company." Marfa was the backup target. It tells the players the head matters, which pushes them toward getting it questioned.
- **Marfa.** She's been digging for weeks, and the head was just in the way. This is simpler, but the head becomes less central.
- **Both.** The contract named both. This is the most menacing option, because the Annex would reissue whatever's left of it.

**If the dart was meant for the head, the poison should do something.** For example, it's a memory-rot, and the head is slowly losing what it knows. That puts a clock on getting it questioned, without anyone chasing the party.

## Getting the answer from the Iron Annex

The Annex runs on *"Discretion. Efficiency. Results. Results."* It won't just hand over a client's name. Some ways in:

- **The ruby ring is evidence.** *Proposed:* the Annex issues these rings to contractors as a "discretion" measure, so there's no body and no witness. Showing it to [Tilptek](../npcs/tilptek.md) proves the contract failed. It also tells the party the Annex is behind it. The party doesn't know yet that it has one charge left.
- **A failed contract gets reissued.** Tilptek can say, politely, that the contract is still open. That tells the party someone will try again.
- **Information for work.** Tilptek offers the client's name, or the client's contract alias, in exchange for the party taking an Annex job. This puts them in the Annex's debt, which is useful for later seasons.
- **Each PC's way in:**
  - **Lucy:** the Ruth name and money.
  - **Pip:** what's left of the diamonds.
  - **Korran:** he's the infiltrator, so he could get into the records.
  - **Megan:** intimidation, which will go badly in a building full of contract killers, and is funny for exactly that reason.
- **Tilptek remembers them.** He brokered the contract to find Tamenik by scrying Pip, so he knows exactly who these people are.
- **The DAV cross-check.** While Korran registers the Shrike, he can spot the client's ship registration, or its flight plan out toward the Expanse.

## Why everyone ends up on the same ship to the Ral'taka Expanse

| Who | Why they go |
| --- | --- |
| **Korran** | The client is heading for his homeland, is from his past, and is after the gold and the hoard he has the map to |
| **Lucy** | Recover the ingredient source and the card before Babe finds out, beat whoever framed the Jiffs, and prove herself to Babe |
| **Pip** | Marfa's deal: secure the supply line and the Ruth Syndicate backs his revolution. The Hadozee tribes could become allies too |
| **Megan** | The client ordered the route taken, and Krysta died for it. Marfa also promises to get him home afterwards |

**The ship** is Korran's newly registered Shrike. Naming it at the DAV is a moment for Brad.

## Marfa's deals (now that she knows everything)

- **Lucy:** if she solves it, Marfa makes sure Babe knows Lucy did it, which is proof toward the succession. Marfa also keeps quiet about Lucy trading the family favour IOU to the Psurlons. The party told her about that.
- **Korran:** the registration costs are already waived. Marfa knows about the map now, so she can offer Ruth supplies and crew for the trip, in exchange for a cut, or for the pepper groves staying friendly.
- **Pip:** a joint venture backing the revolution, conditional on results.
- **Megan:**
  - the Ruth network to find Thorndumm and a way home;
  - the family's records on Krysta's IOU;
  - someone on Bral who can cast Speak with Dead on the head.

## Cold open ideas (Ral'taka Expanse)

- **The groves.** Hadozee pepper farmers on a floating island watch an unfamiliar ship come in by **the old trade route**, the one only the Ruths' supplier used. The farmers welcome it as the usual buyer. It isn't.
- **The tribe.** A Cloudjumper elder tells children the story of the squid ship that came for the gold. One child points at the sky: *"Like that one?"*
- **The client.** The client's captain stands at the bow, holding Babe's recipe card. They tear off the last line and drop the rest overboard.

## Pacing

That's a lot for one session: the deals, the DAV, the Annex, getting the head questioned, the reveal, and departure. Some ways to tighten it:

- Put the DAV and the Annex in the same district, or have the DAV clerk point at the Annex.
- Let Speak with Dead be the payoff at the Annex, if the Annex knows a caster, rather than a separate errand.
- End the session as the Shrike leaves Bral. The Expanse itself is Session 3.

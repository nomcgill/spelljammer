# Dottie

**Appears in:** Season 2

Giff. **Boatswain** on [Korran's stolen Shrike](../ships/korrans-stolen-ship.md). Older. Shirt, no pants. Last name `[unconfirmed]`.

## Status

Aboard the Shrike. [Marfa](marfa-ruth.md) hired her. **Marfa's first**, Korran's second: she will keep the ship alive and **report home**.

She has run Ruth decks for thirty years: **cargo that never hits a registry, watches that get paid in hush.** Appeared in Season 2, Session 5; [Korran](../player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md) met her at the docks. [Luckyluciano](../player-characters/luckyluciano-ruth.md) has not met her this voyage. Marfa called her "one of us."

## Who she is

The crew's boss under the captain. Lines, hatches, watches, who is late. She is already disappointed, and she will keep them alive anyway. Marfa asked her to **bring Lucy home in one piece**.

**Korran's lever:** she is already in the life. Extra cut, look the other way, if Lucy stays in one piece. She still reports home. Hardest of Marfa's six to *own*.

## Look (draft)

Older Giff woman. Hippo head, grey around the muzzle, one tusk chipped. Rumpled white shirt, no pants, a clipboard she does not need. She's already disappointed.

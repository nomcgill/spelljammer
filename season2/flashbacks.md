# Season 2: Flashbacks

Short roleplay flashbacks, **one character at a time**, each set off by something in the present. They're exposition that the players act out rather than hear. They also pass the time nicely between bigger beats.

**Nothing here is canon until it's played.** Each player owns their character's past, so these are **prompts**. Nolan sets the scene and asks the questions, and the player fills in the answers. Whatever they say at the table becomes canon.

## How to run them

- **Size:** about five minutes each. Session 2 has three (Pip, Lucy, Korran), so keep each one tight and put them in quiet moments between the bigger beats. The placement is in the [Session 2 running order](session2-brainstorm.md#running-order-with-flashbacks).
- **The trigger:** a physical object or sensation in the present, such as a smell, a sound or a weapon. Describe the trigger, then cut: *"...and you're somewhere else."*
- **Who's in it:** the spotlight player plays themselves. Other players can be handed NPC roles from the memory, which keeps everyone involved.
- **Coming back:** cut back to the present on the same trigger, so the scene stays connected.

## Flashback 1: Pip (Nolan's)

**Trigger:** Pip's **warhammer**, still smacked with the Kenku's blood.

**The memory:** Pip back with a **family member, when things weren't going well.**

**Nolan's choice: the night before the coup, with his brother**, where Pip tries to talk him down first. Per Drew, the throne was meant to rotate between the three siblings: **Pip Jr.** (the eldest and now king), **Pip**, then **Phillis**. This is the night Pip's turn should have begun.
- **Inside the memory:** Pip loses his temper, the way a barbarian does, and smashes an object in the room. It breaks, and the shards injure a **Giff mercenary guard** by the door. **The guard's blood ends up on his warhammer**, which is why the Kenku's blood brings it back.
- **The opening script** is in the [Session 2 plan](session2-plan.md#flashback-pip).

The original options:
- **The night before the coup,** with his brother, before he was "the usurper." Did Pip try to talk him down first?
- **With his cousin,** the one who served the king through the coup. This was the last time they were on the same side. It plants the possible-defector thread.
- **The first time Pip drew blood for the crown.** That explains why blood on the hammer is the trigger.

Prompts for Drew:
- Who's with you, and where are you?
- What's going wrong for the kingdom, or for you?
- What does the family member say that you've never forgotten?
- Is this before or after you decided to move against your brother?

**Coming back:** the blood on the hammer. Whose was it in the memory?

## Flashback 2: Korran (proposed)

**Nolan's choice for Session 2: "the day the pirates arrived."**
- **The trigger:** the Kenku's **Darkness** falling in the Iron Annex.
- **The memory:** young Korran is counting stars on his island, until the *Star-Reaver*'s shape blots them out.
- **The opening script** is in the [Session 2 plan](session2-plan.md#flashback-korran).

**Other trigger options:**
- **The smell of goldfleck pepper** at the spice house or the Pie Parlor. It smells like home.
- **A Kenku mimicking a voice he knows,** for example his mother's, or Gloamcast's.
- **Gold leaf** on something in the Expanse.

**Scene options:**
- His mother, before the *Star-Reaver* came.
- The day the pirates arrived.
- Gloamcast at the helm, the captain who "always had a thing for" his mother.

**Careful:** the crew killings and the Hadozee slave he killed are Korran's secrets. **Let Brad choose** what the flashback shows. A flashback the players watch can reveal things to the party, so ask Brad before the session whether this one is private or shared.

Prompts for Brad:
- How old are you, and what does the island look like?
- What did your mother teach you about the gold?
- What's the last thing you said to her?

## Flashback 3: Lucy (proposed)

**Nolan's choice for Session 2:**
- **The trigger:** the empty leather **folder** from Babe's corner booth.
- **The memory:** Babe making the Stromboli, with Lucy watching.
- **The opening script** is in the [Session 2 plan](session2-plan.md#flashback-lucy).
- **A clue for her to find:** she remembers him writing something at the bottom of the card and saying it was *"for the pepper man."* That's the first hint of the grower's mark.
- **Alternative:** the "Bugsy's voice" clue from the reopening night triggers a memory of Lucy and Bugsy as kids.

**Other trigger options:**
- **The corner booth** at the Pie Parlor.
- **The taste of the real Stromboli,** once the card is recovered.
- **Hearing Tino's name.**

**Scene options:**
- **Babe teaching her to make the Stromboli,** or refusing to. Did he ever show her the card?
- **The Crustfall sting:** mid-deal with the fake "moonstone pie," the moment it went wrong.
- **Lucy and Bugsy as kids,** and when it became clear Bugsy was the heir.

Prompts for Nicole:
- What's Babe like when it's just the two of you?
- What did you want him to say that he never did?
- Did you know Tino was going to flip?

Megan is an NPC, so his flashback can come through **the head**. When it's questioned, play **Krysta's memory** as a flashback. Hand the players roles: Krysta's last days at the Psurlon hub, the Ruth shipment, the Ringer. Megan watches.

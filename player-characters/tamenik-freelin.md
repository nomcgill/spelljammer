## Background

Of the [Freelin Corporation](../factions.md) family. His nephew [Kirrak](../npcs/kirrak-freelin.md) is the CEO — and had Tamenik imprisoned in [Warden's Maw](../locations/wardens-maw.md) **for being annoying with his drums.**

In his own words: the corporation knows he was sent away, but most don't know his nephew did it. Whatever they've heard about him and why he's gone, he wouldn't know. And if they ever meet, Kirrak would be angry that he's out and would probably have him detained by security as a threat — on the assumption that Tamenik wants revenge.

## Season 1

The party's senses and their supernatural switchboard. Sick or unconscious for a large part of the season, and still the source of its most important information.

- **Session 2.** Noted as having "a minor disease."
- **Session 3.** It becomes **Blackstar Fever** — black veins appearing beneath the skin, glowing faintly. He goes unconscious, with nightmares. Mechanically it gave him **disadvantage on Wisdom and Intelligence checks and saves**, and he had less than a week to live.
- **Session 5.** The fever dream. He meets [Captain Gloamcast](../npcs/gloamcast.md) at the *Star-Reaver*'s spelljamming helm, and comes back with three things: that the fever is a **curse rather than a disease**, that he needs Greater Restoration or voidfish eggs within the week, and that [Korran](korran-cloudjumper.md) has been reading his treasure map wrong — the Thieves' Cant cipher is **Korran's late mother's birthday**. He relays it.
- Woken by [Jetson](../npcs/jetson.md) screaming in his face to an empty ship with five Psurlons spelling their way in. He **Feared** them off.
- **Session 6.** [Megan Forthfang](../npcs/megan-forthfang.md) — a **high-level paladin** — cast **Greater Restoration** and **cured the Blackstar Fever.** (Not the Chwinga, not Charm of Ruin.)
- **Session 7.** Opened a bedroom door mid-boarding and found someone who "has not been there and is a surprise" — per Nolan's prep, [the Chwinga](../npcs/chwinga.md).

## Threads on him

**He cannot be scried or teleported.** Something about his shell blocks both. This is the load-bearing fact of the endgame: it's why the hunters scried [Pip](pip-oozewald.md) instead, and why [Alkgleb](../npcs/alkgleb.md)'s teleportation circle can't extract anyone from the Damselfly. Megan suspects a curse and has offered to try lifting it, but hadn't attempted it as of the end of Session 7.

Worth saying plainly: **that curse is what kept Alkgleb from teleporting the party off their own ship.** Removing it would be a cure with a cost. Oddly, Marfa's beam-out worked anyway. Nolan hasn't decided how; his working idea is that the curse can be bypassed with Tamenik's consent.

His nephew is aboard the warship outside, specifically to collect him.

He was also the most recent carrier of a curse that killed the previous Hadozee who had it. Whether being cured means it's gone, or just moved on, is unaddressed.

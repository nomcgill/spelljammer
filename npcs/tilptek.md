# Tilptek

**Appears in:** Season 1

Thri-kreen. A **clerk** behind the reception desk at the [Iron Annex](../locations/iron-annex.md) on the Rock of Bral. He is not a fighter, not aboard the mothership, and not a threat to the party in person — he pages people and relays contracts.

## Status

Alive, on the Rock of Bral, working the desk.

## Appearance

Matte black exoskeleton with hints of bronze, reflecting the lobby lights at weird insectoid angles. Nested in a seat fitted for chitinous physiology behind a curved alloy desk.

All four hands are busy: one types Thri-kreen claw-code, one scrolls contract listings with a carved obsidian orb, one holds a ceramic mug labelled **"#1 Subordinate Drone"** full of a bubbling green liquid that smells faintly of sage and engine grease, and the fourth idly twiddles a metal triangular fidget spinner that hums airily. His mandibles twitch twice in what might be a smile.

## The dangling question

**Who is "the boss"?** Tilptek is a desk clerk relaying someone else's instructions. Whoever that is knew, within hours, both that Tamenik couldn't be scried and that Pip was a usable proxy. That's a third party with unusually good information, and they have not been named.

## Season 1

### What he did

Within a few hours of the Warden's Maw breakout, Tilptek paged [Kirrak](kirrak-freelin.md) to tell him his uncle had escaped. He already had instructions **from "the boss"** on how Kirrak could retrieve Tamenik despite Tamenik's immunity to scrying — the workaround being to scry [Pip](../player-characters/pip-oozewald.md) instead, since the plasmoids were already hunting him.

He also knew the plasmoids who bombarded Warden's Maw were after their "slime-brother-nemesis named Pip."

That is the extent of his role. The mothership threat came from [Alkgleb](alkgleb.md) and [Kirrak](kirrak-freelin.md); Tilptek was never aboard.

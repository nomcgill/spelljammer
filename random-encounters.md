# Random Encounters

Ideas for travel through Wildspace and the Astral Sea, so long trips don't have to be time-skipped. Copied from a website (source not recorded) and tidied up. **Inspiration only, not canon** for this campaign.

## 1. Trapped Traveler

After a meteor shower, the ship drifts past a wreck. Inside is a sealed room with no gravity and no air. A haggard **Astral Elf** floats inside, kept alive by an Air Bubble spell, and is out of spell slots and about to suffocate.

**Twist:** it's bait. Once the party rescues the Elf, pirates swarm them.

## 2. Religious Experience

After weeks of empty travel, the void takes on texture, and someone perceptive realizes the ship is **flying into the face of a god**. Its size is up to you.

- **Contact:** every creature with Intelligence 8 or higher hears a voice introducing itself as something beyond dimension and time. It has drifted here since "before the calamity," and has many names and powers.
- **Alignment is up to you.** A neutral god might give advice, answer some cosmic questions, then leave this plane.

## 3. A Plasmoid on a Mission

A young **chromatic dragon** streaks past, trailing light, then circles back. Riding it is a friendly **plasmoid named Sasquatch**, and the dragon is **Rasmadah**. They're on a mission to save their world, but they're lost and low on rations.

- **DC 12 Survival:** the party can direct them to the nearest hub to resupply and find a portal.
- **Reward:** as they leave, a **Chromatic Rose** drops onto the deck as thanks.

## 4. Gargantuans Arguing

In an empty stretch of Wildspace, **two ancient colossi** have been fighting for ages, for reasons mortals can't follow.
- Spells fill the air, and summoned minions fight endlessly.
- Celestial spirits keep healing both of them.

- **Taking sides** barely matters, because both are far too powerful.
- **The real prize:** artifacts the fighters have conjured, orbiting in their gravity among the rubble and corpses.

## 5. Fishy Space Fishermen

A fishing boat floats among peaceful star-creatures, its crew hauling lines. The ship looks distorted, the crew ignores the party, and if the party approaches from the side, the boat vanishes.

- **What's happening:** a **Fractine** has trapped the boat in another plane. The Fractine is two-dimensional and peaceful unless attacked, and it feeds on the magical creatures the crew fishes up.
- **Reward:** free the crew and they share their loot.

## 6. Ship of Royalty

A **Space Galleon** flees a pack of **Squid Ships** that are firing projectiles and spells at it. It's carrying a **noble family that owns a cluster of small planets**, and pirates want to kidnap and ransom them.

- **Options:** fight off the pirates, guide the Galleon to a portal, or ransom the nobles yourselves.
- **Reward for getting them home:** possibly the party's own asteroid, with an atmosphere.

## 7. Clockwork Commotion

A crew member finds a small bronze ball on deck. When touched, it whirs, heats up, sprouts spider legs and scuttles into the hold, eating anything metal.

- **If it's ignored for an hour:** a swarm of **Clockwork Horrors** pours out, destroying anything metal or crystalline.
- **The real danger:** they **take over the spelljamming helm** and start steering the ship into the Astral depths while tearing it apart.
- **Notes:** they speak Ziklight and only want to reproduce.

## 8. Classic Space Pirates

In a busy asteroid field, a **Nightspider ship** scuttles out from behind the rocks, latches on and boards. Scale the crew to the party. Some crew ideas:
- **Kuo-toa** whose god and captain is a barrel they willed to life.
- **Mind flayers** who call a Brain Dragon if they start losing.
- **Space clowns** who just want to put on a carnival for the crew.

- **Loot:** put good treasure in the captain's quarters.
- **Hazard:** the food in an alien galley may have side effects.

## 9. Astral Aberration Guard

A colossal, very slow aberration hovers around a strange structure. It might be an Astral Turtle Dragon or a Dreadnought.
- **The structure:** perhaps an obsidian altar that can summon great power or terrible creatures, or a portal to another plane.
- **Sneaking past:** a stealthy ship can slip behind it to investigate.
- **If caught:** the crew might be brutally killed, set impossible riddles, or end up living in its belly.

## 10. Isolated Community

An asteroid is hurtling toward the ship, with figures and buildings on its surface. If the party lands, they meet **a species that has never met another**.
- They have no space travel, and yours may be the first ship they've seen in hundreds of generations. To them, other life is a myth from old books.
- Their customs are strange, and they're endlessly curious.
- **Hook:** a brave young local stows away to see the multiverse.

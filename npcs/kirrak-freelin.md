# Kirrak

**Appears in:** Season 1

Tortle. **CEO of Freelin Corporation.** [Tamenik](../player-characters/tamenik-freelin.md)'s nephew, and the reason Tamenik was in prison at all.

## Status

Alive and hostile. Last seen aboard the plasmoid mothership. Tamenik escaped him at the end of Session 7 and is now on the Rock of Bral, where Freelin Corporation is based.

## Why he's the antagonist

He had his own uncle imprisoned in Warden's Maw **for being annoying with his drums.** From Tamenik's backstory: Freelin Corporation knows Tamenik was sent away, but most don't know it was Kirrak who did it, and Tamenik doesn't know what they've been told. If they meet, Kirrak would be angry that he's out and would probably have him detained by security as a threat — assuming, correctly or not, that Tamenik wants revenge.

## Season 1

### How he found them

Tamenik cannot be scried. [Tilptek](tilptek.md) at the [Iron Annex](../locations/iron-annex.md) on the Rock of Bral paged Kirrak within hours of the breakout, and already had instructions from "the boss" on how to retrieve Tamenik despite that protection. The workaround: **scry [Pip](../player-characters/pip-oozewald.md) instead**, since the plasmoids were already hunting him, and follow Pip to Tamenik.

Kirrak paid to join the plasmoid ship and hunt down his uncle personally. He travels with a hulking Tortle bodyguard carrying a hand-laser crossbow.

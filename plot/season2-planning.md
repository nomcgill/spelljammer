## What happened to Tamenik?

**Decided: option 1.** He goes home to Freelin Corporation, skipping Marfa's pizza dinner. Option 3 can still layer on top of it later, because of the "at least, he thinks he is" line.

The original ideas for his exit after the party reached the Rock of Bral. The **Remove Curse** beat in the prologue is fixed either way.

1. **He went home to Freelin Corporation.** Kirrak is still off aboard the plasmoid mothership, so Tamenik walks into the family company while its CEO is away. Kirrak eventually comes home to find his uncle back in the building. This gives a clean, happy exit, and a funny offscreen consequence you can mention later.
2. **He stayed on Bral as a drummer.** He's playing in the Low City, finally allowed to be annoying with his drums. The party can bump into him as a cameo, or he can pass along rumours.
3. **"At least, he thinks he is."** Lifting the curse made him **scryable** for the first time. Within days, someone found him: the Iron Annex's unnamed "boss," Kirrak, or someone else. He's simply gone. If you don't want a rescue mission, the party only learns about it later, and he's fine.
4. **The curse moved on instead of ending.** Blackstar Fever "passed" to him from Captain Gloamcast. The shell curse might pass too. If Remove Curse pushed it onto someone else, the obvious candidate is **Korran**, which ties into the Gloamcast and *Star-Reaver* story. That PC becomes unscryable and doesn't know why yet. Talk to Brad first.
5. **He signed on with another crew** and sends the occasional letter or Sending. This is the lowest-maintenance option.

## The common motivator

**Nolan's pick for Sessions 1 and 2:** Marfa's deal and the Stromboli case (see "Decided" above, and the [Session 1 brainstorm](../season2/session1-brainstorm.md#how-the-dinner-leads-to-the-end-of-session-2)). Solving the case should point everyone at one destination. The options below are still useful for what that destination is and what keeps them together after it.

Season 1 was about running away. Season 2 should be about **going after something**. Three ways to keep the party together:

### Option A: One shared goal, the *Star-Reaver* hoard

Korran's complete map leads to the hoard the *Star-Reaver* crew took from the [Ral'taka Expanse](../locations/raltaka-expanse.md). Each PC wants it for a different reason:

- **Korran:** his homeland, his people's gold, and his history with that crew.
- **Lucy:** infamy and fortune, and a way to prove to Babe she can run the family.
- **Pip:** money to raise allies for the revolution against his brother.

The tension is built in: Korran's people treated that gold as part of nature, not currency.

### Option B: The party takes contracts from the Iron Annex

This inverts Season 1. The Annex organised the manhunt against them; now they're the ones hired to hunt or retrieve. A contracting office suits a session-by-session structure, and the contracts can steer toward Korran's past. The party needs work and money, Pip needs mercenaries, and Lucy needs a reputation.

### Option C: Individual reasons that happen to align

- Korran owns the only ship they have, the [stolen plasmoid ship](../ships/korrans-stolen-ship.md).
- The party owes Marfa for the rescue.
- Megan owes Korran a debt.
- Pip needs the others as his first "allies."

This is the weakest on its own, but good as reinforcement for A or B.

**A possible combination:** a contract appears at the Iron Annex (B) that leads to the *Star-Reaver* hoard (A). The contract was posted by someone who knows Korran's history.

## Korran's past: what's already in play

Everything here is already canon, so the season can build on it:

- **The secret.** He killed the original *Star-Reaver* crew one by one. The party doesn't know. Gloamcast called him "the only one left" and appeared not to know either. **Decide with Brad privately** how and when this comes out.
- **His start with the crew.** He earned the pirates' respect by **killing another Hadozee slave**. That's a second buried sin, and this one is against his own people.
- **Old enemies.** Per his backstory, some original or partnered crews may still be alive and want him dead.
- **The map.** He has both halves and the cipher is his mother's birthday, but it's only partly decoded. Two symbols near the destination were unreadable.
- **The *Star-Reaver*.** Its location is unknown. The Spelljamming Helm, the Star Chart of the Lost Spheres and the Void Crystal Focus are still aboard, and the **Star Chart helps navigate the Ral'taka Expanse**.
- **Gloamcast loved Korran's mother.**
- **Korran's stolen ship** came from the plasmoid fleet, and its details are still to be decided.

## Twist ideas

Brainstorm only. Check the Korran twists with Brad first, since they rewrite his backstory.

- **A survivor of the purge.** Someone from the original *Star-Reaver* crew lived, knows what Korran did, and wants the hoard, Korran, or both. They might have been in Warden's Maw the whole time, which ties into the prison-revenge hook.
- **The slave Korran killed had family.** Someone from the Ral'taka tribes knows how "Driftblade" earned his place.
- **Gloamcast and Korran's mother.** Gloamcast said "I always had a thing for her." That could be more than a crush. If it went as far as Gloamcast being Korran's father, that is a huge rewrite, and Brad would have to agree to it.
- **The gold is cursed.** Gloamcast said Blackstar Fever is "more so a curse than a poison or a disease." What if it's the curse on whoever plundered the Expanse? That would explain why Gloamcast had it, and why Tamenik caught it near the *Star-Reaver*.
- **The Iron Annex's "boss" is from Korran's past**, perhaps a captain from one of the partnered crews. Whoever it is knew within hours that Tamenik couldn't be scried and that Pip could be.
- **Someone else wants the hoard.** It could be Pip's brother's court (Gelp, Alkgleb), which would let Pip's plot collide with Korran's. It could be the **Joisey Jiffs**, which would bring in Lucy's plot.
- **The hoard isn't just gold.** Something sacred to the Hadozee is in it, which changes what Korran owes his people.

## Season beats

**Nolan's frame:** the party solves **a small mystery on the Rock of Bral**, then becomes strongly motivated to go to the **Ral'taka Expanse**. Everything below is proposed and can be rearranged. The detailed Session 2 version is in [`season2/session2-brainstorm.md`](../season2/session2-brainstorm.md).

### Beat 1: The Case of the Missing Stromboli, on Bral (Sessions 1 to 2, possibly 3)

- **Already played:** docking, Marfa's breakfast, and the Kenku attack.
- **The investigation:** Marfa is flustered and sends the party to the Iron Annex. From there they follow several avenues: outpaying or pressuring the Annex, the Kenku guild and its ring, the reopening night, the Jiffs, the docks, the DAV, and getting Krysta's head questioned.
- **How it closes:** the case closes on Bral. The card is recovered, Bugsy and the Jiffs are cleared, and the culprit is named. But **the client has already left for the Expanse.**
- **Loose ends tied off:** the Shrike is registered and named. The head **gave a route in the Psurlon's voice** (Session 4): *"Through the gold. Follow the warm current, until the islands."* That is the merchant route within the Expanse. Tino sold it to Varrow; the party may still need Tino, the warehouse, the DAV filing, or Korran's map to follow it.

### Beat 2: The crossing (one or two sessions)

- **Possible encounters,** all from the hook bank:
  - neogi in a nightspider;
  - ship gravity failing;
  - teleporting on and off the ship.
- **The *Star-Reaver* is now optional.** Its Star Chart helps navigate the Expanse, but the party may have the way in from Tino or the DAV filing. It's still there for the Spelljamming Helm and Void Crystal Focus, and for learning that **someone else has been aboard**.
- **Flashbacks** fill the travel time. See [`season2/flashbacks.md`](../season2/flashbacks.md).

### Beat 3: The Ral'taka Expanse (the middle of the season)

- **The setting:** floating jungle islands, gold that the Hadozee treat as part of nature, and **the goldfleck pepper groves**.
- **The client's crew** is already there, using Babe's grower's mark to get past the growers.
- **Korran's past comes due.** Pick from the [twist ideas](#twist-ideas): a survivor of the purge, the family of the Hadozee slave he killed, or the Cloudjumper Tribe itself. **How much Korran's secret surfaces is Brad's call.**
- **Pip** could win the Hadozee tribes as **allies for the revolution**.
- **Lucy** can secure the supply line, Babe's legacy, herself.

### Beat 4: The hoard (the finale)

- **The confrontation:** the party against the client's crew at the hoard, using Korran's map.
- **The choice:** Korran's people see the gold as nature. To Pip it's a war chest, and to Lucy it's fortune. The hoard should **force a choice** between the PCs' motives, not just pay them all out.
- **Unresolved:** which twist lands here is still open.

### Threads that run in the background

- **Megan's war at home.** He wants to get back to the war between the **Red Lions and the Brown Lions**, and doesn't know how it's going. Krysta's head gives him the first news. Paying this off is a strong **season finale or Season 3 hook**.
- **The Iron Annex and "the boss."** The Annex brokered both the manhunt in Season 1 and the Kenku contract.
- ~~**The Kenku guild wants its ring back.**~~ **Dropped:** The Unkindness doesn't care about the rings once they're issued (Nolan).
- **Someone from Warden's Maw wants revenge.** This is the later hook from the hook bank.
- **The plasmoid court and Gelp.** Pip's revolution needs a face to the enemy at some point.
- **Tamenik at Freelin Corporation.** He's available as a cameo or a rumour, and Kirrak comes home eventually.

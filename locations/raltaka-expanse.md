# The Ral'taka Expanse

**Appears in:** Season 1, Season 2

[Korran](../player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md)'s homeland, and the campaign's most clearly signposted future destination.

## What it is

A **jungle plane of floating islands** in Wildspace. Home of the Cloudjumper Tribe and other primal Hadozee tribes.

The plane had an **excessive amount of gold**. The Hadozee tribes respected that gold as part of nature and used it in crafts rather than as currency.

**Gold as tradition** (Season 2, Session 3):
- **As building material:** in the Cloudjumper Tribe's home, gold is plentiful enough to be **built into their structures**.
- **As honor:** Korran's mother taught him it's part of their tradition, **how their people honor the land.**
- **The growers:** that tradition is why the growers **dust their crops with gold**. It belongs to the **peoples of the Ral'taka Expanse**, who aren't necessarily all Hadozee (Nolan).

## What happened to it

Pirates out of the Astral Sea plundered the plane for its gold and took several Hadozee as slaves. Their ship was the [*Star-Reaver*](../ships/star-reaver.md). Korran was among the captives.

## Why the party will go back

Korran holds a **complete treasure map** to the hoard the original *Star-Reaver* crew took — the plundered Hadozee gold, plus whatever else the crew accumulated. It's written in Thieves' Cant, in two halves that he has now pieced together.

The map's real value is navigational: the Expanse is a maze of floating islands, and the map gives the **route through it** to where the gold resides. Without it the hoard is effectively unreachable.

The cipher is **Korran's late mother's birthday**, relayed through Tamenik's fever dream by [Captain Gloamcast](../npcs/gloamcast.md). Before that was known, two symbols near the destination were unreadable. As of Session 7, Korran has made "a little progress" decoding it.

Per the captain: *"That treasure belongs to the crew of the Star-Reaver, and he's the only one left after this."*

## Status

Never visited by the party. Seen once in a Season 2 cold open (below). Everything above comes from Korran's backstory document and the session notes.

## Season 1

Never visited. The party only heard about it through Korran, and the map to it became readable once the cipher came out of Tamenik's fever dream.

## Season 2

### Session 2

Shown in a **cold open** the players watched without their characters ([session notes](../season2/session-notes.md#cold-open-the-raltaka-expanse)):
- **Hadozee growers** on a small floating island grow peppers that glint because they're **dusted with gold**.
- An old trade route reaches the island that **only one buyer ever uses**.
- A strange ship arrived along that route. Someone aboard held up a **torn strip of card bearing a mark in an old Giff's handwriting**, and the grower welcomed them: *"Welcome back, friend."*

Who was aboard, and what the mark is, the players don't know.

A Midjourney prompt for the Expanse, seen from one of its islands, is in the [Session 3 plan](../season2/session3-plan.md#1-opening-korrans-flashback).

### Session 3

The raid on the Cloudjumper Tribe was played as Korran's [flashback](../season2/session-notes.md#opening-korrans-flashback):
- **The raiders:** the *Star-Reaver* came with **several other pirate ships**, crewed by Hadozee and other races.
- **The island:** Korran glided island to island toward home.
- **Captured:** he was taken aboard the *Star-Reaver*.

At the end of the session, the party found a ***Varrow & Daughters, Fine Spices*** card on Tino. Korran smelled gold-dusted pepper on it.

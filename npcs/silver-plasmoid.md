# The Silver Plasmoid

**Appears in:** Season 1

The assassin that boarded the Damselfly in Session 7. Unnamed, and **never said a single word.**

## Status

**Dead.** Killed by the party in Session 7, which bought enough time for [Marfa](marfa-ruth.md) to beam them out.

## Stats

Nolan built it on the [Drow Inquisitor (Summoner Variant)](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/2560785-drow-inquisitor-summoner-variant) — **CR 14**, 11,500 XP, darkvision 120 ft., immune to frightened, passive Perception 20, and a suite of high-DC divination and psychic abilities (*Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* p. 184). Called the "Plasmoid Inquisitor" in the prep notes.

The relevant variant feature is **Summon Demon (1/day)**: a 50% chance to summon a **Yochlol** that acts as its ally for 10 minutes. That is what happened — the party killed the Yochlol it conjured.

## Season 1

### What it did

- Summoned an abyssal fiend — a Yochlol — which the party slayed.
- Stabbed with a **silver sword**.
- Summoned a **separate sword at range** to attack Pip.
- Said nothing at all.
- Was killed by the party.

### Reading it

It arrived on [Alkgleb](alkgleb.md)'s order, given in Primordial: *"Send in the other ones."* The phrasing suggests more than one thing came aboard, and Nolan's prep said he intended to add one or two more summoned creatures that were **"not affiliated" with the plasmoid crew**.

A silent CR 14 inquisitor with silver weaponry is a notably specialised thing to send after a barbarian, a bard, a warlock and a rogue. It died without giving away anything about its motive.

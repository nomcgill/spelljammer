# The Rock of Bral

**Appears in:** Season 1, Season 2

The destination for all of Season 1, and where **the party finally arrived** at the end of it.

## Status

**The party is here.** After the mothership boarding in Session 7, the rest of the party arrived in Marfa's [*Slice Runner*](../ships/ruth-family-ships.md#the-slice-runner), with Korran alongside in his [stolen ship](../ships/korrans-stolen-ship.md). Everyone made it, including [Jetson](../npcs/jetson.md) and [Megan](../npcs/megan-forthfang.md).

[Pip](../player-characters/pip-oozewald.md)'s plan on arrival: **gather allies to lead a revolution against his usurping brother, the plasmoid king.**

## What it is

The major port city of the setting — an asteroid city, and in published Spelljammer material a grimy free port known for gambling, exotic goods, and no questions asked. Luckyluciano's backstory describes it exactly that way.

**It's a hub world: any D&D race can appear here.** Because of its universal nature, people from every world pass through, so a Bral crowd or NPC can be any race (Nolan's ruling).

## Who has what there

- **[Luckyluciano](../player-characters/luckyluciano-ruth.md)** ran one of the flagship [Babe Ruth's Galactic Pie Parlors](../npcs/babe-ruth.md) orbiting Bral. It was the syndicate's Rock of Bral front, and her arrest there is what put her in Warden's Maw.
- **[The Iron Annex](iron-annex.md)** — a contracting office, a division of the Spherewide Contracting Compact, staffed by [Tilptek](../npcs/tilptek.md).
- **[Kirrak](../npcs/kirrak-freelin.md)** and **Freelin Corporation** — Kirrak visited the Iron Annex here after the breakout. Tamenik's family company.

So being on Bral means sharing the same city as the corporation run by the nephew who imprisoned Tamenik, the brokerage that organised the manhunt, and the scene of Luckyluciano's arrest.

## Season 1

### Getting there

The campaign treated it as the place everyone was trying to get back to: *"We should reach Bral within a day or 2! Everyone is going home!"*

Quoted as one to two days from Warden's Maw at 3 million miles per hour. Sabotage to the hyperspace drive stretched that out across five sessions; restoring the Hyperdrive Core with diamond dust brought it back to one day.

The unused alternative, per the adventure ideas doc: with the spelljamming helm damaged and no repair, that one-day journey becomes **445 years.**

### Arrival

Nolan's outline ends with an **Epilogue — The Rock of Bral**, with the question "What's next for everyone?" Arrival closed the Season 1 arc.

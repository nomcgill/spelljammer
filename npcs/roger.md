# Roger

**Appears in:** Season 2

Giant space hamster. Treadwheel crane at the [Varrow & Daughters warehouse](../locations/varrow-warehouse.md). **Roger** is the name in his own head. Nobody at Varrow & Daughters called him that; they called him Hamster.

## Status

Left in the warehouse after Season 2, Session 5. Too fat to climb out of the wheel. [Luckyluciano](../player-characters/luckyluciano-ruth.md) spoke with him (Speak with Animals) and tried to recruit him as a war steed. He declined.

## Who he is

The warehouse's lift. He walks the wheel that hauls sacks to the loft. Power can be off; he doesn't care. He wants feed, digesting, and water. He does not want battle, pizza, or to leave.

He knows **one** two-legged creature uses the upstairs office: yellow hair, doesn't feed him, walks around like he owns the place. That is always [Varrow](varrow.md) (Nolan). Blonde. Astral elf.

## Season 2

### Session 5

Lucy calmed him after the thunder glyph (Animal Handling 16 with advantage), fed him a torn sack, and left him there.

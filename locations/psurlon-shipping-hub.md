# The Psurlon Shipping Hub

**Appears in:** Season 1

A Psurlon trading and storage operation built **inside the hollowed-out remains of a dead god**, in the ribs specifically. Where Sessions 4 through 6 took place.

## How the Psurlons deal

Their opening lines:

> "What does the group have to offer?"
>
> "Would you like to incur a debt?"

They are not intimidated by threats, only skeptical of newcomers. What they want is **favours** — in the party's case, anything connected to [Luckyluciano](../player-characters/luckyluciano-ruth.md)'s family's influence.

## Loose ends

- **Krysta's body** is still here, with a Babe Ruth's Galactic Pie Parlors **IOU** on it — evidence tying the Psurlon operation to the Ruth syndicate.
- The party killed several Psurlons on the way out. This is a trading hub that keeps ledgers and asks about debts; they left owing blood.
- Pip noticed he was being **scried** while standing in this yard.
- A.R.I.A.'s **third candidate coordinate** was never visited.

## Season 1

### Finding it

Psurlons were rumoured to have made a city inside a dead god's remains in this sector. [A.R.I.A.](../npcs/aria.md) marked **three candidate coordinates**. The first was a [Cosmic Horror](../bestiary/season1-encounters.md) instead. The second was this hub. **The third was never visited.**

Dozens of Psurlons watched the Damselfly approach warily. The party ultimately traded Luckyluciano's favour IOU for what they needed.

### The container yard

Cargo containers stacked in a yard, one of which held [Megan Forthfang](../npcs/megan-forthfang.md) petrified by Flesh to Stone, stored like cargo — Nolan's comparison was Han Solo in carbonite. A Scroll of Greater Restoration was stuck to the outside of his container with green goo.

[Krysta](../npcs/krysta.md) waited outside the container, posing as Megan's daughter and acting as the party's guide while openly communicating with the Psurlons around her.

The full cargo list and its complications live in a **private Google Doc** that isn't readable, so the yard's other contents aren't recorded here.

### What the party got and lost

Got: 100,000 gp of diamonds (inside Pip), voidfish eggs, Megan, and Krysta's head. Lost: nothing material, though Luckyluciano's family favour was spent, and half the cargo rations had already been destroyed by her own fireball at a Feyr.

Never obtained: **astral silk** for Jetson's wound.

## Background

Born to a primal Hadozee tribe — the **Cloudjumper Tribe** of the [Ral'taka Expanse](../locations/raltaka-expanse.md), a jungle plane of floating islands with an excess of gold. The tribes respected that gold as part of nature and used it in crafts. A tough youthful life, and he was always fascinated with the stars.

Pirates out of the Astral Sea plundered the plane for its gold and took several Hadozee as slaves. Their ship was the [*Star-Reaver*](../ships/star-reaver.md), hull built from a dead god's ribcage, a skull at the bow. Korran was among the captives.

He earned the crew's respect **by fighting and killing another Hadozee slave**, and was recruited into the pirate life. He took the name **Driftblade** for a fighting style that was brutal but swift.

Then he betrayed them. He killed the original crew **one by one**, as revenge for the plundering of his homeland, and escaped without leaving a trace. Years of bouncing between pirate crews for gold and glory followed, until his latest crew was ambushed and captured by a githyanki patrol — which is how he ended up in [Warden's Maw](../locations/wardens-maw.md).

## Season 1

The party's scout and infiltrator, and the one whose past keeps steering the plot.

- **Session 2.** Recognised the derelict squid ship as the *Star-Reaver*, the ship he was enslaved on and later emptied. He remembered it was still loaded with plundered magic items. Took the **Fish Suit** and **Boots of Gravitation**; the party left the Spelljamming Helm, Star Chart, and Void Crystal Focus behind.
- Revealed he holds **half of a treasure map** in Thieves' Cant, leading to the hoard the original crew took — including the Hadozee gold from his homeland.
- **Session 5.** Freed [Megan Forthfang](../npcs/megan-forthfang.md) from the Flesh to Stone that had petrified him, earning a debt: *"I owe a debt to you, Hadozee."*
- **Session 6.** Received the map cipher from [Tamenik](tamenik-freelin.md), relayed out of a fever dream from [Captain Gloamcast](../npcs/gloamcast.md): the key is **Korran's late mother's birthday**, and the captain had loved her. Korran had been reading the Cant too literally.
- **Session 7.** Made a little progress decoding the map. Took over enough piloting to let Luckyluciano take a long rest. Then went **outside** — spacewalking 120 feet of a 300-foot crossing toward a vulnerable point behind the cannons on the ship's underside, on a **stealth roll of 29**. The Fish Suit makes the vacuum walk possible. He found a **crack in the mothership's hull** and got inside, planning to thwart either the ship itself or Alkgleb and Kirrak. Instead he found a **smaller ship in the loading bay**, took control of its spelljamming helm, and **hijacked it**. He wasn't aboard the Damselfly when Marfa beamed the others out. He flew his [stolen ship](../ships/korrans-stolen-ship.md) to the Rock of Bral alongside the *Slice Runner*.

## Season 2

- **Session 1.** At the Bral docks he told [Lawrence](../npcs/lawrence.md) he had **"not much of a last name to give."** His Shrike still has to be registered at the DAV, with the costs waived thanks to Marfa. At the Pie Parlor he ordered a pizza with everything on it, including fruits, vegetables, meats and far too many toppings.
- **Session 2.** Marfa offered him a **crew of 6** for the Shrike's upcoming journey. He went to check on the Shrike (all fine), and on the way told Megan to give up on getting home. His **History** roll pointed Megan to the nearest temple, to get Speak with Dead cast on Krysta's head. He then joined Lucy, Pip and Jetson heading to the Iron Annex.
- **His account to Marfa:** he was captured after a few **failed raids on Githyanki outposts**. Some raids succeeded, but the crew bit off more than it could chew, and he was sent to Warden's Maw for **petty revenge**. Nolan calls this an "overall partial truth." It fits with, and adds to, the backstory's "ambushed and captured by a githyanki patrol."
- **The fight:** he was the **only one to spot the Kenku assassin** by the exit. He threw a dagger and missed, which bought Megan the moment to block the dart with Krysta's head.

## Threads on him

The captain called him "the only one left" of the *Star-Reaver*'s crew — true, because Korran killed the rest, and the captain does not appear to know it. Korran hasn't told the party either.

His own backstory raises two more: some of the original or partnered crews **may still be alive and want him dead**, and where the *Star-Reaver* is now is unanswered.

He is now on the Rock of Bral with a ship of his own, stolen from the plasmoid fleet. Whether the plasmoids noticed him, and whether they want it back, is `[unconfirmed]`.

# Gu

**Appears in:** Season 1

Plasmoid. A long-term prisoner at [Warden's Maw](../locations/wardens-maw.md), and — according to the plasmoids who came for him — their **vice president.**

## Status

**Unknown.** Survived the Githyanki ship's balcony blast. Last seen tackling [Aryndac](aryndac.md) off the ship harbor into the Astral Sea. The party does not know what became of him after that.

## Why he matters

He is the reason the party is free. The fleet that came for him is the same plasmoid mothership under [Alkgleb](alkgleb.md) that caught the party in Session 7. The rescue that freed them circled back around.

Nobody has asked what the plasmoids' vice president was doing in a Githyanki prison, or whether the party's escape looked to anyone like a plasmoid extraction — or whether Gu is still out there.

## Season 1

### What he did

Gu was the prisoner who kept telling people to stay away from the windows at night. He'd been saying it for over a thousand meals, so nobody listened. And what even is night, when time is distorted and hard to track outside a Wildspace system?

He was right. A plasmoid fleet arrived to **bombard the prison and free their vice president**.

Freed, he did not vanish into the fleet's arms. He came for the party — **cell by cell, one by one** — *"Follow me!"* — and led them onto a balcony, where he announced himself to the sky: *"I'm free! I'm down here! Here!"* A Githyanki ship blasted him.

**He survived.** His body then flew across the hangar and tackled Aryndac off the ship harbor into the Astral Sea. Aryndac Misty Stepped aboard the Damselfly mid-fall — unnoticed — so Gu's intervention put the party's first real antagonist on their ship.

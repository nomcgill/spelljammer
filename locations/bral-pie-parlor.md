# Babe Ruth's Galactic Pie Parlor (Bral Flagship)

**Appears in:** Season 2

The flagship parlor that [Luckyluciano](../player-characters/luckyluciano-ruth.md) used to run, orbiting the [Rock of Bral](rock-of-bral.md). It was the Ruth Syndicate's Bral front until her arrest in the Crustfall sting. It has been **reopened under [Bugsy](../npcs/bugsy.md)**.

## What it's like

The read-aloud description and Midjourney prompt used at the table are in the [Session 1 plan](../season2/session1-plan.md#3-breakfast-at-lucys-old-pie-parlor). It's retro-futuristic: checkerboard floor, red vinyl booths, chrome, neon and porthole windows looking down on Bral.

- **The house drink is Hawaiian Punch**, the Pie Parlors' equivalent of Coca-Cola.
- **The corner table** is where **the Case of the Missing Stromboli began**, according to [Marfa](../npcs/marfa-ruth.md).

## Season 2

### Session 1

Marfa brought the party here for breakfast after docking, and shooed Bugsy away so she could have the room to herself. [Jetson](../npcs/jetson.md) was sent to sit with his mother at the end of the bar.

At the table the party told Marfa the whole Season 1 story. When she asked for their help with the Stromboli case, a **Kenku assassin** by the exit fired a poison dart. Megan blocked it with Krysta's head, and the Kenku disintegrated itself with a ruby ring. See [Season 2 session notes](../season2/session-notes.md#session-1--breakfast-at-the-pie-parlor).

# Captain Gloamcast

**Appears in:** Season 1

Old Hadozee. Former captain of the [*Star-Reaver*](../ships/star-reaver.md). Dead, and still moving the plot.

*(Session 5's fever dream once called him "Aachen Eelson" — that name is discarded. Gloamcast is canon.)*

## Status

Dead — of Blackstar Fever, which he passed to [Tamenik](../player-characters/tamenik-freelin.md). Appeared to Tamenik in a fever dream, seated at the *Star-Reaver*'s spelljamming helm.

## Why he matters

Three things come from his one conversation with Tamenik:

1. Blackstar Fever is a **curse**, not a disease — which reframes Tamenik's illness and possibly the anti-scrying curse on his shell.
2. The cipher to Korran's treasure map is **Korran's late mother's birthday**, which unlocked the Ral'taka Expanse hoard.
3. He **loved Korran's mother**, and he says Korran is the last surviving crew member of the *Star-Reaver* — which Korran knows is true because Korran killed the rest of them.

That last line is a loaded one. The captain is being warm to the man who murdered his crew, and doesn't appear to know it. Korran hasn't told anyone.

## Season 1

### What he said

In Tamenik's fever dream. On the fever:

> "I was the last one with this fever. Thought it might die with me. But seems you got it next. It's more so a curse than a poison or a disease."
>
> "You're gonna need a Greater Restoration or some voidfish eggs. Otherwise you'll be a goner in less than a week."

On the map, referring to [Korran](../player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md):

> "If he's having trouble with my half of the map, tell him that he's reading it wrong. He always took the Cant too literally. Tell him that the cipher is his late mother's birthday. I always had a thing for her."
>
> "That treasure belongs to the crew of the *Star-Reaver*, and he's the only one left after this."

# The Chwinga

**Appears in:** Season 1

A Chwinga — tiny, wordless-adjacent, enormously powerful, and treating the entire campaign as a game of hide and seek.

## Status

**Whereabouts `[unconfirmed]`.** Tamenik opened a bedroom door in Session 7 and found "someone who has not been there and is a surprise." Per Nolan's prep, that's the Chwinga. It said *"You're not blue shiny! Hide & Seek, Find me!"* and immediately vanished. The party was then beamed off the Damselfly, and whether the Chwinga stayed aboard or followed is unknown.

## What happens next

Nolan's prep note is short and significant: **once found, an enormous anti-magic circle erupts.**

Whether anyone found it before the party left the Damselfly is `[unconfirmed]`. As far as the notes go, the circle hasn't erupted yet.

Its pattern so far is consistent: it shows up when Pip is in danger, it finds him funny, and it helps sideways.

## Season 1

### First appearance

Mid-fight with the Cosmic Horror in Session 4, **time stopped** for everyone except [Pip](../player-characters/pip-oozewald.md). Giggling. *"Hide and Seek!"*

Its essence had been trapped in one of the Feyr's **bronze eyes**, which hit the ground with a thud. Freed, it looked Pip over:

> "You're a funny color." *Giggles.* "I help? I help big thing to attack ship?"

It left him the **Charm of Ruin** — as an action, touch a nonmagical object or a 5-foot-cube section of a larger one and reduce it to dust. Three uses, then the charm vanishes. See [`items.md`](../items.md).

### Since then

In Session 5 it giggled at Pip's diamond-filled stomach: *"You're shinier than your cousin."* That cousin is a real, separate relative — not Pip's brother the king — who served the king through Pip's failed coup.

In Session 7 it turned up aboard the Damselfly mid-boarding (see Status).

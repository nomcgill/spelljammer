## 1. Opening: Tallyworth

The party is in the director's office with Tino slumped on the carpet. The window crank is still stuck half open. A speaker in the ceiling crackles:

> *"This is Director Tallyworth. Results... are pending."*

**The camera:** in the corner of the ceiling, beside the speaker, is a small **security camera**. Its lens turns with a soft whir to follow whoever moves. No check needed. Tallyworth is watching all of this, and he wants them to know it. (Lucy blasted the lounge camera, but not this one.)

A pause. Then, flat and precise:

> *"You have damaged a clerk, a camera, a computer and a window. You are holding a client of this office. Please remain where you are. An invoice is being prepared."*

**What Tallyworth wants:** Discretion is the Annex's brand. He wants this tidied up quietly and paid for. He won't fight. He can't afford to be seen losing a client to the Ruths, but he also can't afford a war with them.

**How the party gets out with Tino.** Let them pick an approach. One check decides it:

| Approach | Check | DC | Success | Failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lucy invokes the family | **Intimidation** or **Persuasion** (Lucy) | 15 | The invoice goes to Marfa, and they walk out with Tino | They still walk out, but Tallyworth bills Marfa **double**, and she'll mention it |
| Talk business with Tallyworth | **Persuasion** or **Deception** | 15 | He'll sell them a **dock manifest** for the Varrow ship (see below) | He says nothing more and lets them leave |
| Leave the way Tino tried to | **Athletics** (Pip, to force the window) and **Stealth** for the group | 13 | They're out before anyone comes to see | Thri-kreen security meets them at the bottom, and someone has to talk their way past |

**The manifest (optional):** for the right price, Tallyworth sells a copy of a dock manifest showing a ship registered to Varrow & Daughters, which **cleared Bral for the Ral'taka Expanse days ago**. He won't name his client, because that would break Discretion. Selling a dock record isn't the same thing.

**The long hook (Nolan's pick):** before the speaker clicks off, the camera settles on Pip:

> *"Do give my regards to the Prince. We have found him very... easy to locate."*

The Annex arranged the Season 1 scrying of Pip, so Tallyworth knows exactly who Pip is, who's hunting him, and how they found him.

**Why he doesn't offer to help Pip now: it would be very expensive.** The Annex sells to whoever pays, and what Tallyworth knows about the plasmoid court is worth a lot. Right now the party can't afford it, and Tallyworth knows that too. If Pip pushes, my draft:

> *"The Annex is always happy to discuss the Prince's... situation. When the Prince can afford us. I'm sure you understand: Discretion is not cheap. Results, even less so."*

That leaves a door open for later in the season, when Pip has the Ruths' money or the Expanse's gold. And it raises a question for Pip and the party: what would Tallyworth charge, and what does he already know that he's selling to the other side?

## 2. Tino talks

Somewhere private: the Pie Parlor's back room is the natural place, before Marfa comes in. Tino wakes up tied to a chair or held by Pip.

### The jar in his coat

When anyone searches Tino, or the moment he's dumped in a chair, his coat clinks. In an inside pocket is **a small glass jar of peppers dusted with gold**. Its lid is loose, and that's the smell on the Varrow card.
- **No check to find it.** It's the first thing in his coat.
- **Lucy, Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (History), DC 12:** she knows that smell. It's **her father's kitchen**, late at night, when he made the Stromboli. She's smelled it her whole life without knowing what it was.
- **Korran** already knows it from the card. It smells like home.
- **Put the two of them in the same moment.** Lucy smells her father's kitchen, and Korran smells his childhood. That's the first time the players can see that the Stromboli and Korran's homeland are the same story.

**Tino, caught with it** (my draft): *"It's a sample! You gotta show people the goods. Nobody pays for a drawing on a card unless you show 'em what it gets 'em."*

### Who he is now

He's been hiding since his testimony. He's nervous, quick-talking and self-pitying, and he'd sell anyone to save himself, as he already has.

### The interrogation

He resists at first, then breaks. **Run it on two checks:**
- **Insight (DC 14), any time he talks:** on a success, the player knows whether that line is true or self-serving. He lies about small things, like how much he was paid, and tells the truth about big ones, because he's scared.
- **Intimidation or Persuasion (DC 15), to get the buyer's name:**
  - **Advantage:** if Pip simply picks him up, or if Lucy appeals to family.
  - **Success:** *"Varrow."* He's more afraid of them than of the Ruths.
  - **Failure:** he holds out, and they'll have to get the name from the card, the manifest or the head.

### What he says

My drafts, grouped by what the players ask. Use them in whatever order the questions come. He cracks a little more with each answer.

**When they first lean on him:**

> "You think I wanted this? You think I *liked* it? They were gonna feed me to a beholder, Lucy."

**If Lucy brings up the trial, or the family:**

> "After the trial, nobody in this family would look at me. So I found somebody who would pay me to look at *them*."

**"Who sent the Kenku?"**

> "Okay! Okay. Me. I put it through the Annex. It was just business, it wasn't *personal*... it was a little personal."

**"Why steal the recipe?"**

> "The recipe? Who cares about the recipe. It was the little thing he drew at the bottom."

**"What's the drawing for?"**

> "That drawing's how the pepper man knows you're Babe's. Show it, and they let you land. So I bought the way in off the Psurlons. Paid with one of Babe's own IOUs, how's that for funny? I was gonna be the pepper man's new best friend. Me. Not Babe."

**"So where is it now?"**

> "Somebody found out what I had. Don't ask me how. They made me an offer, Lucy. A *pretty penny*, for the way in and the drawing, both. I took it. Who wouldn't? They're long gone. Out in the Expanse by now."

If they passed the check for the buyer's name, he finally says it, barely above a whisper:

> "...Varrow."

**"Why go after the head?"**

Tino **doesn't know why the head knows what it knows**. He only knows that it does. He heard it from some good-for-nothings talking at a bar.

> "I hear things, okay? Couple of lowlifes at a bar, drunk, talking about some lion girl's head that 'knows things it shouldn't.' Knows about a deal. *My* deal. I don't know how, I don't wanna know how. I just know a head like that can put me in front of a beholder. Do you know how hard it is to find a *specific* dead lion?"

**"What does Varrow want with the growers?"**

> "I dunno! Pepper? Money? I didn't ask. You don't ask people like that questions."

### Decision 1: what happens to Tino

**This is Lucy's call.** Give the others a chance to weigh in, then put it to Nicole plainly.

1. **Hand him to Marfa.** Family justice, off-screen and unsettling. Lucy earns standing with her mother, and maybe with Babe.
   > *"Hello, Tino. Sit down. No, not there. There."*

   End the scene before anything happens.
2. **Bring him along.** He knows Varrow, and he had the route before he sold it, which makes him a useful and very untrustworthy guide. (Whether he kept a copy is up to you. My proposal: he memorized enough of it to bargain with.) He's a Giff, and a liability on a small ship. Marfa won't like it.
3. **Let him go.** This says something about who Lucy is now. He'll turn up again, owing her or hating her.

## 3. Marfa settles accounts

Back in the Pie Parlor, Marfa has heard what happened at the Annex, or hears it now.

**What she already offered** (Session 2): Korran gets a **crew of 6**, and Pip gets a real conversation about the Ruths backing **another coup**. Solving the case is the price.

**Her turn:** the case isn't closed while the mark is gone. My draft:

> *"The recipe, I can live without. Your father can make that Stromboli in his sleep. But the little drawing at the bottom? That's how the pepper man knows us. Without it, there's no pepper. Without the pepper, there's no Stromboli. And somebody else is out there using our name."*

That line explains the torn mark, which matters because the "pepper man" clue was never planted. It also makes the trip a job: **bring the mark back**, and the deal stands.

### Decision 2: Pip's price

Marfa makes Pip a real offer: smugglers, money and supply lines for the revolution. **But she wants something back**, and Drew chooses which price Pip accepts. My drafts, to pick from or replace:
- **A piece of the kingdom.** Exclusive Pie Parlor rights across the plasmoid kingdom once Pip holds the throne, with the plasmoids as captive customers.
- **A seat at the table.** A Ruth adviser at Pip's court. That means a Ruth listening in on every decision.
- **Something from the Expanse.** A share of whatever the party brings back, which puts Pip's war chest in conflict with Korran's people.

**The check: Persuasion (DC 15, Pip).** Success gets Pip **better terms**: she drops one condition or waits for payment. Failure doesn't kill the deal. She simply holds firm.

**Korran's crew:** Marfa has the six ready by the docks for the launch (see scene 7).

**A short rest fits here,** if the party wants one.

## 4. The temple

**Megan still needs money** for Speak with Dead. Ask the party who pays:
- **Pip** pays with **one of his last diamonds**. Most went into the Damselfly's Hyperdrive Core, so this one hurts. Make it disgusting.
- **Lucy** charges it to the family. Marfa will add it to the Annex invoice.
- **Korran** haggles: **Persuasion (DC 13)** halves the fee.

**The temple** is the nearest one, nondescript. Give the priest one line of personality and nothing more.

**Optional checks before the spell:**
- **Religion (DC 12):** the player knows the etiquette. The questions must be asked aloud, one at a time, and the dead answer only what they knew.
- **Medicine or Arcana (DC 14):** the head has been damaged by the Kenku's poison dart. On a success, the party learns ahead of time that some answers may come out broken.

### Decision 3: the five questions

**Speak with Dead allows five questions.** Let the players agree on them out of character while Megan pushes for his own. **There are never enough.** That's the point.

The answers come from **the Ringer, carrying Krysta's memories**. Some come out in her voice, and some in something colder. The poison has eaten at it, so let some answers break off.

**What the head actually knows** (Nolan): Krysta was **aboard a spaceship** and **witnessed Tino buy the route** from the Psurlons. A Psurlon ate her before that ship docked. **It doesn't know the route itself.** It only saw it sold.

**Answers ready for likely questions** (my drafts; adjust freely):
- **"What did you see?" or "Who did you meet?"** *"A ship. A Giff with nervous hands. He bought something from them... paid with a paper. A promise from a pizza house."* (Tino and the IOU.)
- **"What is the route into the Expanse?"** *"I saw it sold. I never saw inside."* The head can't give the route, so the question is a near miss. The route has to come from Tino, the DAV filing, or Korran's map.
- **"How did Krysta die?"** In Krysta's voice: *"On the ship. Before we docked. I trusted it."* Then the colder voice: *"She tasted of lions."* Keep it short and horrible.
- **"Why were you in space?"** **Nolan decides.** Megan hadn't seen her in years. However it's answered, it's the moment that lands hardest on him.
- **Megan's question, about the war:** whatever Krysta knew is **years old**. *"The Brown Lions were... at the river. When I left."* Who's winning stays unknown.
- **"What was the IOU for?"** *"For the way in."*
- **"Who is Varrow?"** Nothing. Krysta was eaten before Varrow came into it, and the dead only answer what they knew. That's a wasted question, which is a good table moment.

**Megan, hearing that she was in space:** he goes very still. *"She has never left the lands. She* had *never..."* He doesn't finish. Let the players fill the silence.

### Megan's goodbye

After the five questions. **He keeps the head.**

**Why he leaves** (Nolan): Megan needs to get home to his war, and **he doesn't know where home is**. The party can't help him with that. Korran even told him to give up. So he'll **earn his own passage**, and find someone on the Rock of Bral who can tell him where Thorndumm lies. He isn't bitter about it. **He still owes them his life**, and he says so.

*His goodbye* (my draft):

> *"It is not my daughter. But it was the last thing that saw her."* *(He wraps the head.)*
>
> *"My kingdom is at war, and I do not even know which way to point a ship to reach it. You cannot tell me. I do not blame you for that. You have your own road."*
>
> *"So I will earn my passage, as a warrior does. Somewhere on this rock is someone who knows where Thorndumm lies. I will find them."*
>
> *(a long pause)* *"The pizza. That was... very good. I will remember it."*

He bows, then clasps Korran's arm:

> *"My debt to you stands, Hadozee. Call on it, and I will come."*

Then he leaves. *(The last line quietly sets up his return in the Expanse.)*

## Saved for Session 5

Session 4 ended inside the warehouse, not at the chart table. Session 5 picks up there, then (if there's time) Marfa, the DAV, boarding, plotting the course, Decision 4 and the launch.

### Plotting the course

Aboard the newly named Shrike, before launch. Korran spreads out the **treasure map**, which is in Thieves' Cant with his mother's birthday as the cipher. Two symbols near the destination have never been readable.

**The check: Intelligence with navigator's tools (DC 15, Korran).** Krysta's head can't give the route (it only saw the route being sold), so combine the map with whatever else they have. Grant advantage if they have one of these: Tino's memory of the route, Tallyworth's manifest, or the DAV filing.
- **Success:** the route and the map line up. One of the two unreadable symbols now makes sense. **What it means is for you to decide**, or you can leave it as "a place, and he knows it."
- **Failure:** the route is clear enough to get into the Expanse, but the symbols stay dark.
- **Help:** Lucy has navigator's tools too, so she can give advantage.

### Decision 4: where to aim first

The Expanse is a maze of floating islands. Put the choice to the party, with Korran's voice loudest:
1. **Chase Varrow.** Follow the old trade route straight to **the growers**, where Varrow is using Babe's mark. This is the fastest way to get the mark back for Marfa and Pip's deal, and to find out what Varrow wants with Korran's people.
2. **Go for the hoard.** Follow Korran's map to the *Star-Reaver* crew's hoard first. That's a war chest for Pip and a fortune for Lucy, and it may put them ahead of anyone else who knows it's there.
3. **Go home first.** Head for the **Cloudjumper Tribe**: Korran's home island and his people, and maybe news of his mother. **This is Brad's to offer, or not.** Korran never went looking before.

Whatever they pick sets their heading.

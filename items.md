# Items and Treasure

Everything the notes name. Possession is tracked here. Each entry notes the season it was acquired, found or lost in.

## Found in Season 2

**Ruby ring: a Ring of Disintegrate** (homebrew; found Season 2, Session 1). The [Kenku assassin](bestiary/season2-encounters.md#session-1--the-kenku-assassin) at the [Pie Parlor](locations/bral-pie-parlor.md) pulled off its glove and used this ring's Disintegrate on itself, turning to dust. Only the ring remains.
- **Charges:** **one charge remaining.**
- **Where it comes from:** **every Kenku in The Unkindness wears a Disintegrate ring**, alongside a second ring with a spell of its own. Only this ring was found on the Session 1 Kenku.
- **What the players know:** they don't yet know what it is. They only saw it work once.
- **Where it is:** whether anyone picked it up is `[unconfirmed]`.

## Confirmed in the party's hands

**Fish Suit** (Uncommon; acquired Season 1, Session 2) — [Korran](player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md). From the [*Star-Reaver*](ships/star-reaver.md). Breathe in a vacuum and swim through space as if through water. Made his Session 7 spacewalk to the mothership possible.

**Boots of Gravitation** (Uncommon; acquired Season 1, Session 2) — [Korran](player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md). From the *Star-Reaver*. Change personal gravity orientation as a bonus action.

**100,000 gp of diamonds** (acquired Season 1, Sessions 5–6) — inside [Pip](player-characters/pip-oozewald.md), swallowed in two goes (50,000 gp during the shipyard distraction in Session 5, 100,000 gp total by Session 6). Some has been spent fuelling the Hyperdrive Core, which brought the Rock of Bral back to one day away. The remainder is still in him. [The Chwinga](npcs/chwinga.md) finds this hilarious: *"You're shinier than your cousin."*

**Charm of Ruin** (acquired Season 1, Session 4) — granted to Pip by the Chwinga after the Cosmic Horror fight.

> As an action, you can touch a nonmagical object, or a section of a larger nonmagical object, that fits in a 5-foot cube. The target is reduced to dust. Once used three times, the charm vanishes from you.

Uses spent so far are not recorded. Worth tracking — a charm that disintegrates hull sections and doors is a live option.

**Krysta's head** (acquired Season 1, Sessions 5–6) — the severed head of a [Psurlon Ringer](npcs/krysta.md) carrying a murdered Leonin's memories. [Megan](npcs/megan-forthfang.md) still had it when Marfa beamed everyone off the Damselfly. Megan claimed Speak with Dead, but per Nolan he doesn't know it.
- **Season 2, Session 1:** Megan raised it as a shield, and it **took the Kenku assassin's poison dart** meant for the table. It holds clues to the theft of the Stromboli recipe's ingredient. What the poison did to it is `[unconfirmed]`.

**Korran's treasure map, complete** (completed Season 1) — both halves of a Thieves' Cant map to the *Star-Reaver* crew's hoard in the [Ral'taka Expanse](locations/raltaka-expanse.md). Cipher: Korran's late mother's birthday. Partially decoded as of Session 7.

## Whereabouts uncertain

These were aboard the [Damselfly](ships/the-damselfly.md) at some point. Whether anyone took them when Marfa beamed the party off, or they were left behind with the ship, is `[unconfirmed]`.

**Voidfish eggs** (acquired Season 1, Session 6) — obtained at the Psurlon hub as the cure for Blackstar Fever, then made redundant when Megan cured Tamenik outright. Blackstar Fever is transmissible, so keeping them would be sensible.

**Scroll of [Greater Restoration](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2129-greater-restoration)** (found Season 1, Session 5) — found stuck to Megan's container with green goo. Noted as also being able to solve Jetson's curse. Megan used his own casting to cure Tamenik and later healed Jetson, so **the scroll may well be unspent.** Status `[unconfirmed]`.

**The remaining cargo rations** — half had already been burned (see Lost or destroyed).

## Left behind on the *Star-Reaver* (Season 1, Session 2)

[Korran](player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md) took the Fish Suit and Boots of Gravitation. **No one got anything else.** These three remain on the derelict (or lost with it):

| Item | Rarity | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Spelljamming Helm** | Rare | Lets a spellcaster control a spelljamming ship |
| **Star Chart of the Lost Spheres** | Uncommon | Reveals hidden pathways and celestial coordinates; advantage on Navigation (Survival) checks in Wildspace |
| **Void Crystal Focus** | Uncommon | Spellcasting focus of voidstone. Once per long rest, when you cast a spell dealing force, radiant or psychic damage, reroll one damage die |

All five *Star-Reaver* items are homebrew. The unclaimed **Spelljamming Helm** would let the party fly a ship without depending on [Jetson](npcs/jetson.md). The **Star Chart** bears directly on navigating the Ral'taka Expanse. Either is a reason someone might go back.

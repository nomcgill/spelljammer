## The warehouse: the hamster

They start where Session 4 ended. Korran is inside. Lucy and Pip come in behind him. The grinding is a **giant space hamster** in a treadwheel, slowly turning a belt of empty platforms from the floor up about 25 feet to a loft. The room is about 40 feet high, two stories. A dark **foreman's office** is on the second story, maybe 30 feet across.

Korran has no darkvision. He smells fur, animal must, and **gold-dusted pepper from home**. Lucy has devil's sight. Pip has darkvision 60 ft. Lucy spots a **light-switch lever** by the back door, wires going to the ceiling. They **do not flip it** — they don't want to advertise they're there. They go the long way around toward the stairs. Lucy holds Korran's hand in the dark.

The hamster is huge (Nolan: maybe **600 pounds**), tired, eyes mostly closed, not looking at them, too fat to climb out of the wheel. Lucy pockets some feed. Pip **tastes** it (seed mix, spit it out). Table joke that Giffs are a purely pizza-based diet; someone tells Craig that's canon.

Lucy wants to "make nice" with the hamster; Korran wants the office. They split: Lucy toward the hamster, Korran and Pip to the office door.

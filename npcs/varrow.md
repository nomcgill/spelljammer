# Varrow

**Appears in:** Season 2

The name on **Varrow & Daughters, Fine Spices**. **Astral elf.** First name and history are `[unconfirmed]`. Has **four daughters** (also astral elves); their names are `[unconfirmed]`. No mother in the warehouse photo.

## Status

**Not appeared.** Nolan: **probably the big bad of Season 2.** The party has seen a **photo** of him and left it in the office. They cannot read the Elvish plate. [Roger](roger.md) described him as the yellow-haired two-leg who uses the office and never feeds the hamster.

## What is decided

- He bought **the grower's mark and the merchant route** from [Tino Fennetti](tino-fennetti.md) after learning Tino meant to use them himself.
- Before that, per [Marfa](marfa-ruth.md) in Session 5, he **coordinated** the pepper for the Ruths **without having the route himself**.
- He plans to **use the growers' trust to raid them** (Nolan).
- The Speak with Dead on [Krysta](krysta.md)'s head: *"The one with the daughters. They smell of..."*

## Look

**Blonde** (Nolan, after Session 5; Roger said yellow). Tall, pale, midnight-blue merchant coat, **gold pin shaped like a pepper leaf** on the lapel — that's the distant-readable detail. Dark, sparkly eyes. The photo at the table was also described as silver-white hair to his collar; same man. Brass plate on the frame is **Elvish**: *Varrow & Daughters*. They left the frame.

## Unused pitch (not canon)

During Session 2 prep the agent pitched a former quartermaster of a crew that sailed with the [*Star-Reaver*](../ships/star-reaver.md). Nolan has not taken that.

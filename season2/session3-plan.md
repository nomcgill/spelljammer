# Season 2, Session 3: Plan

The run sheet for Session 3, in table order. The ideas behind it are in [`session3-brainstorm.md`](session3-brainstorm.md). What happened in Session 2 is in [`session-notes.md`](session-notes.md#session-2). Some scripts are carried over from the [Session 2 plan](session2-plan.md#4-the-iron-annex), which never reached them.

**Spoilers throughout. Nothing here is canon until it's run.** Read-aloud and scripted text is my draft for Nolan to edit.

## What the players don't know

- **The Kenku** is *Scritch* of **The Unkindness**. His name hasn't been said. He's blind and echolocates. He wears **two rings, Darkness and Disintegrate**, and nobody noticed either.
- **Tino Fennetti hired him.** Tino is Lucy's cousin and the informant who sent her to Warden's Maw. He stole the grower's mark from Babe's recipe card and sold it to a buyer who has **already left for the Ral'taka Expanse**.
- **Why the head is the target:** Tino bought the route into the Expanse from the Psurlons, paying with a Ruth IOU. The Ringer wearing Krysta was there for the deal, so the head can identify him.
- **Tino is in the building right now.** He came to raise the fee and make sure the head was finished.

## Before you run this

- **Tino is a Giff** (Nolan).
- **Tino's stats:** a **Spy** stat block. Cunning Action lets him Dash or Disengage as a bonus action, which suits a man who runs. He has **two Thugs**.
- **The buyer** is still the placeholder *Varrow & Daughters, Fine Spices*, a name the agent made up during Session 2 prep. This session only needs the name. The agent's pitch was a former quartermaster of a crew that sailed with the *Star-Reaver*. Ask Brad only if you want to tie it into his backstory's "partnered crews."
- **Megan's exit** (Nolan): he leaves after the temple **and keeps the head.** It's his daughter's face.
- **The crew of 6.** Two are given names below as placeholders. Rename them freely.

## 1. Opening: Korran's flashback

**The session opens on this,** before any recap. It was planned for Session 2 and never run (Nolan forgot), so it now opens Session 3. The trigger is the Kenku's Darkness, where Session 2 ended. **Ask Brad beforehand** whether he wants it played in front of the whole table.

*Read-aloud, to Brad:*

> The light doesn't fade. It's *swallowed*, all at once, the way you've only seen it happen once before...
>
> ...and you're a boy again, lying on your back on a high branch at the edge of your island, the way you always did. Counting the stars above the Ral'taka Expanse, giving them names your mother would laugh at. The sky is warm and gold, and the islands drift.
>
> And then the stars go out. Not all at once, but in a line, one after another, as if something enormous is sliding across them. It's a shape: long, ridged, curved like the bones of something that died a very long time ago. A ribcage the size of a mountain, with a skull at its front. Behind it, things like tentacles trail through the sky.
>
> Its shadow falls across the island. Every bird goes silent. Somewhere below you, the warning drums start.
>
> And you hear your mother calling your name.

**Midjourney prompt** for the view from Korran's branch (adjust the version parameter to whatever you use):

```text
view from a high jungle tree branch at the edge of a floating island, looking out across a vast sky of countless floating jungle islands drifting in warm golden light, thick vines hanging like beards from the islands' rocky undersides, veins of raw gold glinting in the exposed rock and roots, waterfalls spilling off island edges into endless open sky with no ground below, dusk with the first stars appearing in a deep blue-gold sky, lush tropical canopy, birds circling between islands, sense of enormous scale and peace, fantasy, Spelljammer, Dungeons and Dragons, cinematic, painterly, highly detailed --ar 16:9
```

**Settle the scene with two questions for Brad:**
- How old are you, and what does the island look like?
- What did your mother teach you about the gold?

### The raid (improvised)

Then play out **what Korran did when the ship came** as a short scene. **The secrets are Brad's call.** Beat 3 is scripted. The others are prompts to improvise from: use what fits and skip what doesn't. This is before the slave fight, and long before the crew.

**What's fixed:** the *Star-Reaver*'s pirates plundered the Expanse for its gold and **took Hadozee as slaves, and Korran was among them.** So the scene ends with him taken, however he gets there.

**Beats to offer Brad, roughly in order:**
1. **Getting down from the tree.** The drums are going and the shadow is over everything. *Do you glide down, climb, or stay hidden up there?* (Hadozee can glide, so a boy might leap.)
2. **Finding his mother.** She's calling his name. *Where is she, and what is she doing: gathering children, grabbing a weapon, hiding the gold?* Then: *What does she tell you to do?* Whether he obeys is the heart of the scene.
3. **The pirates land, and Gloamcast** *(scripted; read it)*:

   > The ship doesn't land. It *hangs* there above the canopy, close enough that you can see the bone of its hull, yellow and pitted, and the tentacles trailing beneath it like a jellyfish's. Ropes come down. Then nets. Then men, sliding down the ropes, landing hard in the clearing with weapons already out.
   >
   > And the first thing they look at isn't your people. It's the gold. The gold on the shrine posts, in the carvings, in the beads around the elders' necks. You watch a man pry a golden carving off the old prayer tree with a knife, and he laughs while he does it.
   >
   > Then the last one comes down. He doesn't slide; he *glides*, arms out, the skin between them catching the wind, the way your people do. He lands light as a leaf. He's a Hadozee. Older than the rest of them, coat too fine for a raid, a captain's hat pulled low. The pirates go quiet when he lands.
   >
   > He looks around the clearing like he's buying it. And then he sees your mother.
   >
   > He stops. He just... looks at her. For a long moment, in the middle of all of it, the shouting and the drums and the smoke, he looks at her the way you've only ever seen someone look at the stars.
   >
   > Your mother looks back at him. She doesn't lower her eyes.
   >
   > *"Captain Gloamcast,"* one of the pirates calls. *"Orders?"*
   >
   > He doesn't look away from her when he answers. *"The gold. And any who'll fetch a price."* A beat. *"Not her."*

   **Why it lands:** this is the man who, years later and dead, told Tamenik *"I always had a thing for her."* Brad may remember. Let the moment hang, then go on.

   *"Not her"* doesn't settle what happened to Korran's mother. It only says the captain spared her **that day**.
4. **One choice.** Give him a moment where he can act: a younger child frozen in the open, an elder going down under a pirate's club, or a gold-carved shrine being pried apart. *Does he hide, run to help, or fight with whatever he has?* Let it be brave, or let it be the thing he's ashamed of. Either is Korran.
5. **Taken.** A net, a rope or a hand on his collar. The last thing he sees from the deck is his island getting small, and his mother calling his name.

**The final prompt for Brad:**
- *"Did you ever see your mother again?"*

**Don't decide** what happened to his mother. The wiki only knows she's "late," and that Gloamcast loved her. Brad's answer to the final prompt is the canon.

**Cut back:** in the present, in the dark, **the Kenku mimics a voice calling Korran's name.** Whether it's his mother's voice is Brad's call.

## 3. The fight, and the face at the back door

Roll initiative.

### Round one: the back door

**Just before Lucy's first turn,** the lobby's back door opens.

*Pass Nicole a note, or tell her quietly:*

> A staff door behind the front desk slides open, and light spills in from the hallway beyond, then stops dead at the edge of the Darkness. A Giff stands in the doorway, mid-step, with a satchel under his arm. He's thinner than you remember, and his coat is too nice. **It's Tino.** Your cousin. The man whose testimony put you in Warden's Maw.
>
> He sees the fight. He sees *you*. His eyes go wide.
>
> The door slides shut.

**Only Lucy saw it.** Everyone else is blind, and the Kenku's echolocation only reaches 30 feet. Then it's her turn.

### Lucy's choice

- **She chases him.** Through the staff door, alone, while the fight goes on behind her. Go to [the chase](#4-the-chase-out-the-back). The others can follow once they get out of the Darkness.
- **She shouts.** The whole table hears it. Whatever she yells, the others now know someone ran out the back.
- **She keeps quiet** and keeps fighting. Tino gets away for now, and Lucy has a secret. It's a strong mob-daughter choice. **Don't push her either way.**

### The Kenku

- **Before the door:** his target is **the head**, so he goes for Megan.
- **After the door:** he hears it and realizes his client has been seen. **He switches to covering Tino's escape**, backing toward the staff door, mimicking voices so nobody can tell where he is.
- **Stats:** an **Assassin** stat block, plus **blindsight 30 ft. (echolocation)**.
- **His weakness:** his sight is sound. A Silence spell, or a loud enough noise (a slammed pneumatic door, the glitching motto sign, a burst of brass tubes), leaves him blind for that moment. Reward players who work it out.
- **His Disintegrate ring:** his desperate move. Aimed at the head, it would finish the contract instantly. Telegraph it (he pulls off a glove, and a ring glints) so the party gets a turn to stop him. If he's cornered with no way out, he uses it on himself, like the first Kenku.

### Everyone else

- **Megan** fights beside the party, protecting the head.
- **Tilptek** spends his turns **trying to get the lights back on**, impartial to everyone:
  - *"Please remain calm. The lighting is experiencing a disruption."*
  - *"I am not on anyone's side. But really, the lights* should *be on."*
  - *"Damages will be invoiced."*
  - If Lucy talks to him: *"Luckyluciano. You look... well."* *(a beat)* *"This is not a good time."*
- **The Thri-kreen clerks** keep typing through the whole fight, ducking with two arms and working with the other two.
- **Jetson** is "learning the business." He hides behind the desk, but he can do one useful, brave thing if the moment calls for it.

### The battlefield

- **The pneumatic doors** can slam shut on someone, or jam halfway.
- **The floor sigils** flare under anyone who draws a weapon, giving away positions in the dark.
- **The light panels** strobe at the edges of the Darkness.
- **The motto sign** glitches when hit: *"Results. Results. Results. Results."*
- **The brass message tubes** whoosh contracts overhead.

### When the lights come back

The Darkness lasts 10 minutes, or less if Tilptek wins. When the lights return, a speaker crackles:

> *"This is Director Tallyworth. Results... are pending."*

## 4. The chase out the back

Use **your Iron Annex map** here. Tino has a head start, two Thugs, and the Kenku covering him if he's still alive. Give each room one complication:

- **The tube hub.** Contracts fire through glass tubes. A tube bursts and blinds someone in a storm of paper, or a player rides a cargo tube one room ahead.
- **The archive.** Tall stacks, with Thri-kreen archivists who keep filing. A stack topples. An archivist politely asks the party to sign in.
- **The loading dock.** A cargo lift down to the street, and **a small skiff** waiting for Tino. This is the finish line.
- **Anywhere else on your map:** Thri-kreen who don't look up, doors that need a stamped pass, and a pneumatic door that slams at the worst moment.

**Tino's moves:** he Dashes every turn, sends a Thug to block a doorway, and throws the satchel's papers into the air for cover. He doesn't fight unless he's cornered.

### If they catch him

Go to [the interrogation](#5-tino).

### If he escapes

- The skiff drops off the edge of the dock into the city.
- **He drops something:** a **trade card** reading *Varrow & Daughters, Fine Spices*, with a dock address. It smells strongly of gold-dusted pepper.
- **Lucy knows his face.** Tino becomes a villain who can turn up again, maybe in the Expanse.

## 5. Tino

### Who he is now

He's been hiding since his testimony. He's nervous, quick-talking and self-pitying, and he'd sell anyone to save himself, as he already has.

### What he says

He resists at first, then breaks:
- *"You think I wanted this? You think I* liked *it? They were gonna feed me to a beholder, Lucy."*
- *"After the trial, nobody in this family would look at me. So I found somebody who would pay me to look at* them."
- **What he admits:**
  - He hired The Unkindness, through the Annex.
  - He wanted the card for **the mark at the bottom**, not the recipe. *"The recipe? Who cares about the recipe. It was the little thing he drew at the bottom."*
  - He sold the mark to a buyer who has **already gone to the Ral'taka Expanse**.
  - He bought the route from the Psurlons, and the head **saw him do it**. That's why it had to go.
- **What he won't give up easily:** the buyer's name. He's more afraid of them than of the Ruths. A good Intimidation roll, or Pip simply picking him up, gets *"Varrow"*, and maybe *"they knew the Star-Reaver."* **(Only if you use the *Star-Reaver* tie.)**

### Lucy decides his fate

- **Hand him to Marfa.** Family justice, off-screen and unsettling.
- **Use him.** He knows the buyer and the route, so he could come along as a very untrustworthy guide.
- **Let him go,** with whatever that says about Lucy now.

### Marfa's scene

If Tino goes to Marfa, she's quiet and very calm:

> *"Hello, Tino. Sit down. No, not there. There."*

Let the scene end before anything happens.

## 6. The temple

**Megan still needs money** for Speak with Dead. Ask the party who pays.
- **Pip** can pay in diamonds from his stomach, if any are left.
- **Lucy** can charge it to the family.
- **Korran** can haggle.

**The temple** is the nearest one, nondescript. Give the priest one line of personality and nothing more.

### Five questions

**Speak with Dead allows five questions.** Let the players agree on them while Megan pushes for his own. The answers come from **the Ringer, carrying Krysta's memories**, so some come out in her voice and some in something colder. The poison from the first dart has eaten at it, so let some answers come out broken.

**Answers ready for likely questions** (my drafts; adjust freely):
- **"Who hired the Kenku?" or "Who did you meet?"** *"A Giff. Nervous hands. He paid with a paper... a promise from a pizza house."* (Tino and the IOU.)
- **"What is the route into the Expanse?"** *"Through the gold... follow the warm current until the islands... the islands..."* Then a real direction, if you want to give them one. **This is the strongest reason to go.**
- **"How did Krysta die?"** In Krysta's voice: *"I trusted it."* Then in the colder voice: *"She tasted of lions."* Keep it short and horrible. What really happened to the real Krysta is `[unconfirmed]`.
- **Megan's question, about the war:** *"The Brown Lions were at the gates when I... when she left."* Who's winning stays unknown.
- **"What was the IOU for?"** *"For the way in."*

### Megan's goodbye

After the five questions. He keeps the head.

> *"It is not my daughter. But it was the last thing that saw her."* *(He wraps the head.)* *"I have a kingdom at war. I must find my way home."* *(a long pause)* *"The pizza. That was... very good. I will remember it."*

He bows, clasps Korran's arm (*"My debt to you stands, Hadozee"*), and leaves.

**What the players don't know:** Megan returns later in the Ral'taka Expanse.

## 7. Closing the case (optional, if there's time)

### The spice house

If they have the trade card or the dock address, they can raid **Varrow & Daughters'** dockside warehouse.
- **What they find:** **Babe's recipe card, with the bottom torn off.** The recipe is all there, but the strip with the mark is gone, and the Varrow ship left for the Expanse days ago.
- **The players have already seen that strip.** In the Session 2 cold open, a ship arrived in the Expanse holding it up. Let them make the connection.
- **Someone has to explain the torn edge,** since the "pepper man" clue was never planted. Choose one:
  - **Marfa:** *"That's where he wrote his mark. For the pepper man. Nobody else has it."*
  - **Korran** smells the pepper on the card and knows it from home.
- **Set dressing if it turns into a fight:** cargo cranes, spice crates that burst into choking clouds, a treadwheel crane run by a **giant space hamster** that panics, and a stack of Giff smokepowder.

**If time is short, skip it.** Tino's confession or the head's answers already point to the Expanse. The card can come back to Lucy later.

### The DAV

Korran registers the Shrike, and the costs are waived. This wipes the scry trace.
- *"Name of vessel?"* **Brad names the ship.**
- **A clue, if wanted:** the register shows a Varrow & Daughters ship that **departed days ago**, filing toward the Ral'taka Expanse.

## 8. The launch

Marfa delivers what she promised:
- **Korran's crew of 6.** Introduce two of them by name (placeholders):
  - **Dottie**, a gruff old Giff deckhand who has worked Ruth ships for thirty years and calls everyone "kid."
  - **Spoons**, a jittery young cook who won't stop asking whether the Expanse has good produce.
- **Pip's conversation** about the Ruths backing his coup happens now, or on the voyage.

*Read-aloud:*

> The Shrike slips out of its berth, the Rock of Bral falling away beneath you: towers on towers, lanterns, laundry lines, a hundred colours that don't belong to any one world. Ahead, the silver haze of the Astral Sea. Somewhere past it, warm gold light.

**Jetson** is at the helm, or in the co-pilot's seat "learning the business." Give him the line:

> *"PUNCH IT."*

**Optional final cut:** back to the Ral'taka Expanse. The Hadozee grower stands at the edge of their island, watching the trade route, and frowns. Their "friend" never came back.

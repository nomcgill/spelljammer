# The Star-Reaver

**Appears in:** Season 1

The pirate ship that destroyed [Korran](../player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md)'s homeland, then became his home, then became his victim.

## What it is

An ancient ship, converted into a **squid ship**, whose **hull was built from the ribcage of a dead god**, with a skull at the bow. Efficiently done, by all accounts.

## History

Under [Captain Gloamcast](../npcs/gloamcast.md), the *Star-Reaver* raided the [Ral'taka Expanse](../locations/raltaka-expanse.md) for its gold and took Hadozee slaves, Korran among them. Korran earned the crew's respect by fighting and killing another Hadozee slave, was recruited into the pirate life, and took the name **Driftblade** for his brutal but swift fighting style.

He went on several raids with them. Then he **betrayed and killed the entire original crew, one by one, as revenge for the plundering of his homeland**, and escaped without leaving a trace.

The captain died separately, of Blackstar Fever.

## Loose ends

- **Where is the *Star-Reaver* now?** Korran's backstory asks this directly. The party left it derelict.
- **Some of the crew, or partnered crews, may still be alive** — and may want Korran dead. His own backstory raises this.
- Korran holds **half of a treasure map** written by the captain, leading to the crew's hoard. The captain considers it Korran's by right, being the last of the crew. The captain does not appear to know why.

## Season 1

### Where the party found it

Session 2. The Damselfly lurched to a halt beside it: a squid-style ship with its **front tentacles broken off and the stumps decayed**, sitting in a **toxic air envelope** larger than the party's own. A.R.I.A. confirmed the toxicity was evenly distributed and that anti-hazard precautions were pointless. Left unaddressed, the contamination would have made the party's own envelope increasingly toxic within hours.

Abandoned — but Korran remembered it was still loaded with the magic items his old crew had plundered. The wreck sat in a **toxic air envelope** with an acidic green aura of ailment. [Captain Gloamcast](../npcs/gloamcast.md)'s **corpse sat at the spelljamming helm**.

### The loot

[Korran](../player-characters/korran-cloudjumper.md) took the **Fish Suit** and **Boots of Gravitation**. No one got anything else. Still aboard (or lost with the derelict): Spelljamming Helm, Star Chart of the Lost Spheres, Void Crystal Focus. Full details in [`items.md`](../items.md).
